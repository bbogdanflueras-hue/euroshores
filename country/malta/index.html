Beach by beach in Malta

Ghajn Tuffieha Bay (4.7 / 5)

A sandy bay on the northwest coast of Malta below a steep headland, reached by a long flight of steps.

Best season: June–September. Typical water temperature: about 25 degrees C. Access: Moderate.

Facilities: Sunbed and umbrella hire, a snack bar and restaurant, pedalos and seasonal lifeguards. Getting there and parking: Car park at the top, free but often full by 09:30 in summer, then roughly 180 steps down (sources give 100 to 200).

Ramla Bay (4.7 / 5)

A wide sandy beach on Gozo known for its reddish-orange sand, with Calypso's Cave in the cliffs above.

Best season: June–September. Typical water temperature: about 25 degrees C. Access: Easy.

Facilities: A bar and snack bars, equipment hire, public toilets, water sports and summer lifeguards. Getting there and parking: Parking along the road to the beach; bus 302 stops about a 5-minute walk away.

Blue Lagoon (4.5 / 5)

A sheltered bay of vivid turquoise water between Comino and the islet of Cominotto.

Best season: May–October. Typical water temperature: about 25 degrees C. Access: Moderate.

Facilities: Kiosks and sunbed rental in season. Getting there and parking: Boat only — ferries and tours run from Malta and Gozo.

Golden Bay (4.5 / 5)

A wide sandy bay on the northwest coast of Malta, backed by cliffs, with the amenities for a full beach day.

Best season: June–September. Typical water temperature: about 25 degrees C. Access: Easy.

Facilities: Cafe-restaurant, sunbed and umbrella hire, toilets and lifeguards in summer. Getting there and parking: Car park above the beach (fills by mid-morning in summer), then steps or a ramp down; buses run from Valletta and Sliema.

St Peter's Pool (4.5 / 5)

A natural rock pool on the Delimara peninsula, with clear water, pale limestone ledges and no sand.

Best season: May–October. Typical water temperature: about 24 degrees C. Access: Moderate.

Facilities: None — bring water, food and shade; there are no lifeguards. Getting there and parking: Park near the end of the road, then walk about five minutes down a rough track and steps to the rocks.

San Blas Bay (4.5 / 5)

A secluded cove below Nadur on Gozo that can only be reached on foot or by quad down a steep, rough road.

Best season: June–September. Typical water temperature: about 25 degrees C. Access: Challenging.

Facilities: None — bring water, food and shade; the nearest food is about 1.6 km away. Getting there and parking: A small parking area before the steep descent, then a walk of about 15 minutes down; do not drive down the track.

Paradise Bay (4.4 / 5)

A small sandy cove at the northern tip of Malta, with views across to Gozo and Comino.

Best season: June–September. Typical water temperature: about 25 degrees C. Access: Moderate.

Facilities: Sunbed and umbrella hire, refreshments and a restaurant, toilets and showers. Getting there and parking: Car park at the top, then about 100 steps down; public transport is limited.

Ghadira Bay (4.4 / 5)

A long sandy bay that is often described as Malta's largest beach, with shallow water that stays calm well out from shore.

Best season: June–September. Typical water temperature: about 25 degrees C. Access: Easy.

Facilities: Sunbeds and umbrellas, kiosks and restaurants, water-sports hire, wheelchair access and summer lifeguards. Getting there and parking: Parking along the road behind the beach; buses 42 and 49 run from Valletta.

Mgarr ix-Xini (4.4 / 5)

A narrow, sheltered inlet on the south coast of Gozo with a small pebble beach, clear water and a historic watchtower above.

Best season: May–October. Typical water temperature: about 24 degrees C. Access: Moderate.

Facilities: Few facilities; bring water and shade. Getting there and parking: Limited parking near the inlet; arrive early in summer.

Gnejna Bay (4.4 / 5)

A sandy bay on the north-west coast of Malta, framed by low cliffs and known for its traditional boathouses.

Best season: May–October. Typical water temperature: about 24 degrees C. Access: Easy.

Facilities: Basic facilities in summer; bring water and shade. Getting there and parking: Parking near the bay; fee unconfirmed.

Fomm ir-Rih Bay (4.4 / 5)

A remote pebble bay at the foot of steep cliffs on the west coast of Malta, with a rugged setting and no development.

Best season: May–October. Typical water temperature: about 23 degrees C. Access: Challenging.

Facilities: None; bring water and food. Getting there and parking: No official car park; you park by the road and walk down.

Xlendi Bay, Gozo (4.4 / 5)

Xlendi is a narrow bay on the south-west coast of Gozo, with steep golden cliffs on both sides and a small beach at the head. A promenade with restaurants runs along the water, and the sea is clear and deep for swimming and diving. Cliff walks lead to a watchtower on the headland.

Best season: June–September. Typical water temperature: about 26 degrees C. Access: Easy.

Facilities: Cafes, restaurants, dive centres and a small beach area. Getting there and parking: Parking along the road above the bay; limited in summer.

Imgiebah Bay (4.3 / 5)

A small sandy bay on the north coast of Malta, reached by a path down from Selmun, with clear water and rocky headlands.

Best season: May–October. Typical water temperature: about 23 degrees C. Access: Moderate.

Facilities: None; bring water and food. Getting there and parking: Parking near Selmun; a walk down to the bay.

Mellieha Bay (4.2 / 5)

One of the longest sandy beaches in Malta, with shallow water that suits families and a wide bay facing the open sea.

Best season: May–October. Typical water temperature: about 24 degrees C. Access: Easy.

Facilities: Sunbeds, umbrellas and beach cafes in season. Getting there and parking: Roadside and nearby car parks; busy at weekends.

Pretty Bay (4.0 / 5)

A sandy bay beside the town of Birzebbuga in the south of Malta, with a promenade and views across the harbour.

Best season: May–October. Typical water temperature: about 23 degrees C. Access: Easy.

Facilities: Cafes and restaurants along the promenade; some seasonal facilities. Getting there and parking: Street parking nearby.

About beaches in Malta

The Blue Lagoon on the small island of Comino is a shallow bay with very clear turquoise water over white sand, and it is one of the best-known swimming spots in the Mediterranean.

When to go

June to September is the best time. The lagoon is extremely busy in July and August, especially around midday when the day boats arrive. Early morning and the shoulder months are much calmer.

Getting around

Ferries and boat trips to Comino run from Malta and Gozo. Comino has almost no road traffic, so you reach the lagoon on foot from the landing area or directly by boat.

Good to know

Malta uses the euro. There is little shade on Comino, so bring sun protection, water and food. The island is protected, so follow the signs and leave no litter.

Frequently asked questions

When is the best time to visit the Blue Lagoon?

Early morning or September, when it is warm and far less crowded than July and August middays.

Are there facilities on Comino?

There are limited facilities, and there is little shade, so bring water and sun protection.
