Beach by beach in Cyprus

Konnos Bay, Cape Greco (4.7 / 5)

Konnos Bay is a small sandy cove at the foot of pine-covered cliffs near Cape Greco, with clear turquoise water that is good for snorkelling. A short walk leads to the headlands of the Cape Greco national forest park.

Best season: May–October. Typical water temperature: about 26 degrees C. Access: Easy.

Facilities: Sunbeds and umbrellas for hire, a seasonal kiosk, toilets and watersports in season. Getting there and parking: Car park above the beach; it can fill by midday in summer.

Nissi Beach, Ayia Napa (4.6 / 5)

Nissi Beach is a wide white-sand beach on the edge of Ayia Napa, with shallow turquoise water and a small islet that can be reached by wading in calm weather. It is one of the liveliest beaches in Cyprus, with beach bars, music and watersports in summer.

Best season: May–October. Typical water temperature: about 26 degrees C. Access: Easy.

Facilities: Sunbeds and umbrellas for hire, beach bars, restaurants, toilets, showers and watersports. Getting there and parking: Car parks near the beach; they fill up by late morning in July and August.

Fig Tree Bay, Protaras (4.6 / 5)

Fig Tree Bay is a sheltered sandy bay in Protaras with shallow, calm water that suits children and beginners. A small rocky outcrop with a fig tree gives the bay its name, and cafes line the promenade behind the beach.

Best season: May–October. Typical water temperature: about 26 degrees C. Access: Easy.

Facilities: Sunbeds and umbrellas, cafes, restaurants, toilets, showers and watersports. Getting there and parking: Paid and free parking nearby; space is limited in peak season.

Lara Beach, Akamas (4.5 / 5)

Lara Beach is a sandy bay on the wild west coast of the Akamas peninsula, one of the places where green and loggerhead turtles nest in summer. Access is by a rough track from the Agios Georgios side, which keeps the bay quiet and the surroundings unspoilt.

Best season: May–October. Typical water temperature: about 25 degrees C. Access: Moderate.

Facilities: Very limited; a small seasonal snack bar at most, so bring water, food and shade. Getting there and parking: Informal parking at the end of the track.

Makronissos Beach, Ayia Napa (4.4 / 5)

Makronissos Beach is a long sandy beach on the east side of Ayia Napa with calm, shallow water and a more relaxed feel than Nissi Beach. Ancient rock-cut tombs lie close to the beach and can be visited on the same trip.

Best season: May–October. Typical water temperature: about 26 degrees C. Access: Easy.

Facilities: Sunbeds and umbrellas, beach bars, tavernas, toilets and showers in season. Getting there and parking: Parking near the beach, with more space than at Nissi Beach.

Coral Bay, Peyia (4.4 / 5)

Coral Bay near Peyia is a sandy bay sheltered between two headlands, with calm, shallow water and a well-developed resort strip behind it. It is one of the most popular beaches in the Paphos area for families.

Best season: May–October. Typical water temperature: about 25 degrees C. Access: Easy.

Facilities: Sunbeds and umbrellas, cafes, restaurants, toilets, showers and watersports. Getting there and parking: Car parks near the beach; spaces are limited in peak season.

Petra tou Romiou (Aphrodite's Rock) (4.4 / 5)

Petra tou Romiou, also called Aphrodite's Rock, is a pebble beach with sea stacks that legend links to the birth of Aphrodite. It is a famous stop on the road between Limassol and Paphos, and the sea can be rough, so many visitors come for the view and a short dip.

Best season: May–October. Typical water temperature: about 25 degrees C. Access: Moderate.

Facilities: Visitor cafe and toilets near the car park; no sunbeds on the beach. Getting there and parking: Free parking at the roadside visitor area.

Landa Beach, Ayia Napa (4.4 / 5)

Landa Beach is a golden sand beach a short walk west of Nissi Beach in Ayia Napa, with shallow, clear water and a calmer feel than its busier neighbour. It suits families and anyone who wants Ayia Napa sand without the crowds of Nissi.

Best season: May–October. Typical water temperature: about 26 degrees C. Access: Easy.

Facilities: Sunbeds and umbrellas, beach bar, toilets and watersports in season. Getting there and parking: Parking along the beach road and in nearby car parks.

Ayia Thekla Beach (4.4 / 5)

Ayia Thekla Beach lies on the coast west of Ayia Napa beside a small seaside chapel. The sand is soft, the water is calm and shallow, and the beach is quieter than the main resort beaches, which makes it popular with local families.

Best season: May–October. Typical water temperature: about 26 degrees C. Access: Easy.

Facilities: Sunbeds and umbrellas in season, cafe or taverna nearby, toilets. Getting there and parking: Free parking near the chapel and the beach.

Glyki Nero Beach, Ayia Napa (4.4 / 5)

Glyki Nero is a small sandy cove on the coast between Ayia Napa and Cape Greco. The water is clear and usually calm, and the setting is quieter than the big resort beaches nearby.

Best season: May–October. Typical water temperature: about 25 degrees C. Access: Easy.

Facilities: Limited facilities, bring water and snacks. Getting there and parking: Small car park close to the cove.

Cape Greco Coves, Ayia Napa (4.4 / 5)

Cape Greco is a rocky headland between Ayia Napa and Protaras with small coves, sea caves and very clear water. It is a popular spot for swimming, snorkelling and cliff views, and it is far less built up than the resort beaches.

Best season: May–October. Typical water temperature: about 25 degrees C. Access: Easy.

Facilities: No facilities at most coves, a snack bar near the main car park in season. Getting there and parking: Free car parks along the cape road.

Governor's Beach, Limassol (4.3 / 5)

Governor's Beach lies below white chalk cliffs between Limassol and Larnaca, with dark grey sand and pebbles that contrast with the pale rock. The water is clear and the beach has a mix of organised areas and quieter stretches.

Best season: May–October. Typical water temperature: about 26 degrees C. Access: Easy.

Facilities: Beach tavernas, sunbeds and umbrellas in season, toilets. Getting there and parking: Parking near the beach.

Sunrise Beach, Protaras (4.3 / 5)

Sunrise Beach is a sandy beach on the Protaras coast with calm, shallow water and a line of hotels behind it. It is a convenient family beach with services close by, and it faces east, so the morning light is good.

Best season: May–October. Typical water temperature: about 26 degrees C. Access: Easy.

Facilities: Sunbeds and umbrellas, cafes, toilets, showers and watersports in season. Getting there and parking: Parking near the beach; spaces fill up in peak season.

Toxeftra Beach, Pegeia (4.3 / 5)

Toxeftra Beach is a quiet sandy bay on the coast near Pegeia, north of Coral Bay. It is less crowded than Coral Bay itself, with clear water and a relaxed feel.

Best season: May–October. Typical water temperature: about 25 degrees C. Access: Easy.

Facilities: Limited facilities, a few tavernas nearby. Getting there and parking: Small car park or roadside parking.

Pissouri Bay Beach (4.2 / 5)

Pissouri Bay Beach is a long beach of pebbles and coarse sand on the south coast between Limassol and Paphos, with a quieter feel than the larger resorts. White cliffs frame the bay and the village sits on the hill above.

Best season: May–October. Typical water temperature: about 25 degrees C. Access: Easy.

Facilities: Sunbeds and umbrellas in season, tavernas and cafes, toilets. Getting there and parking: Parking along the beach road.

Latchi Beach, Polis (4.2 / 5)

Latchi, or Latsi, is a small fishing harbour near Polis with a pebble and coarse sand beach and a line of seafront tavernas serving fresh fish. It is a quiet base for the Akamas peninsula and boat trips along the coast.

Best season: May–October. Typical water temperature: about 25 degrees C. Access: Easy.

Facilities: Tavernas and cafes by the harbour, sunbeds in season, toilets. Getting there and parking: Parking near the harbour and beach.

Venus Beach, Paphos (4.2 / 5)

Venus Beach is a sandy beach on the coastal road north of Kato Paphos, close to the Tombs of the Kings. It is named for Aphrodite, the goddess said to have been born on this coast. Hotels line the road behind the beach and the water is shallow and calm.

Best season: May–October. Typical water temperature: about 25 degrees C. Access: Easy.

Facilities: Sunbeds and umbrellas, cafes, toilets and showers in season. Getting there and parking: Parking along the coastal road and near the hotels.

Pernera Beach, Protaras (4.2 / 5)

Pernera Beach is a sandy resort beach in Protaras with shallow, clear water. Hotels, cafes and shops line the area behind the beach, so it suits families who want everything close by.

Best season: May–October. Typical water temperature: about 25 degrees C. Access: Easy.

Facilities: Sunbeds and umbrellas, cafes and bars nearby. Getting there and parking: Roadside and small car parks nearby.

Kalamies Beach, Protaras (4.2 / 5)

Kalamies Beach is a sandy cove in Protaras with clear shallow water. A small fishing harbour and several tavernas sit close to the shore.

Best season: May–October. Typical water temperature: about 25 degrees C. Access: Easy.

Facilities: Tavernas nearby, sunbeds in season. Getting there and parking: Small car parks nearby.

Finikoudes Beach, Larnaca (4.1 / 5)

Finikoudes is a sandy city beach with a palm-lined promenade in the centre of Larnaca, with shallow water and cafes just behind the sand. It is lively in the evening and convenient for visitors without a car.

Best season: May–October. Typical water temperature: about 26 degrees C. Access: Easy.

Facilities: Sunbeds and umbrellas, cafes, restaurants, toilets and showers close by. Getting there and parking: Paid parking in the town centre near the seafront.

Mackenzie Beach, Larnaca (4.1 / 5)

Mackenzie Beach is a sandy beach just south of Larnaca with a row of bars and tavernas along the sand. It lies close to the airport, so planes pass low overhead on landing, which is part of the attraction for plane spotters. The water is shallow and warm.

Best season: May–October. Typical water temperature: about 26 degrees C. Access: Easy.

Facilities: Sunbeds and umbrellas, bars, tavernas, toilets, showers and watersports. Getting there and parking: Car parks and roadside parking behind the beach.

Kefalos Beach, Paphos (4.1 / 5)

Kefalos Beach is a sandy beach near the Paphos lighthouse on the west edge of Kato Paphos. It is usually quieter than the harbour area, with calm, shallow water and a view of the lighthouse at the point.

Best season: May–October. Typical water temperature: about 25 degrees C. Access: Easy.

Facilities: Sunbeds and umbrellas in season, cafes and toilets close by. Getting there and parking: Parking near the lighthouse and along the coastal road.

Polyxenia Beach, Protaras (4.1 / 5)

Polyxenia Beach is a short sandy beach in Protaras, close to Pernera. The sea is shallow and clear, and the beach is backed by hotels and tavernas.

Best season: May–October. Typical water temperature: about 25 degrees C. Access: Easy.

Facilities: Sunbeds and umbrellas in season, tavernas nearby. Getting there and parking: Roadside parking nearby.

Polis Chrysochous Beach (4.1 / 5)

Polis Chrysochous Beach is the town beach of Polis on the north-west coast. It is a long stretch of sand and pebbles with calm water, close to the shops and cafes, and the base for exploring the Akamas Peninsula.

Best season: May–October. Typical water temperature: about 25 degrees C. Access: Easy.

Facilities: Cafes and shops in town, sunbeds in season. Getting there and parking: Parking in Polis town near the beach.

Alykes Beach, Kato Paphos (4.0 / 5)

Alykes Beach is a small town beach in Kato Paphos, close to the harbour, the castle and the archaeological sites. It works well for a short swim between sightseeing stops, with cafes and restaurants within easy walking distance.

Best season: May–October. Typical water temperature: about 25 degrees C. Access: Easy.

Facilities: Cafes, restaurants and toilets nearby; sunbeds in season. Getting there and parking: Public parking in Kato Paphos; spaces are limited near the harbour in summer.

Faros Beach, Pervolia (4.0 / 5)

Faros Beach is a quiet local sandy beach near the village of Pervolia on the coast south-west of Larnaca. It has fewer hotels and crowds than the main Larnaca beaches and is popular with local families at weekends.

Best season: May–October. Typical water temperature: about 26 degrees C. Access: Easy.

Facilities: Limited; a taverna or snack bar nearby in season, so bring water and shade. Getting there and parking: Informal parking near the beach.

Kissonerga Beach, Paphos (4.0 / 5)

Kissonerga Beach is a quiet stretch of pebbles and rock on the coast north of Paphos, below the village of Kissonerga. The water is clear and the mood is relaxed, with a few tavernas and beach bars close to the shore.

Best season: May–October. Typical water temperature: about 25 degrees C. Access: Easy.

Facilities: Beach bar or taverna nearby in season, limited other facilities. Getting there and parking: Small car parks and roadside parking near the shore.

Agios Georgios Beach, Pegeia (4.0 / 5)

Agios Georgios is a small harbour area on the Pegeia coast with a church, a few tavernas and a pebble and rock shoreline. It is quiet and good for a calm swim and a seafood lunch.

Best season: May–October. Typical water temperature: about 25 degrees C. Access: Easy.

Facilities: Tavernas near the harbour, few other facilities. Getting there and parking: Parking near the harbour.

Kato Pyrgos Beach, Tillyria (4.0 / 5)

Kato Pyrgos is a remote pebble coast in the Tillyria region, far from the big resorts. The beaches are quiet, the scenery is wild, and a few simple tavernas serve the area.

Best season: May–October. Typical water temperature: about 25 degrees C. Access: Easy.

Facilities: Simple tavernas nearby, very few other facilities. Getting there and parking: Roadside parking near the shore.

Pomos Beach, Tillyria (4.0 / 5)

Pomos is a small fishing village on the Tillyria coast with a pebble shoreline and a quiet atmosphere. It is a calm place to swim and eat fish away from the busier resorts.

Best season: May–October. Typical water temperature: about 25 degrees C. Access: Easy.

Facilities: Tavernas in the village, few other facilities. Getting there and parking: Parking near the harbour and shore.

About beaches in Cyprus

Cyprus has warm, clear water, sandy bays around Ayia Napa and Protaras, quieter pebble coves near Paphos and Polis, and a long swimming season. Most beaches have sunbeds and cafes in summer, and the island is easy to explore by car.

When to go

May to October is the main swimming season, and the sea stays warm into November. July and August are the hottest and busiest months, while May, June and September give warm water with more space.

Getting around

Larnaca and Paphos airports serve most visitors. Public buses link the main towns, but a hire car is the easiest way to reach quieter coves. Cyprus drives on the left.

Good to know

Cyprus uses the euro. Sunbeds and umbrellas are usually paid, and public nudity is not allowed. Protect yourself from the strong midday sun and check flags and local signs before swimming.

Frequently asked questions

When is the best time to swim in Cyprus?

June and September offer warm water with fewer people. July and August are the hottest and busiest months.

Do you need a car to visit Cyprus beaches?

Resort beaches such as Nissi Beach and Fig Tree Bay are easy by bus or taxi, but a car helps for Konnos Bay and the quieter coves on the west coast.
