Sand or pebble?

72% of our beaches are sand, 24% are pebble and 4% are rocky. The balance differs a lot by country, so check the table above: countries with a high pebble share, such as Croatia, call for water shoes, while sand-heavy countries suit families with small children.

Warmest and coolest seas

Temperatures are typical in-season values in degrees Celsius, not live readings. Averaged by country, the warmest in our data are Cyprus (25.4), Turkey (25.3), Malta (24.8), and the coolest are Portugal (20.5), France (20.9), Spain (22.5).

How to read these numbers

The dataset is editorial and selective: we cover beaches worth planning a trip around, not every shoreline, so country counts are not a measure of coastline length or quality. Temperatures and scores are approximate. See how we pick and rate beaches for the method. Last updated 2026-11-14.
