Planning your visit to Super Paradise Beach

Sand beaches are usually gentle underfoot and easy for swimmers and children, though they can be exposed to wind and waves, so check conditions on the day. Bring a hat and extra water, as shade can be scarce away from facilities.

Access is rated easy, so most visitors can reach the beach without a long walk or special equipment.

The usual season runs May–September. Visiting near the start or end of that window usually means fewer people, but the sea is cooler and some facilities may not be open yet or may have closed for the year.

Best months to visit Super Paradise Beach

The swimming season here runs from May to September. June, July, August are the warmest and busiest, with the sea around 25 °C, so arrive early for parking and a good spot. May and September are quieter and cooler: good for walking and photos, though some beach bars and lifeguard posts may not be open yet or may already have closed for the year.

Who Super Paradise Beach suits

Sand makes it comfortable for sunbathing, building sandcastles and long walks along the water line. Easy access makes it practical for families, older visitors and anyone with a pushchair. Food and drink close by make it easy to stay for the whole day.

Getting there and nearby

Free parking above the beach; the final stretch of road is steep, and valet attendants may expect a tip. The beach sits at about 37.415, 25.369 on the map, so use the map link above for directions. If you are planning a day around it, the closest other beaches on our list are Elia Beach (0 km), Paradise Beach (1 km), Platis Gialos Beach (2 km).
