Butterfly Valley Beach, Faralya

4.5 / 5 Hidden cove at the foot of a canyon reached by boat in Butterfly Valley, Faralya, Fethiye, Turkey · Hidden gem

Butterfly Valley is a hidden cove at the foot of steep cliffs and a forested canyon near Faralya. Most visitors arrive by boat from Oludeniz, and there is no road to the beach. The setting is quiet and dramatic, with a small waterfall inside the valley.

Best months to visit Butterfly Valley Beach, Faralya

The swimming season here runs from May to October. June, July, August, September are the warmest and busiest, with the sea around 26 °C, so arrive early for parking and a good spot. May and October are quieter and cooler: good for walking and photos, though some beach bars and lifeguard posts may not be open yet or may already have closed for the year.

Who Butterfly Valley Beach, Faralya suits

A pebble shore means clear water for swimming and snorkelling; shoes for the water help on the stones. Access is demanding, so it suits fit, well-prepared visitors; wear sturdy shoes and carry water. It is flagged as a hidden gem, so it suits travellers who prefer quieter places over the headline beaches. Food and drink close by make it easy to stay for the whole day.

Getting there and nearby

No road access, so take a boat from Oludeniz. Use the map link above for directions. If you are planning a day around it, the closest other beaches on our list are Kabak Bay, Fethiye (4 km), Oludeniz Beach, Fethiye (6 km), Calis Beach, Fethiye (19 km).
