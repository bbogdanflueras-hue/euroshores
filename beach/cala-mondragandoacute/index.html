Planning your visit to Cala Mondrag&oacute;

Sand beaches are usually gentle underfoot and easy for swimmers and children, though they can be exposed to wind and waves, so check conditions on the day. Bring a hat and extra water, as shade can be scarce away from facilities.

Access is rated easy, so most visitors can reach the beach without a long walk or special equipment.

The usual season runs May–October. Visiting near the start or end of that window usually means fewer people, but the sea is cooler and some facilities may not be open yet or may have closed for the year.

Frequently asked questions

When is the best time to visit Cala Mondrag&oacute;?

Cala Mondrag&oacute; is best visited May to October. The sea is typically around 24 °C in season. Arrive before 10 AM in July and August for parking, and bring cash or a card for the car park fee.

How do I get to Cala Mondrag&oacute;?

Access is rated easy. Paid car parks at both ends, about a 5–10 minute walk from the sand; they can be full by late morning in high season.

What facilities does Cala Mondrag&oacute; have?

Showers and a restaurant; walking trails through the natural park.

Where is Cala Mondrag&oacute;?

Cala Mondrag&oacute; is in Mallorca, Spain (approx. coordinates 39.3492, 3.1903).

Is Cala Mondrag&oacute; good for children?

Access is rated easy and the beach is sandy, which usually suits families. Check water and weather conditions on the day and keep children in sight near the water.

What should I bring to Cala Mondrag&oacute;?

Facilities: Showers and a restaurant; walking trails through the natural park. A hat and sun protection are useful. Bring water and snacks if there is no kiosk or restaurant.
