Planning your visit to Pampelonne Beach

Sand beaches are usually gentle underfoot and easy for swimmers and children, though they can be exposed to wind and waves, so check conditions on the day. Bring a hat and extra water, as shade can be scarce away from facilities.

Access is rated easy, so most visitors can reach the beach without a long walk or special equipment.

The usual season runs June–September. Visiting near the start or end of that window usually means fewer people, but the sea is cooler and some facilities may not be open yet or may have closed for the year.

Frequently asked questions

When is the best time to visit Pampelonne Beach?

Pampelonne Beach is best visited June to September. The sea is typically around 23 °C in season. The clubs take most of the sand in peak season, so look for the public sections if you want to bring your own towel. Go early or by bike to avoid queues on the road.

How do I get to Pampelonne Beach?

Access is rated easy. Paid car parks along the access roads; traffic to the beach is heavy in July and August.

What facilities does Pampelonne Beach have?

Beach clubs with paid sunbeds, restaurants and bars, water-sports hire, and public free stretches.

Where is Pampelonne Beach?

Pampelonne Beach is in Ramatuelle, Provence, France (approx. coordinates 43.2276, 6.6648).

Is Pampelonne Beach good for children?

Access is rated easy and the beach is sandy, which usually suits families. Check water and weather conditions on the day and keep children in sight near the water.

What should I bring to Pampelonne Beach?

Facilities: Beach clubs with paid sunbeds, restaurants and bars, water-sports hire, and public free stretches. A hat and sun protection are useful. Bring water and snacks if there is no kiosk or restaurant.

Is there parking at Pampelonne Beach?

Paid, about EUR 4-5/day; hard in Jul-Aug; some clubs offer valet. Campers and motorhomes: Paid motorhome area behind the beach: EUR 22/night from 1 April 2026 (single night tickets), no electricity/toilets/showers; bridge limit 5.5 t. Fees and rules change, so confirm locally.

Are there sunbeds at Pampelonne Beach?

Paid and varied: about EUR 30-60 per person per day, up to EUR 100; Shellona EUR 300-500+ (includes lunch). Free public sand sections. Prices are approximate and change by season.
