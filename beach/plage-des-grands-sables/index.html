Plage des Grands Sables

4.6 / 5 Rare convex beach on a Breton island in Groix, Brittany · Hidden gem

A long sandy beach on the east coast of the island of Groix, famous as one of the few convex beaches in Europe. The shape is created by opposing currents and slowly shifts over time, and the water is clear.

Planning your visit to Plage des Grands Sables

Sand beaches are usually gentle underfoot and easy for swimmers and children, though they can be exposed to wind and waves, so check conditions on the day. Bring a hat and extra water, as shade can be scarce away from facilities.

Access is rated moderate: expect stairs, a short walk or a rough road, and wear proper shoes rather than flip-flops.

The usual season runs June–September. Visiting near the start or end of that window usually means fewer people, but the sea is cooler and some facilities may not be open yet or may have closed for the year. We flag this beach as a hidden gem, so it is quieter than the headline beaches nearby, but it is no secret: arrive early in peak summer.

Best months to visit Plage des Grands Sables

The swimming season here runs from June to September. July, August are the warmest and busiest, with the sea around 18 °C, so arrive early for parking and a good spot. June and September are quieter and cooler: good for walking and photos, though some beach bars and lifeguard posts may not be open yet or may already have closed for the year.

Who Plage des Grands Sables suits

Sand makes it comfortable for sunbathing, building sandcastles and long walks along the water line. Expect a short walk or uneven ground, so it suits most visitors with sensible footwear. It is flagged as a hidden gem, so it suits travellers who prefer quieter places over the headline beaches.

Getting there and nearby

A car park near the beach; most visitors arrive by bike. The beach sits at about 47.635, -3.421 on the map, so use the map link above for directions.
