Planning your visit to Myrtos Beach

Pebble beaches usually mean clear water but awkward walking. Bring water shoes and a thick towel or mat to sit on.

Access is rated moderate: expect stairs, a short walk or a rough road, and wear proper shoes rather than flip-flops.

The usual season runs June–September. Visiting near the start or end of that window usually means fewer people, but the sea is cooler and some facilities may not be open yet or may have closed for the year.

Frequently asked questions

When is the best time to visit Myrtos Beach?

Myrtos Beach is best visited June to September. The sea is typically around 27 °C in season. Visit before midday — shade disappears early in the afternoon.

How do I get to Myrtos Beach?

Access is rated moderate. Viewpoint car park above; winding road down.

What facilities does Myrtos Beach have?

Small cafe, sunbeds, viewpoint car park.

Where is Myrtos Beach?

Myrtos Beach is in Kefalonia, Greece (approx. coordinates 38.335, 20.542).

Is Myrtos Beach good for children?

Access is rated moderate, so check that it suits young children before you go. Our family guide lists easier options.

What should I bring to Myrtos Beach?

Facilities: Small cafe, sunbeds, viewpoint car park. Water shoes help on the pebbles. Bring water and snacks if there is no kiosk or restaurant.
