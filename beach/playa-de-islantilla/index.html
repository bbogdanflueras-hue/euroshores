Playa de Islantilla

4.2 / 5 Quiet golden beach with low dunes and golf courses in Islantilla, Huelva, Costa de la Luz

Islantilla is a long, quiet beach of golden sand on the Huelva coast, with low dunes, pine woods and golf courses behind. The tide goes out a long way, leaving wide flats of firm sand, and the sea is calmer than on the open coast further east.

Planning your visit to Playa de Islantilla

Sand beaches are usually gentle underfoot and easy for swimmers and children, though they can be exposed to wind and waves, so check conditions on the day. Bring a hat and extra water, as shade can be scarce away from facilities.

Access is rated easy, so most visitors can reach the beach without a long walk or special equipment.

The usual season runs June–September. Visiting near the start or end of that window usually means fewer people, but the sea is cooler and some facilities may not be open yet or may have closed for the year.

Best months to visit Playa de Islantilla

The swimming season here runs from June to September. July, August are the warmest and busiest, with the sea around 22 °C, so arrive early for parking and a good spot. June and September are quieter and cooler: good for walking and photos, though some beach bars and lifeguard posts may not be open yet or may already have closed for the year.

Who Playa de Islantilla suits

Sand makes it comfortable for sunbathing, building sandcastles and long walks along the water line. Easy access makes it practical for families, older visitors and anyone with a pushchair. Food and drink close by make it easy to stay for the whole day.

Getting there and nearby

Free parking along the seafront; larger paid lots near the resort. The beach sits at about 37.203, -7.225 on the map, so use the map link above for directions. If you are planning a day around it, the closest other beaches on our list are Playa de Isla Canela, Huelva (13 km), Playa de Mazagon, Huelva (36 km), Playa de Matalascanas (64 km).
