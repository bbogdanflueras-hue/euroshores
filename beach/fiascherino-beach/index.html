Best months to visit Fiascherino Beach

The swimming season here runs from May to September. June, July, August are the warmest and busiest, with the sea around 22 °C, so arrive early for parking and a good spot. May and September are quieter and cooler: good for walking and photos, though some beach bars and lifeguard posts may not be open yet or may already have closed for the year.

Who Fiascherino Beach suits

Pebbles and clear water suit swimmers and snorkellers; bring water shoes for an easier entry. Easy access makes it practical for families, older visitors and anyone with a pushchair. It is flagged as a hidden gem, so it suits travellers who prefer quieter places over the headline beaches.
