Planning your visit to Spiaggia dei Conigli

Sand beaches are usually gentle underfoot and easy for swimmers and children, though they can be exposed to wind and waves, so check conditions on the day. Bring a hat and extra water, as shade can be scarce away from facilities.

Access is rated moderate: expect stairs, a short walk or a rough road, and wear proper shoes rather than flip-flops.

The usual season runs June–October. Visiting near the start or end of that window usually means fewer people, but the sea is cooler and some facilities may not be open yet or may have closed for the year.

Frequently asked questions

When is the best time to visit Spiaggia dei Conigli?

Spiaggia dei Conigli is best visited June to October. The sea is typically around 25 °C in season. Book your slot well in advance for July and August and wear proper shoes for the path down. The return walk is uphill, so save energy and water for it.

How do I get to Spiaggia dei Conigli?

Access is rated moderate. About an 800 metre walk from the road, with a bus, bike or scooter to the trailhead. In summer entry is limited to booked shifts through the reserve, so reserve ahead on the official booking site.

What facilities does Spiaggia dei Conigli have?

None on the beach: no lifeguards, toilets, shade or refreshments, so bring water, food and sun protection.

Where is Spiaggia dei Conigli?

Spiaggia dei Conigli is in Lampedusa, Sicily, Italy (approx. coordinates 35.513, 12.5575).

Is Spiaggia dei Conigli good for children?

Access is rated moderate, so check that it suits young children before you go. Our family guide lists easier options.

What should I bring to Spiaggia dei Conigli?

Facilities: None on the beach: no lifeguards, toilets, shade or refreshments, so bring water, food and sun protection. A hat and sun protection are useful. Bring water and snacks if there is no kiosk or restaurant.
