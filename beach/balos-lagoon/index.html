Planning your visit to Balos Lagoon

Pink sand gets its tint from crushed shell and coral fragments. Some pink-sand beaches are protected, so take nothing away and follow any posted rules.

Access is rated moderate: expect stairs, a short walk or a rough road, and wear proper shoes rather than flip-flops.

The usual season runs May–October. Visiting near the start or end of that window usually means fewer people, but the sea is cooler and some facilities may not be open yet or may have closed for the year. We flag this beach as a hidden gem, so it is quieter than the headline beaches nearby, but it is no secret: arrive early in peak summer.

Frequently asked questions

When is the best time to visit Balos Lagoon?

Balos Lagoon is best visited May to October. The sea is typically around 25 °C in season. Go early — the day-boat crowds arrive late morning.

How do I get to Balos Lagoon?

Access is rated moderate. Unpaved road to the car park, then a stepped path.

What facilities does Balos Lagoon have?

Canteen, sunbed rental in season.

Where is Balos Lagoon?

Balos Lagoon is in Crete, Greece (approx. coordinates 35.5917, 23.5917).

Is Balos Lagoon good for children?

Access is rated moderate, so check that it suits young children before you go. Our family guide lists easier options.

What should I bring to Balos Lagoon?

Facilities: Canteen, sunbed rental in season. A hat and sun protection are useful. Bring water and snacks if there is no kiosk or restaurant.
