Planning your visit to Balos Lagoon

Pink sand gets its tint from crushed shell and coral fragments. Some pink-sand beaches are protected, so take nothing away and follow any posted rules.

Access is rated moderate: expect stairs, a short walk or a rough road, and wear proper shoes rather than flip-flops.

The usual season runs May–October. Visiting near the start or end of that window usually means fewer people, but the sea is cooler and some facilities may not be open yet or may have closed for the year. We flag this beach as a hidden gem, so it is quieter than the headline beaches nearby, but it is no secret: arrive early in peak summer.

Best months to visit Balos Lagoon

The swimming season here runs from May to October. June, July, August, September are the warmest and busiest, with the sea around 25 °C, so arrive early for parking and a good spot. May and October are quieter and cooler: good for walking and photos, though some beach bars and lifeguard posts may not be open yet or may already have closed for the year.

Who Balos Lagoon suits

The pink-tinted sand makes it a favourite for photographers and couples. Expect a short walk or uneven ground, so it suits most visitors with sensible footwear. It is flagged as a hidden gem, so it suits travellers who prefer quieter places over the headline beaches.

Getting there and nearby

Unpaved road to the car park, then a stepped path. The beach sits at about 35.592, 23.592 on the map, so use the map link above for directions.
