Scilla Marina Grande

A long town beach in the old fishing town of Scilla, with the Ruffo castle rising above one end and views across to Sicily. The sand is pale and the water is clean, and the old quarter of Chianalea is a short walk away.

Best months to visit Scilla Marina Grande

Most people swim at Scilla Marina Grande between June and September. The warmest and busiest months are July, August, when the sea sits around 25 °C. Shoulder months bring cooler water and fewer crowds, although some bars and lifeguard posts may be closed.

Who Scilla Marina Grande suits

This is a sand beach, which makes it easy for sunbathing, sandcastles and a long walk at the water edge. The simple approach makes it a practical choice for families and visitors who prefer not to walk far. Food and drink close by make it easy to stay for the whole day.

Getting there and nearby

A car park near the beach, paid in summer, plus street parking in town. Use the map link above for directions. If you are planning a day around it, the closest other beaches on our list are Capo Vaticano Beach, Calabria (42 km), Tropea Beach (50 km), Mazzaro Beach, Taormina (57 km).

Frequently asked questions

When is the best time to visit Scilla Marina Grande?

Scilla Marina Grande is best visited June to September. On average the sea is warmest in August, at about 27 °C, and coolest in February, at about 14 °C. Walk up to the castle terrace for the best view. Chianalea is uphill from the beach, so allow extra time.

How do I get to Scilla Marina Grande?

Access is rated easy. A car park near the beach, paid in summer, plus street parking in town.

Is Scilla Marina Grande good for children?

Access is rated easy and the beach is sandy, which usually suits families. Check water and weather conditions on the day and keep children in sight near the water.

Is there parking at Scilla Marina Grande?

A car park near the beach (reported about 2 EUR per hour) and street parking. Fees and rules change, so confirm locally.

Are there sunbeds at Scilla Marina Grande?

Sunbeds and umbrellas rented by beach bars. Prices are approximate and change by season.

Do you need water shoes at Scilla Marina Grande?

Useful. Sand with pebbles along the shore; seabed described as rough and rocky.

Is nudism allowed at Scilla Marina Grande?

Forbidden. Clothing is expected; no naturist area is recorded for this beach in our sources.
