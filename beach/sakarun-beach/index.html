Planning your visit to Sakarun Beach

Sand beaches are usually gentle underfoot and easy for swimmers and children, though they can be exposed to wind and waves, so check conditions on the day. Bring a hat and extra water, as shade can be scarce away from facilities.

Access is rated easy, so most visitors can reach the beach without a long walk or special equipment.

The usual season runs June–September. Visiting near the start or end of that window usually means fewer people, but the sea is cooler and some facilities may not be open yet or may have closed for the year. We flag this beach as a hidden gem, so it is quieter than the headline beaches nearby, but it is no secret: arrive early in peak summer.

Frequently asked questions

When is the best time to visit Sakarun Beach?

Sakarun Beach is best visited June to September. The sea is typically around 25 °C in season. Shallow, warm water makes it a good pick for young children.

How do I get to Sakarun Beach?

Access is rated easy. Parking above the bay.

What facilities does Sakarun Beach have?

Beach bar, sunbed rental.

Where is Sakarun Beach?

Sakarun Beach is in Dugi Otok, Croatia (approx. coordinates 44.13, 14.97).

Is Sakarun Beach good for children?

Access is rated easy and the beach is sandy, which usually suits families. Check water and weather conditions on the day and keep children in sight near the water.

What should I bring to Sakarun Beach?

Facilities: Beach bar, sunbed rental. A hat and sun protection are useful. Bring water and snacks if there is no kiosk or restaurant.
