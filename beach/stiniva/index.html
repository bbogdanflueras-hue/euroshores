Planning your visit to Stiniva

Pebble beaches usually mean clear water but awkward walking. Bring water shoes and a thick towel or mat to sit on.

Access is rated challenging and involves a steep hike. Take plenty of water, start early and allow enough daylight for the walk back.

The usual season runs May–September. Visiting near the start or end of that window usually means fewer people, but the sea is cooler and some facilities may not be open yet or may have closed for the year. We flag this beach as a hidden gem, so it is quieter than the headline beaches nearby, but it is no secret: arrive early in peak summer.

Frequently asked questions

When is the best time to visit Stiniva?

Stiniva is best visited May to September. The sea is typically around 24 °C in season. Go early, before the tour boats arrive, and wear sturdy closed shoes. The path is steep and the climb back up is the harder part.

How do I get to Stiniva?

Access is rated challenging. Trailhead above the cove, then a steep path of about 20 minutes down; boat tours also visit.

What facilities does Stiniva have?

Minimal — bring water, shade and snacks.

Where is Stiniva?

Stiniva is in Vis, Croatia (approx. coordinates 43.0184, 16.171).

Is Stiniva good for children?

Access is rated challenging, so check that it suits young children before you go. Our family guide lists easier options.

What should I bring to Stiniva?

Facilities: Minimal — bring water, shade and snacks. Water shoes help on the pebbles. Bring water and snacks if there is no kiosk or restaurant.
