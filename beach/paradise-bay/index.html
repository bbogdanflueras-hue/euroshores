Planning your visit to Paradise Bay

Sand beaches are usually gentle underfoot and easy for swimmers and children, though they can be exposed to wind and waves, so check conditions on the day. Bring a hat and extra water, as shade can be scarce away from facilities.

Access is rated moderate: expect stairs, a short walk or a rough road, and wear proper shoes rather than flip-flops.

The usual season runs June–September. Visiting near the start or end of that window usually means fewer people, but the sea is cooler and some facilities may not be open yet or may have closed for the year.

Frequently asked questions

When is the best time to visit Paradise Bay?

Paradise Bay is best visited June to September. The sea is typically around 25 °C in season. Arrive early, as sunbeds are first come, first served and the bay is often full by about 10 AM in summer. The climb back up takes effort. The public bay is separate from the hotel's private beach.

How do I get to Paradise Bay?

Access is rated moderate. Car park at the top, then about 100 steps down; public transport is limited.

What facilities does Paradise Bay have?

Sunbed and umbrella hire, refreshments and a restaurant, toilets and showers.

Where is Paradise Bay?

Paradise Bay is in Mellieha, Malta (approx. coordinates 35.982, 14.3326).

Is Paradise Bay good for children?

Access is rated moderate, so check that it suits young children before you go. Our family guide lists easier options.

What should I bring to Paradise Bay?

Facilities: Sunbed and umbrella hire, refreshments and a restaurant, toilets and showers. A hat and sun protection are useful. Bring water and snacks if there is no kiosk or restaurant.
