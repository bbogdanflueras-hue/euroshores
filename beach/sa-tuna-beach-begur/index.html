Sa Tuna Beach, Begur

4.6 / 5 Tiny cove with fishermen houses on the Costa Brava in Sa Tuna, Begur, Girona · Hidden gem

Sa Tuna is a tiny cove near Begur on the Costa Brava, with a small beach of coarse sand and a row of whitewashed fishermen houses along the shore. Pine-covered cliffs surround the bay and the water is clear. A coast path links Sa Tuna to the neighbouring coves.

Best months to visit Sa Tuna Beach, Begur

The swimming season here runs from June to September. July, August are the warmest and busiest, with the sea around 22 °C, so arrive early for parking and a good spot. June and September are quieter and cooler: good for walking and photos, though some beach bars and lifeguard posts may not be open yet or may already have closed for the year.

Who Sa Tuna Beach, Begur suits

Sand makes it comfortable for sunbathing, building sandcastles and long walks along the water line. Easy access makes it practical for families, older visitors and anyone with a pushchair. It is flagged as a hidden gem, so it suits travellers who prefer quieter places over the headline beaches. Food and drink close by make it easy to stay for the whole day.
