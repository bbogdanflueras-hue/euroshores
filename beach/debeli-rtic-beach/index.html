Debeli Rtic Beach

4.2 / 5 Protected cape with quiet coves in Debeli Rtic, Ankaran, Slovenia · Hidden gem

Debeli rtic is a protected headland at the north-western tip of the Slovenian coast, near the Italian border, with low cliffs, woodland and small pebble coves. It is a natural monument and much quieter than the resorts to the south.

Best months to visit Debeli Rtic Beach

The swimming season here runs from June to September. July, August are the warmest and busiest, with the sea around 22 °C, so arrive early for parking and a good spot. June and September are quieter and cooler: good for walking and photos, though some beach bars and lifeguard posts may not be open yet or may already have closed for the year.

Who Debeli Rtic Beach suits

Pebbles and clear water suit swimmers and snorkellers; bring water shoes for an easier entry. Expect a short walk or uneven ground, so it suits most visitors with sensible footwear. It is flagged as a hidden gem, so it suits travellers who prefer quieter places over the headline beaches. There are few or no facilities on the sand, so bring water, food and shade.

Getting there and nearby

Parking near the Debeli rtic centre, then a short walk to the coves. Use the map link above for directions. If you are planning a day around it, the closest other beaches on our list are Simonov Zaliv Beach, Izola (8 km), Moon Bay (Mesecev Zaliv), Strunjan (9 km), Portoroz Central Beach (12 km).
