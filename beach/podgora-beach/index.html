Planning your visit to Podgora Beach

Pebble beaches usually mean clear water but awkward walking. Bring water shoes and a thick towel or mat to sit on.

Access is rated easy, so most visitors can reach the beach without a long walk or special equipment.

The usual season runs June–September. Visiting near the start or end of that window usually means fewer people, but the sea is cooler and some facilities may not be open yet or may have closed for the year.

Best months to visit Podgora Beach

The swimming season here runs from June to September. July, August are the warmest and busiest, with the sea around 25 °C, so arrive early for parking and a good spot. June and September are quieter and cooler: good for walking and photos, though some beach bars and lifeguard posts may not be open yet or may already have closed for the year.

Who Podgora Beach suits

Pebbles and clear water suit swimmers and snorkellers; bring water shoes for an easier entry. Easy access makes it practical for families, older visitors and anyone with a pushchair. Food and drink close by make it easy to stay for the whole day.

Getting there and nearby

Paid parking in town and near the beach. The beach sits at about 43.249, 17.067 on the map, so use the map link above for directions. If you are planning a day around it, the closest other beaches on our list are Tucepi Beach (3 km), Nugal Beach (5 km), Igrane Beach, Makarska Riviera (8 km).
