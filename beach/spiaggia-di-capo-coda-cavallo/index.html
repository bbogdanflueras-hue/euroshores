Spiaggia di Capo Coda Cavallo

4.5 / 5 Wild curved bay of pale sand and red rocks in north-east Sardinia in Capo Coda Cavallo, San Teodoro, Sardinia · Hidden gem

Capo Coda Cavallo is a protected headland south of San Teodoro in north-east Sardinia, with a long curve of pale sand, red granite rocks and clear turquoise water. The area is part of a marine reserve, and the macchia scrub behind the beach is left wild.

Planning your visit to Spiaggia di Capo Coda Cavallo

Sand beaches are usually gentle underfoot and easy for swimmers and children, though they can be exposed to wind and waves, so check conditions on the day. Bring a hat and extra water, as shade can be scarce away from facilities.

Access is rated easy, so most visitors can reach the beach without a long walk or special equipment.

The usual season runs June–September. Visiting near the start or end of that window usually means fewer people, but the sea is cooler and some facilities may not be open yet or may have closed for the year. We flag this beach as a hidden gem, so it is quieter than the headline beaches nearby, but it is no secret: arrive early in peak summer.

Best months to visit Spiaggia di Capo Coda Cavallo

The swimming season here runs from June to September. July, August are the warmest and busiest, with the sea around 25 °C, so arrive early for parking and a good spot. June and September are quieter and cooler: good for walking and photos, though some beach bars and lifeguard posts may not be open yet or may already have closed for the year.

Who Spiaggia di Capo Coda Cavallo suits

Sand makes it comfortable for sunbathing, building sandcastles and long walks along the water line. Easy access makes it practical for families, older visitors and anyone with a pushchair. It is flagged as a hidden gem, so it suits travellers who prefer quieter places over the headline beaches. There are few or no facilities on the sand, so bring water, food and shade.

Getting there and nearby

Paid parking at the access points; limited spaces in August. The beach sits at about 40.864, 9.705 on the map, so use the map link above for directions. If you are planning a day around it, the closest other beaches on our list are Cala Brandinchi (4 km), La Cinta Beach (8 km), Budoni Beach (17 km).
