Planning your visit to Zlatni Rat

Pebble beaches usually mean clear water but awkward walking. Bring water shoes and a thick towel or mat to sit on.

Access is rated easy, so most visitors can reach the beach without a long walk or special equipment.

The usual season runs May–October. Visiting near the start or end of that window usually means fewer people, but the sea is cooler and some facilities may not be open yet or may have closed for the year.

Frequently asked questions

When is the best time to visit Zlatni Rat?

Zlatni Rat is best visited May to October. The sea is typically around 26 °C in season. Windy afternoons make it a top windsurfing spot — mornings are calmer.

How do I get to Zlatni Rat?

Access is rated easy. Car park in Bol, short walk.

What facilities does Zlatni Rat have?

Water sports, beach bars, showers.

Where is Zlatni Rat?

Zlatni Rat is in Brač Island, Croatia (approx. coordinates 43.2569, 16.6372).

Is Zlatni Rat good for children?

Access is rated easy, so check that it suits young children before you go. Our family guide lists easier options.

What should I bring to Zlatni Rat?

Facilities: Water sports, beach bars, showers. Water shoes help on the pebbles. Bring water and snacks if there is no kiosk or restaurant.
