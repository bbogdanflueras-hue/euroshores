Valdanos Bay

4.5 / 5 Hidden Cove in Ulcinj, Montenegro · Hidden gem

Photo: Zlatomir Bacic, CC BY-SA 3.0, via Wikimedia Commons

A crescent-shaped bay of rounded pebbles, roughly 300 metres long, tucked below old olive groves on the south coast of Montenegro near Ulcinj.

Planning your visit to Valdanos Bay

Pebble beaches usually mean clear water but awkward walking. Bring water shoes and a thick towel or mat to sit on.

Access is rated moderate: expect stairs, a short walk or a rough road, and wear proper shoes rather than flip-flops.

The usual season runs June–September. Visiting near the start or end of that window usually means fewer people, but the sea is cooler and some facilities may not be open yet or may have closed for the year. We flag this beach as a hidden gem, so it is quieter than the headline beaches nearby, but it is no secret: arrive early in peak summer.

Frequently asked questions

When is the best time to visit Valdanos Bay?

Valdanos Bay is best visited June to September. The sea is typically around 24 °C in season. The olive groves give some shade behind the beach. Water shoes help on the pebbles, and the bay is much quieter on weekdays and outside July and August.

How do I get to Valdanos Bay?

Access is rated moderate. By car or boat from Ulcinj; the last stretch is on a rough track, so check road conditions and expect to walk the final part.

What facilities does Valdanos Bay have?

Very limited, with a small seasonal snack bar at best, so bring water and shade.

Where is Valdanos Bay?

Valdanos Bay is in Ulcinj, Montenegro (approx. coordinates 41.9552, 19.1588).

Is Valdanos Bay good for children?

Access is rated moderate, so check that it suits young children before you go. Our family guide lists easier options.

What should I bring to Valdanos Bay?

Facilities: Very limited, with a small seasonal snack bar at best, so bring water and shade. Water shoes help on the pebbles. Bring water and snacks if there is no kiosk or restaurant.
