Frequently asked questions

When is the best time to visit Praia Dona Ana?

Praia Dona Ana is best visited May to October. The sea is typically around 21 °C in season. Arrive before 10am in July and August, or visit near sunset for the best light.

How do I get to Praia Dona Ana?

Access is rated easy. Car park near the top of the steps (fills early in summer).

What facilities does Praia Dona Ana have?

Steps down to the beach; restaurants and shops in nearby Lagos.

Where is Praia Dona Ana?

Praia Dona Ana is in Lagos, Algarve, Portugal (approx. coordinates 37.0956, -8.7669).
