Rossnowlagh Beach

4.2 / 5 Long Donegal beach for surfing and walks in Rossnowlagh, Donegal, Ireland

Rossnowlagh is a long, wide beach of firm sand on Donegal Bay, popular with surfers and families. Gentle, consistent waves make it one of the best places in Ireland to learn to surf.

Best months to visit Rossnowlagh Beach

Most people swim at Rossnowlagh Beach between June and August. The warmest and busiest month is July, when the sea sits around 14 °C. Shoulder months bring cooler water and fewer crowds, although some bars and lifeguard posts may be closed.

Who Rossnowlagh Beach suits

Sand makes it comfortable for sunbathing, building sandcastles and long walks along the water line. Easy access makes it practical for families, older visitors and anyone with a pushchair. Food and drink close by make it easy to stay for the whole day.

Frequently asked questions

When is the best time to visit Rossnowlagh Beach?

Rossnowlagh Beach is best visited June to August. On average the sea is warmest in August, at about 16 °C, and coolest in February, at about 8 °C. Walk to the Franciscan friary and its gardens above the beach.

How do I get to Rossnowlagh Beach?

Access is rated easy. Parking near the beach.

Is Rossnowlagh Beach good for children?

Access is rated easy and the beach is sandy, which usually suits families. Check water and weather conditions on the day and keep children in sight near the water.

Do you need water shoes at Rossnowlagh Beach?

Not needed. Sand beach; no rocks or urchins noted in our sources.

Is nudism allowed at Rossnowlagh Beach?

Forbidden. Clothing is expected; no naturist area is recorded for this beach in our sources.
