Planning your visit to Golden Bay

Sand beaches are usually gentle underfoot and easy for swimmers and children, though they can be exposed to wind and waves, so check conditions on the day. Bring a hat and extra water, as shade can be scarce away from facilities.

Access is rated easy, so most visitors can reach the beach without a long walk or special equipment.

The usual season runs June–September. Visiting near the start or end of that window usually means fewer people, but the sea is cooler and some facilities may not be open yet or may have closed for the year.

Frequently asked questions

When is the best time to visit Golden Bay?

Golden Bay is best visited June to September. The sea is typically around 25 °C in season. Arrive early in July and August for parking, and take care when the wind is from the north-west, as currents can be strong.

How do I get to Golden Bay?

Access is rated easy. Car park above the beach (fills by mid-morning in summer), then steps or a ramp down; buses run from Valletta and Sliema.

What facilities does Golden Bay have?

Cafe-restaurant, sunbed and umbrella hire, toilets and lifeguards in summer.

Where is Golden Bay?

Golden Bay is in Mellieha, Malta (approx. coordinates 35.934, 14.343).

Is Golden Bay good for children?

Access is rated easy and the beach is sandy, which usually suits families. Check water and weather conditions on the day and keep children in sight near the water.

What should I bring to Golden Bay?

Facilities: Cafe-restaurant, sunbed and umbrella hire, toilets and lifeguards in summer. A hat and sun protection are useful. Bring water and snacks if there is no kiosk or restaurant.
