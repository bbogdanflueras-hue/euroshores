Planning your visit to Damnoni Beach

Sand beaches are usually gentle underfoot and easy for swimmers and children, though they can be exposed to wind and waves, so check conditions on the day. Bring a hat and extra water, as shade can be scarce away from facilities.

Access is rated easy, so most visitors can reach the beach without a long walk or special equipment.

The usual season runs May–October. Visiting near the start or end of that window usually means fewer people, but the sea is cooler and some facilities may not be open yet or may have closed for the year.

Frequently asked questions

When is the best time to visit Damnoni Beach?

Damnoni Beach is best visited May to October. The sea is typically around 23 °C in season. Check the wind and bring water, as services are seasonal.

How do I get to Damnoni Beach?

Access is rated easy. Parking near the beach; fee unconfirmed.

What facilities does Damnoni Beach have?

Sunbeds and umbrellas; fees unconfirmed.

Where is Damnoni Beach?

Damnoni Beach is in Crete (approx. coordinates 34.1744, 24.4133).

Is Damnoni Beach good for children?

Access is rated easy and the beach is sandy, which usually suits families. Check water and weather conditions on the day and keep children in sight near the water.

What should I bring to Damnoni Beach?

Facilities: Sunbeds and umbrellas; fees unconfirmed. A hat and sun protection are useful. Bring water and snacks if there is no kiosk or restaurant.
