Playa de Castelldefels, Barcelona

4.2 / 5 Wide sandy beach a short train ride from Barcelona in Castelldefels, Barcelona, Catalonia

Castelldefels is one of the longest sandy beaches near Barcelona, with a wide strip of golden sand and a seafront of beach bars. It is reached by a short train ride from the city, and in summer it is busy with families and surfers on windy days.

Planning your visit to Playa de Castelldefels, Barcelona

Sand beaches are usually gentle underfoot and easy for swimmers and children, though they can be exposed to wind and waves, so check conditions on the day. Bring a hat and extra water, as shade can be scarce away from facilities.

Access is rated easy, so most visitors can reach the beach without a long walk or special equipment.

The usual season runs June–September. Visiting near the start or end of that window usually means fewer people, but the sea is cooler and some facilities may not be open yet or may have closed for the year.

Best months to visit Playa de Castelldefels, Barcelona

The swimming season here runs from June to September. July, August are the warmest and busiest, with the sea around 23 °C, so arrive early for parking and a good spot. June and September are quieter and cooler: good for walking and photos, though some beach bars and lifeguard posts may not be open yet or may already have closed for the year.

Who Playa de Castelldefels, Barcelona suits

Sand makes it comfortable for sunbathing, building sandcastles and long walks along the water line. Easy access makes it practical for families, older visitors and anyone with a pushchair. Food and drink close by make it easy to stay for the whole day.
