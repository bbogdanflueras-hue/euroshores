Planning your visit to Jaz Beach

Pebble beaches usually mean clear water but awkward walking. Bring water shoes and a thick towel or mat to sit on.

Access is rated easy, so most visitors can reach the beach without a long walk or special equipment.

The usual season runs June–September. Visiting near the start or end of that window usually means fewer people, but the sea is cooler and some facilities may not be open yet or may have closed for the year.

Frequently asked questions

When is the best time to visit Jaz Beach?

Jaz Beach is best visited June to September. The sea is typically around 24 °C in season. Bring water shoes for the pebbles and some shade, and walk along the beach to find a quieter stretch.

How do I get to Jaz Beach?

Access is rated easy. Road access about 2.5 km west of central Budva; check parking on arrival in peak season.

What facilities does Jaz Beach have?

A campsite behind the beach; other services vary by season.

Where is Jaz Beach?

Jaz Beach is in Budva, Montenegro (approx. coordinates 42.2833, 18.8047).

Is Jaz Beach good for children?

Access is rated easy, so check that it suits young children before you go. Our family guide lists easier options.

What should I bring to Jaz Beach?

Facilities: A campsite behind the beach; other services vary by season. Water shoes help on the pebbles. Bring water and snacks if there is no kiosk or restaurant.
