Planning your visit to Plage Notre-Dame

Sand beaches are usually gentle underfoot and easy for swimmers and children, though they can be exposed to wind and waves, so check conditions on the day. Bring a hat and extra water, as shade can be scarce away from facilities.

Access is rated moderate: expect stairs, a short walk or a rough road, and wear proper shoes rather than flip-flops.

The usual season runs June–September. Visiting near the start or end of that window usually means fewer people, but the sea is cooler and some facilities may not be open yet or may have closed for the year.

Frequently asked questions

When is the best time to visit Plage Notre-Dame?

Plage Notre-Dame is best visited June to September. The sea is typically around 22 °C in season. Forest paths can close on high fire-risk summer days, so check the day before. Go early or late in July and August, when the ferries and beach fill up.

How do I get to Plage Notre-Dame?

Access is rated moderate. No cars for visitors on the island. Take the short passenger ferry from La Tour Fondue near Hyères, then walk or cycle about 3 km from the village of Porquerolles.

What facilities does Plage Notre-Dame have?

Very limited at the beach itself, so bring water, food and shade.

Where is Plage Notre-Dame?

Plage Notre-Dame is in Porquerolles Island, Provence, France (approx. coordinates 43.0101, 6.2276).

Is Plage Notre-Dame good for children?

Access is rated moderate, so check that it suits young children before you go. Our family guide lists easier options.

What should I bring to Plage Notre-Dame?

Facilities: Very limited at the beach itself, so bring water, food and shade. A hat and sun protection are useful. Bring water and snacks if there is no kiosk or restaurant.

Is there parking at Plage Notre-Dame?

No cars on Porquerolles; bike parking at beach. Fees and rules change, so confirm locally.

Are there sunbeds at Plage Notre-Dame?

Unconfirmed; one 2016 blog says no sunbeds/umbrellas on island beaches. Prices are approximate and change by season.
