Planning your visit to Cala Violina

Sand beaches are usually gentle underfoot and easy for swimmers and children, though they can be exposed to wind and waves, so check conditions on the day. Bring a hat and extra water, as shade can be scarce away from facilities.

Access is rated moderate: expect stairs, a short walk or a rough road, and wear proper shoes rather than flip-flops.

The usual season runs June–September. Visiting near the start or end of that window usually means fewer people, but the sea is cooler and some facilities may not be open yet or may have closed for the year.

Frequently asked questions

When is the best time to visit Cala Violina?

Cala Violina is best visited June to September. The sea is typically around 23 °C in season. Book your slot ahead in summer and arrive early or late in the day. Dogs are not allowed, and the walk in is easiest in comfortable shoes with insect repellent.

How do I get to Cala Violina?

Access is rated moderate. Car park at the end of a dirt track off the SS322, then a shaded walk of about 1.5 km through the reserve. From June to September entry is capped and booked online in advance, so check the Scarlino reserve website for current fees.

What facilities does Cala Violina have?

A bar and snack kiosk at the car park, with little at the beach itself and no bins, so carry your litter out.

Where is Cala Violina?

Cala Violina is in Scarlino, Tuscany, Italy (approx. coordinates 42.8565, 10.7744).

Is Cala Violina good for children?

Access is rated moderate, so check that it suits young children before you go. Our family guide lists easier options.

What should I bring to Cala Violina?

Facilities: A bar and snack kiosk at the car park, with little at the beach itself and no bins, so carry your litter out. A hat and sun protection are useful. Bring water and snacks if there is no kiosk or restaurant.

Is there parking at Cala Violina?

Paid; EUR 10 car + EUR 1 pp reported, booking online (limited places). Fees and rules change, so confirm locally.

Are there sunbeds at Cala Violina?

None confirmed. Prices are approximate and change by season.
