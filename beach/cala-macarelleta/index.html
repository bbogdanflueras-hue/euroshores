Frequently asked questions

When is the best time to visit Cala Macarelleta?

Cala Macarelleta is best visited May to October. The sea is typically around 24 °C in season. Arrive early; the cove fills quickly in high season.

How do I get to Cala Macarelleta?

Access is rated moderate. Car park at Cala Galdana side, then a coastal trail.

What facilities does Cala Macarelleta have?

None — bring everything you need.

Where is Cala Macarelleta?

Cala Macarelleta is in Menorca, Spain (approx. coordinates 39.9319, 3.9319).
