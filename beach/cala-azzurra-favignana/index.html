Cala Azzurra, Favignana

4.6 / 5 Clear turquoise cove with sand and rocks on the Egadi islands in Favignana, Egadi Islands, Sicily · Hidden gem

Photo: Cmassari, CC BY 3.0, via Wikimedia Commons

Cala Azzurra is a small cove on the island of Favignana in the Egadi archipelago off west Sicily, with a narrow sand and pebble beach, pale rock and very clear turquoise water. The island is flat, so it is easy to cycle around, and ferries run from Trapani.

Best months to visit Cala Azzurra, Favignana

The swimming season here runs from June to September. July, August are the warmest and busiest, with the sea around 25 °C, so arrive early for parking and a good spot. June and September are quieter and cooler: good for walking and photos, though some beach bars and lifeguard posts may not be open yet or may already have closed for the year.

Who Cala Azzurra, Favignana suits

A rocky shore suits confident swimmers and snorkellers more than families with small children; bring water shoes. Easy access makes it practical for families, older visitors and anyone with a pushchair. It is flagged as a hidden gem, so it suits travellers who prefer quieter places over the headline beaches. Food and drink close by make it easy to stay for the whole day.
