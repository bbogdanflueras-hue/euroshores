Planning your visit to Praia do Camilo

Sand beaches are usually gentle underfoot and easy for swimmers and children, though they can be exposed to wind and waves, so check conditions on the day. Bring a hat and extra water, as shade can be scarce away from facilities.

Access is rated moderate: expect stairs, a short walk or a rough road, and wear proper shoes rather than flip-flops.

The usual season runs June–September. Visiting near the start or end of that window usually means fewer people, but the sea is cooler and some facilities may not be open yet or may have closed for the year.

Frequently asked questions

When is the best time to visit Praia do Camilo?

Praia do Camilo is best visited June to September. The sea is typically around 20 °C in season. The cove is tiny and gets crowded by midday. Go in the morning or late afternoon, and take care on the steps in wet weather.

How do I get to Praia do Camilo?

Access is rated moderate. A small car park by the stairs fills quickly in summer, so arrive early or walk from Lagos.

What facilities does Praia do Camilo have?

A restaurant and toilets at the top of the stairs, with little at sea level; lifeguards in season.

Where is Praia do Camilo?

Praia do Camilo is in Lagos, Algarve, Portugal (approx. coordinates 37.0873, -8.6684).

Is Praia do Camilo good for children?

Access is rated moderate, so check that it suits young children before you go. Our family guide lists easier options.

What should I bring to Praia do Camilo?

Facilities: A restaurant and toilets at the top of the stairs, with little at sea level; lifeguards in season. A hat and sun protection are useful. Bring water and snacks if there is no kiosk or restaurant.

Is there parking at Praia do Camilo?

Small cliff-top lot, limited; some say free early morning. Chaos by ~10:00 in summer. Fees and rules change, so confirm locally.

Are there sunbeds at Praia do Camilo?

None (bring towels/umbrella). Prices are approximate and change by season.
