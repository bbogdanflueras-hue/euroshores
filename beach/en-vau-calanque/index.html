En-Vau Calanque

4.8 / 5 Hidden Cove in Cassis, Provence, France · Hidden gem

Planning your visit to En-Vau Calanque

Pebble beaches usually mean clear water but awkward walking. Bring water shoes and a thick towel or mat to sit on.

Access is rated challenging and involves a steep hike. Take plenty of water, start early and allow enough daylight for the walk back.

The usual season runs May–September. Visiting near the start or end of that window usually means fewer people, but the sea is cooler and some facilities may not be open yet or may have closed for the year. We flag this beach as a hidden gem, so it is quieter than the headline beaches nearby, but it is no secret: arrive early in peak summer.

Frequently asked questions

When is the best time to visit En-Vau Calanque?

En-Vau Calanque is best visited May to September. The sea is typically around 22 °C in season. The walk is steep and exposed, so wear proper shoes and start early. In summer, access to the Calanques massif can be restricted because of fire risk, so check the national park and local authority notices before you go.

How do I get to En-Vau Calanque?

Access is rated challenging. No road access: reach it on foot by marked trails from the Col de la Gardiole or from Cassis along the GR 51-98, or by boat or kayak.

What facilities does En-Vau Calanque have?

None on site, so bring water, food and sun protection and take your litter home.

Where is En-Vau Calanque?

En-Vau Calanque is in Cassis, Provence, France (approx. coordinates 43.2005, 5.5022).

Is En-Vau Calanque good for children?

Access is rated challenging, so check that it suits young children before you go. Our family guide lists easier options.

What should I bring to En-Vau Calanque?

Facilities: None on site, so bring water, food and sun protection and take your litter home. Water shoes help on the pebbles. Bring water and snacks if there is no kiosk or restaurant.

Is there parking at En-Vau Calanque?

Paid meter parking in Cassis; Col de la Gardiole lot no longer available; Port-Miou hard on weekends. Fees and rules change, so confirm locally.

Are there sunbeds at En-Vau Calanque?
