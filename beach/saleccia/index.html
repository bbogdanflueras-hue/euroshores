Planning your visit to Saleccia

Sand beaches are usually gentle underfoot and easy for swimmers and children, though they can be exposed to wind and waves, so check conditions on the day. Bring a hat and extra water, as shade can be scarce away from facilities.

Access is rated moderate: expect stairs, a short walk or a rough road, and wear proper shoes rather than flip-flops.

The usual season runs May–October. Visiting near the start or end of that window usually means fewer people, but the sea is cooler and some facilities may not be open yet or may have closed for the year. We flag this beach as a hidden gem, so it is quieter than the headline beaches nearby, but it is no secret: arrive early in peak summer.

Frequently asked questions

When is the best time to visit Saleccia?

Saleccia is best visited May to October. The sea is typically around 23 °C in season. Book the boat early in July and August, and bring everything you need for the day.

How do I get to Saleccia?

Access is rated moderate. No road to the sand: shuttle boats run from Saint-Florent, or take a guided 4x4 through the Agriates. Do not drive your own car down the track.

What facilities does Saleccia have?

Minimal — a snack bar in season, otherwise bring food, water and sun protection.

Where is Saleccia?

Saleccia is in Agriates, Corsica, France (approx. coordinates 42.7265, 9.2047).

Is Saleccia good for children?

Access is rated moderate, so check that it suits young children before you go. Our family guide lists easier options.

What should I bring to Saleccia?

Facilities: Minimal — a snack bar in season, otherwise bring food, water and sun protection. A hat and sun protection are useful. Bring water and snacks if there is no kiosk or restaurant.

Is there parking at Saleccia?

Paid EUR 5-10 near the beach (sources differ). Fees and rules change, so confirm locally.

Are there sunbeds at Saleccia?

None; bring your own shade. Prices are approximate and change by season.
