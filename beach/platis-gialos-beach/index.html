Planning your visit to Platis Gialos Beach

Sand beaches are usually gentle underfoot and easy for swimmers and children, though they can be exposed to wind and waves, so check conditions on the day. Bring a hat and extra water, as shade can be scarce away from facilities.

Access is rated easy, so most visitors can reach the beach without a long walk or special equipment.

The usual season runs June–September. Visiting near the start or end of that window usually means fewer people, but the sea is cooler and some facilities may not be open yet or may have closed for the year.

Best months to visit Platis Gialos Beach

The swimming season here runs from June to September. July, August are the warmest and busiest, with the sea around 24 °C, so arrive early for parking and a good spot. June and September are quieter and cooler: good for walking and photos, though some beach bars and lifeguard posts may not be open yet or may already have closed for the year.

Who Platis Gialos Beach suits

Sand makes it comfortable for sunbathing, building sandcastles and long walks along the water line. Easy access makes it practical for families, older visitors and anyone with a pushchair. Food and drink close by make it easy to stay for the whole day.

Getting there and nearby

Roadside and nearby parking; limited in summer. The beach sits at about 37.414, 25.342 on the map, so use the map link above for directions. If you are planning a day around it, the closest other beaches on our list are Super Paradise Beach (2 km), Elia Beach (3 km), Kalo Livadi Beach (6 km).
