Planning your visit to Dhërmi

Pebble beaches usually mean clear water but awkward walking. Bring water shoes and a thick towel or mat to sit on.

Access is rated easy, so most visitors can reach the beach without a long walk or special equipment.

The usual season runs June–September. Visiting near the start or end of that window usually means fewer people, but the sea is cooler and some facilities may not be open yet or may have closed for the year.

Frequently asked questions

When is the best time to visit Dhërmi?

Dhërmi is best visited June to September. The sea is typically around 25 °C in season. Bring cash and water shoes. The middle and north of the beach have free space for your own towel, and recent guides put a sunbed set at roughly 5 to 15 euros.

How do I get to Dhërmi?

Access is rated easy. Reached by road to Dhërmi village, with a bus stop in the village and a downhill walk to the beach.

What facilities does Dhërmi have?

Beach bars and sunbed rental at the south end, and restaurants along the promenade.

Where is Dhërmi?

Dhërmi is in Albanian Riviera, Albania (approx. coordinates 40.1399, 19.6421).

Is Dhërmi good for children?

Access is rated easy, so check that it suits young children before you go. Our family guide lists easier options.

What should I bring to Dhërmi?

Facilities: Beach bars and sunbed rental at the south end, and restaurants along the promenade. Water shoes help on the pebbles. Bring water and snacks if there is no kiosk or restaurant.
