Nonza Beach

4.6 / 5 Long dark pebble beach below a cliff-top village in Nonza, Cap Corse, Corsica · Hidden gem

Nonza beach is a long beach of dark grey pebbles at the foot of the Corsican village of Nonza, on the west side of Cap Corse. The village sits on a cliff above with an old tower, and the sea below is clear and deep close to the shore.

Planning your visit to Nonza Beach

Pebble beaches usually mean clear water but awkward walking. Bring water shoes and a thick towel or mat to sit on.

Access is rated easy, so most visitors can reach the beach without a long walk or special equipment.

The usual season runs June–September. Visiting near the start or end of that window usually means fewer people, but the sea is cooler and some facilities may not be open yet or may have closed for the year. We flag this beach as a hidden gem, so it is quieter than the headline beaches nearby, but it is no secret: arrive early in peak summer.

Best months to visit Nonza Beach

The swimming season here runs from June to September. July, August are the warmest and busiest, with the sea around 23 °C, so arrive early for parking and a good spot. June and September are quieter and cooler: good for walking and photos, though some beach bars and lifeguard posts may not be open yet or may already have closed for the year.

Who Nonza Beach suits

Pebbles and clear water suit swimmers and snorkellers; bring water shoes for an easier entry. Easy access makes it practical for families, older visitors and anyone with a pushchair. It is flagged as a hidden gem, so it suits travellers who prefer quieter places over the headline beaches. There are few or no facilities on the sand, so bring water, food and shade.

Getting there and nearby

Small car parks in the village; a path leads down to the beach. The beach sits at about 42.794, 9.348 on the map, so use the map link above for directions. If you are planning a day around it, the closest other beaches on our list are Saleccia (14 km), Lozari Beach (32 km), Ostriconi Beach (33 km).
