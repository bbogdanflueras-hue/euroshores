Planning your visit to Praia da Arrifana

Sand beaches are usually gentle underfoot and easy for swimmers and children, though they can be exposed to wind and waves, so check conditions on the day. Bring a hat and extra water, as shade can be scarce away from facilities.

Access is rated moderate: expect stairs, a short walk or a rough road, and wear proper shoes rather than flip-flops.

The usual season runs June–September. Visiting near the start or end of that window usually means fewer people, but the sea is cooler and some facilities may not be open yet or may have closed for the year.

Best months to visit Praia da Arrifana

The swimming season here runs from June to September. July, August are the warmest and busiest, with the sea around 18 °C, so arrive early for parking and a good spot. June and September are quieter and cooler: good for walking and photos, though some beach bars and lifeguard posts may not be open yet or may already have closed for the year.

Who Praia da Arrifana suits

Sand makes it comfortable for sunbathing, building sandcastles and long walks along the water line. Expect a short walk or uneven ground, so it suits most visitors with sensible footwear. Food and drink close by make it easy to stay for the whole day.

Getting there and nearby

A car park at the top of the cliff, then a zig-zag road down to the beach. It fills quickly on busy days. The beach sits at about 37.292, -8.865 on the map, so use the map link above for directions. If you are planning a day around it, the closest other beaches on our list are Praia do Vale Figueiras (5 km), Praia do Monte Clerigo (6 km), Praia da Amoreira, Aljezur (9 km).
