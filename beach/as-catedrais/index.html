Planning your visit to As Catedrais

Sand beaches are usually gentle underfoot and easy for swimmers and children, though they can be exposed to wind and waves, so check conditions on the day. Bring a hat and extra water, as shade can be scarce away from facilities.

Access is rated moderate: expect stairs, a short walk or a rough road, and wear proper shoes rather than flip-flops.

The usual season runs May–September. Visiting near the start or end of that window usually means fewer people, but the sea is cooler and some facilities may not be open yet or may have closed for the year.

Best months to visit As Catedrais

The swimming season here runs from May to September. June, July, August are the warmest and busiest, with the sea around 18 °C, so arrive early for parking and a good spot. May and September are quieter and cooler: good for walking and photos, though some beach bars and lifeguard posts may not be open yet or may already have closed for the year.

Who As Catedrais suits

Sand makes it comfortable for sunbathing, building sandcastles and long walks along the water line. Expect a short walk or uneven ground, so it suits most visitors with sensible footwear.

Getting there and nearby

A staircase leads down from the clifftop. A free permit is required from 1 July to 30 September and during Holy Week, booked online at ascatedrais.xunta.es. The beach sits at about 43.554, -7.157 on the map, so use the map link above for directions. If you are planning a day around it, the closest other beaches on our list are Playa del Silencio (68 km), Gulpiyuri Beach (184 km), Praia da Lanzada (186 km).
