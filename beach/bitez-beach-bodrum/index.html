Bitez Beach, Bodrum

4.2 / 5 Long calm bay with sand and shallow water on the Bodrum peninsula in Bitez, Bodrum, Mugla, Turkey

Bitez is a long, calm bay on the Bodrum peninsula with a gently shelving seabed and a seafront of cafes and small hotels. It is quieter than central Bodrum and is popular with families and windsurfers.

Planning your visit to Bitez Beach, Bodrum

Sand beaches are usually gentle underfoot and easy for swimmers and children, though they can be exposed to wind and waves, so check conditions on the day. Bring a hat and extra water, as shade can be scarce away from facilities.

Access is straightforward, with an easy approach that suits most people, including those with children or limited mobility.

The usual season runs May–October. Visiting near the start or end of that window usually means fewer people, but the sea is cooler and some facilities may not be open yet or may have closed for the year.

Best months to visit Bitez Beach, Bodrum

The swimming season here runs from May to October. June, July, August, September are the warmest and busiest, with the sea around 25 °C, so arrive early for parking and a good spot. May and October are quieter and cooler: good for walking and photos, though some beach bars and lifeguard posts may not be open yet or may already have closed for the year.

Who Bitez Beach, Bodrum suits

The soft sand is good for lying out, playing with children and strolling along the shore. With easy access it works well for families, older visitors and people with pushchairs. Food and drink close by make it easy to stay for the whole day.

Getting there and nearby

Roadside and village parking, and dolmus minibuses from Bodrum. Use the map link above for directions. If you are planning a day around it, the closest other beaches on our list are Gumusluk Beach, Bodrum (12 km), Altinkum Beach, Didim (37 km), Datca Beach, Mugla (44 km).
