Planning your visit to Fomm ir-Rih Bay

Pebble beaches usually mean clear water but awkward walking. Bring water shoes and a thick towel or mat to sit on.

Access is rated challenging and involves a steep hike. Take plenty of water, start early and allow enough daylight for the walk back.

The usual season runs May–October. Visiting near the start or end of that window usually means fewer people, but the sea is cooler and some facilities may not be open yet or may have closed for the year. We flag this beach as a hidden gem, so it is quieter than the headline beaches nearby, but it is no secret: arrive early in peak summer.

Frequently asked questions

When is the best time to visit Fomm ir-Rih Bay?

Fomm ir-Rih Bay is best visited May to October. The sea is typically around 23 °C in season. The descent is steep and rough; wear sturdy shoes and avoid windy days.

How do I get to Fomm ir-Rih Bay?

Access is rated challenging. No official car park; you park by the road and walk down.

What facilities does Fomm ir-Rih Bay have?

None; bring water and food.

Where is Fomm ir-Rih Bay?

Fomm ir-Rih Bay is in Mgarr, Malta (approx. coordinates 35.9088, 14.3406).

What should I bring to Fomm ir-Rih Bay?

Facilities: None; bring water and food. Water shoes help on the pebbles. Bring water and snacks if there is no kiosk or restaurant.

Is there parking at Fomm ir-Rih Bay?
