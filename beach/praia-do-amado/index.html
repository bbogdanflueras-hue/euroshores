Planning your visit to Praia do Amado

Sand beaches are usually gentle underfoot and easy for swimmers and children, though they can be exposed to wind and waves, so check conditions on the day. Bring a hat and extra water, as shade can be scarce away from facilities.

Access is rated easy, so most visitors can reach the beach without a long walk or special equipment.

The usual season runs June–September. Visiting near the start or end of that window usually means fewer people, but the sea is cooler and some facilities may not be open yet or may have closed for the year.

Frequently asked questions

When is the best time to visit Praia do Amado?

Praia do Amado is best visited June to September. The sea is typically around 19 °C in season. Swell can be strong, so check conditions and stay within the safe areas.

How do I get to Praia do Amado?

Access is rated easy. Car park above the beach.

What facilities does Praia do Amado have?

Surf school and beach cafe in season.

Where is Praia do Amado?

Praia do Amado is in Carrapateira, Aljezur, Algarve, Portugal (approx. coordinates 37.1675, -8.9065).

Is Praia do Amado good for children?

Access is rated easy and the beach is sandy, which usually suits families. Check water and weather conditions on the day and keep children in sight near the water.

What should I bring to Praia do Amado?

Facilities: Surf school and beach cafe in season. A hat and sun protection are useful. Bring water and snacks if there is no kiosk or restaurant.
