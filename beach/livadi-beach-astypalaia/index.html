Livadi Beach, Astypalaia

Livadi is the main beach of Astypalaia, a green valley bay with pebbles and sand below the whitewashed Chora and its Venetian castle. Tamarisk trees give shade, and a few tavernas sit just behind the beach.

Planning your visit to Livadi Beach, Astypalaia

Pebble beaches usually mean clear water but awkward walking. Bring water shoes and a thick towel or mat to sit on.

Access is rated easy, so most visitors can reach the beach without a long walk or special equipment.

The usual season runs June–September. Visiting near the start or end of that window usually means fewer people, but the sea is cooler and some facilities may not be open yet or may have closed for the year. Best months to visit Livadi Beach, Astypalaia

The swimming season here runs from June to September. July, August are the warmest and busiest, with the sea around 26 °C, so arrive early for parking and a good spot. June and September are quieter and cooler: good for walking and photos, though some beach bars and lifeguard posts may not be open yet or may already have closed for the year.

Who Livadi Beach, Astypalaia suits

Pebbles and clear water suit swimmers and snorkellers; bring water shoes for an easier entry. Easy access makes it practical for families, older visitors and anyone with a pushchair. It is flagged as a hidden gem, so it suits travellers who prefer quieter places over the headline beaches. Food and drink close by make it easy to stay for the whole day.
