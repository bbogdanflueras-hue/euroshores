Playazo de Rodalquilar

4.5 / 5 Long beach of sand and gravel with a ruined castle in Rodalquilar, Cabo de Gata-Nijar, Almeria · Hidden gem

El Playazo is a long beach of sand and fine gravel near Rodalquilar, in the Cabo de Gata-Nijar natural park in Almeria, with the ruined Castillo de San Ramon at the north end. Hills of red and brown volcanic rock rise behind the shore.

Planning your visit to Playazo de Rodalquilar

Sand beaches are usually gentle underfoot and easy for swimmers and children, though they can be exposed to wind and waves, so check conditions on the day. Bring a hat and extra water, as shade can be scarce away from facilities.

Access is rated easy, so most visitors can reach the beach without a long walk or special equipment.

The usual season runs June–September. Visiting near the start or end of that window usually means fewer people, but the sea is cooler and some facilities may not be open yet or may have closed for the year. We flag this beach as a hidden gem, so it is quieter than the headline beaches nearby, but it is no secret: arrive early in peak summer.

Best months to visit Playazo de Rodalquilar

The swimming season here runs from June to September. July, August are the warmest and busiest, with the sea around 22 °C, so arrive early for parking and a good spot. June and September are quieter and cooler: good for walking and photos, though some beach bars and lifeguard posts may not be open yet or may already have closed for the year.

Who Playazo de Rodalquilar suits

Sand makes it comfortable for sunbathing, building sandcastles and long walks along the water line. Easy access makes it practical for families, older visitors and anyone with a pushchair. It is flagged as a hidden gem, so it suits travellers who prefer quieter places over the headline beaches. There are few or no facilities on the sand, so bring water, food and shade.

Getting there and nearby

Free car parks at the entrance and near the campsite (50-100 spaces); fills early in high season. The beach sits at about 36.859, -2.015 on the map, so use the map link above for directions. If you are planning a day around it, the closest other beaches on our list are Cala del Plomo (9 km), Playa de los Muertos, Carboneras (14 km), Los Genoveses Beach (15 km).
