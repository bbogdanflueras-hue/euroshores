Myrtos Beach, Ierapetra

4.2 / 5 Pebble beach below a whitewashed village on the south coast of Crete in Myrtos, Ierapetra, Lasithi, Crete

Photo: Unukorno, CC BY 4.0, via Wikimedia Commons

Myrtos is a small village on the south coast of Crete with a long beach of grey pebbles and coarse sand in front of whitewashed houses. The water is clear and the setting is quiet, with mountains behind the village. It has a relaxed feel, with tavernas close to the sea.

Planning your visit to Myrtos Beach, Ierapetra

Pebble beaches usually mean clear water but awkward walking. Bring water shoes and a thick towel or mat to sit on.

Access is rated easy, so most visitors can reach the beach without a long walk or special equipment.

The usual season runs June–September. Visiting near the start or end of that window usually means fewer people, but the sea is cooler and some facilities may not be open yet or may have closed for the year.

Best months to visit Myrtos Beach, Ierapetra

The swimming season here runs from June to September. July, August are the warmest and busiest, with the sea around 26 °C, so arrive early for parking and a good spot. June and September are quieter and cooler: good for walking and photos, though some beach bars and lifeguard posts may not be open yet or may already have closed for the year.

Who Myrtos Beach, Ierapetra suits

Pebbles and clear water suit swimmers and snorkellers; bring water shoes for an easier entry. Easy access makes it practical for families, older visitors and anyone with a pushchair. Food and drink close by make it easy to stay for the whole day.
