Kabak Bay, Fethiye

4.5 / 5 Secluded bay below a forested valley on the Lycian Way in Kabak, Fethiye, Mugla, Turkey · Hidden gem

Kabak is a quiet bay at the bottom of a forested valley near Faralya, on the Lycian Way walking route. The beach is reached by a steep road and paths, and small camps and cafes serve the few visitors. It feels far from the resort coast.

Planning your visit to Kabak Bay, Fethiye

Pebble beaches usually mean clear water but awkward walking. Bring water shoes and a thick towel or mat to sit on.

Access is rated challenging and involves a steep hike. Take plenty of water, start early and allow enough daylight for the walk back.

The usual season runs May–October. Visiting near the start or end of that window usually means fewer people, but the sea is cooler and some facilities may not be open yet or may have closed for the year. We flag this beach as a hidden gem, so it is quieter than the headline beaches nearby, but it is no secret: arrive early in peak summer.

Best months to visit Kabak Bay, Fethiye

Kabak Bay, Fethiye is at its best from May to October. Expect the sea near 26 °C and the biggest crowds in June, July, August, September, so get there early for parking. A visit in May or October is calmer, but check that facilities are open.

Who Kabak Bay, Fethiye suits

Pebbles and clear water suit swimmers and snorkellers; bring water shoes for an easier entry. Access is demanding, so it suits fit, well-prepared visitors; wear sturdy shoes and carry water. It is flagged as a hidden gem, so it suits travellers who prefer quieter places over the headline beaches. Food and drink close by make it easy to stay for the whole day.

Getting there and nearby

Parking at the top of the valley, with a walk or shuttle down to the beach. Use the map link above for directions. If you are planning a day around it, the closest other beaches on our list are Butterfly Valley Beach, Faralya (4 km), Oludeniz Beach, Fethiye (10 km), Calis Beach, Fethiye (23 km).
