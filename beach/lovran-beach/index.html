Lovran Beach

4.2 / 5 Pebble promenade beach in an old Habsburg resort in Lovran, Kvarner

Photo: Silverije, CC BY-SA 4.0, via Wikimedia Commons

Save on Pinterest

Lovran is an old Habsburg seaside resort on the Kvarner coast, with a small pebble beach along a long stone promenade. Villas and parks line the shore, laurel woods rise behind, and the sea is clear and deep close to the edge.

Planning your visit to Lovran Beach

Pebble beaches usually mean clear water but awkward walking. Bring water shoes and a thick towel or mat to sit on.

Access is rated easy, so most visitors can reach the beach without a long walk or special equipment.

The usual season runs June–September. Visiting near the start or end of that window usually means fewer people, but the sea is cooler and some facilities may not be open yet or may have closed for the year.

Best months to visit Lovran Beach

The swimming season here runs from June to September. July, August are the warmest and busiest, with the sea around 23 °C, so arrive early for parking and a good spot. June and September are quieter and cooler: good for walking and photos, though some beach bars and lifeguard posts may not be open yet or may already have closed for the year.

Who Lovran Beach suits

Pebbles and clear water suit swimmers and snorkellers; bring water shoes for an easier entry. Easy access makes it practical for families, older visitors and anyone with a pushchair. Food and drink close by make it easy to stay for the whole day.

Getting there and nearby
