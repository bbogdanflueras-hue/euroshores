Rondinara Beach

4.7 / 5 Sandy Beach in Bonifacio, Corsica, France

A horseshoe-shaped bay of pale sand and shallow, turquoise water on the south-east coast of Corsica, backed by low scrub and quiet hills.

Planning your visit to Rondinara Beach

Sand beaches are usually gentle underfoot and easy for swimmers and children, though they can be exposed to wind and waves, so check conditions on the day. Bring a hat and extra water, as shade can be scarce away from facilities.

Access is rated easy, so most visitors can reach the beach without a long walk or special equipment.

The usual season runs June–September. Visiting near the start or end of that window usually means fewer people, but the sea is cooler and some facilities may not be open yet or may have closed for the year.

Frequently asked questions

When is the best time to visit Rondinara Beach?

Rondinara Beach is best visited June to September. The sea is typically around 24 °C in season. The almost closed shape of the bay keeps the water calm, which suits children, but it fills up in July and August. Arrive early, or visit in June or September.

How do I get to Rondinara Beach?

Access is rated easy. Car park behind the beach reached by a signed road from the Bonifacio to Porto-Vecchio route (parking may be charged in summer).

What facilities does Rondinara Beach have?

Seasonal snack bar in summer; otherwise few facilities, so bring water and shade.

Where is Rondinara Beach?

Rondinara Beach is in Bonifacio, Corsica, France (approx. coordinates 41.4689, 9.2665).

Is Rondinara Beach good for children?

Access is rated easy and the beach is sandy, which usually suits families. Check water and weather conditions on the day and keep children in sight near the water.

What should I bring to Rondinara Beach?

Facilities: Seasonal snack bar in summer; otherwise few facilities, so bring water and shade. A hat and sun protection are useful. Bring water and snacks if there is no kiosk or restaurant.
