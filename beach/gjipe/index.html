Planning your visit to Gjipe

Pebble beaches usually mean clear water but awkward walking. Bring water shoes and a thick towel or mat to sit on.

Access is rated challenging and involves a steep hike. Take plenty of water, start early and allow enough daylight for the walk back.

The usual season runs June–September. Visiting near the start or end of that window usually means fewer people, but the sea is cooler and some facilities may not be open yet or may have closed for the year. We flag this beach as a hidden gem, so it is quieter than the headline beaches nearby, but it is no secret: arrive early in peak summer.

Frequently asked questions

When is the best time to visit Gjipe?

Gjipe is best visited June to September. The sea is typically around 25 °C in season. Arrive before 10 AM and wear sturdy shoes. Consider going in by boat and hiking out through the canyon.

How do I get to Gjipe?

Access is rated challenging. Parking at the top (small daily fee), then a steep hike of 30–45 minutes; boats from Himara and summer jeep shuttles also run.

What facilities does Gjipe have?

Basic seasonal beach bars, umbrella rental and camping.

Where is Gjipe?

Gjipe is in Himara, Albania (approx. coordinates 40.1436, 19.6703).

Is Gjipe good for children?

Access is rated challenging, so check that it suits young children before you go. Our family guide lists easier options.

What should I bring to Gjipe?

Facilities: Basic seasonal beach bars, umbrella rental and camping. Water shoes help on the pebbles. Bring water and snacks if there is no kiosk or restaurant.
