Planning your visit to Rajska Plaza

Sand beaches are usually gentle underfoot and easy for swimmers and children, though they can be exposed to wind and waves, so check conditions on the day. Bring a hat and extra water, as shade can be scarce away from facilities.

Access is rated easy, so most visitors can reach the beach without a long walk or special equipment.

The usual season runs June–September. Visiting near the start or end of that window usually means fewer people, but the sea is cooler and some facilities may not be open yet or may have closed for the year.

Frequently asked questions

When is the best time to visit Rajska Plaza?

Rajska Plaza is best visited June to September. The sea is typically around 25 °C in season. The shallow water suits young children. For quieter sand, walk about 30 minutes to Sahara beach, a naturist beach with no facilities, and take your own food and water.

How do I get to Rajska Plaza?

Access is rated easy. Reached from Lopar village; check parking in peak season.

What facilities does Rajska Plaza have?

Sunbed and umbrella hire, showers, changing cabins, bars and restaurants, lifeguards and water sports.

Where is Rajska Plaza?

Rajska Plaza is in Lopar, Rab, Croatia (approx. coordinates 44.8211, 14.7374).

Is Rajska Plaza good for children?

Access is rated easy and the beach is sandy, which usually suits families. Check water and weather conditions on the day and keep children in sight near the water.

What should I bring to Rajska Plaza?

Facilities: Sunbed and umbrella hire, showers, changing cabins, bars and restaurants, lifeguards and water sports. A hat and sun protection are useful. Bring water and snacks if there is no kiosk or restaurant.

Is there parking at Rajska Plaza?

Car park nearby, about 200 spaces (fee not found). Campers and motorhomes: Camping San Marino beside the beach: pitches from about EUR 40/night (EUR 40-90 range). Fees and rules change, so confirm locally.
