Beldibi is a pebble beach between Antalya and Kemer, with the Taurus Mountains rising steeply behind the coast. The sea is clear, and the shore has fewer crowds than the central resort beaches.

Best months to visit Beldibi Beach, Kemer

The swimming season here runs from May to October. June, July, August, September are the warmest and busiest, with the sea around 25 °C, so arrive early for parking and a good spot. May and October are quieter and cooler: good for walking and photos, though some beach bars and lifeguard posts may not be open yet or may already have closed for the year.

Who Beldibi Beach, Kemer suits

A pebble shore means clear water for swimming and snorkelling; shoes for the water help on the stones. The simple approach makes it a practical choice for families and visitors who prefer not to walk far. Food and drink close by make it easy to stay for the whole day.

Getting there and nearby

Roadside parking and dolmus services along the coast road. Use the map link above for directions. If you are planning a day around it, the closest other beaches on our list are Kemer Beach, Antalya (12 km), Konyaalti Beach, Antalya (16 km), Phaselis Bay, Kemer (23 km).
