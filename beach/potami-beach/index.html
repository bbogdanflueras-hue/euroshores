Potami Beach

4.3 / 5 Pebble Beach in Himara, Albania

Photo: Aggbel, CC BY-SA 3.0, via Wikimedia Commons

A long pebble curve about 2 km south of Himara town, with clear water fed by freshwater springs and a quieter feel than the town beach.

Best seasonJune–September

AccessEasy

Planning your visit to Potami Beach

Pebble beaches usually mean clear water but awkward walking. Bring water shoes and a thick towel or mat to sit on.

Access is rated easy, so most visitors can reach the beach without a long walk or special equipment.

The usual season runs June–September. Visiting near the start or end of that window usually means fewer people, but the sea is cooler and some facilities may not be open yet or may have closed for the year.

Frequently asked questions

When is the best time to visit Potami Beach?

Potami Beach is best visited June to September. The sea is typically around 25 °C in season. The beach is free, with a sunbed set costing roughly 10 to 20 euros in recent guides. It is a good place for a sunset swim.

How do I get to Potami Beach?

Access is rated easy. Parking is available, with some lots charging a small fee; about a 20–25 minute walk along the coastal road from Himara.

What facilities does Potami Beach have?

Beach bars and restaurants, sunbed rental, showers and a lifeguard in season.

Where is Potami Beach?

Potami Beach is in Himara, Albania (approx. coordinates 40.0929, 19.7549).

Is Potami Beach good for children?

Access is rated easy, so check that it suits young children before you go. Our family guide lists easier options.

What should I bring to Potami Beach?

Facilities: Beach bars and restaurants, sunbed rental, showers and a lifeguard in season. Water shoes help on the pebbles. Bring water and snacks if there is no kiosk or restaurant.
