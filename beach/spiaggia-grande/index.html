Planning your visit to Spiaggia Grande

Sand beaches are usually gentle underfoot and easy for swimmers and children, though they can be exposed to wind and waves, so check conditions on the day. Bring a hat and extra water, as shade can be scarce away from facilities.

Access is rated easy, so most visitors can reach the beach without a long walk or special equipment.

The usual season runs May–October. Visiting near the start or end of that window usually means fewer people, but the sea is cooler and some facilities may not be open yet or may have closed for the year.

Frequently asked questions

When is the best time to visit Spiaggia Grande?

Spiaggia Grande is best visited May to October. The sea is typically around 23 °C in season. The stairs back up to the village are steep; pack light.

How do I get to Spiaggia Grande?

Access is rated easy. Limited — use public transport or village car parks.

What facilities does Spiaggia Grande have?

Sunbed rental, restaurants, showers.

Where is Spiaggia Grande?

Spiaggia Grande is in Amalfi Coast, Italy (approx. coordinates 40.6279, 14.485).

Is Spiaggia Grande good for children?

Access is rated easy and the beach is sandy, which usually suits families. Check water and weather conditions on the day and keep children in sight near the water.

What should I bring to Spiaggia Grande?

Facilities: Sunbed rental, restaurants, showers. A hat and sun protection are useful. Bring water and snacks if there is no kiosk or restaurant.

Is there parking at Spiaggia Grande?

Hard to find; paid in town, from about EUR 10/hour with a 3-hour minimum (one review). Driving not advised. Fees and rules change, so confirm locally.

Are there sunbeds at Spiaggia Grande?

Paid at private clubs: roughly EUR 20-35 pp budget, about EUR 30-50 per bed in reviews, premium clubs EUR 200+. Small free public section at the west end. Prices are approximate and change by season.
