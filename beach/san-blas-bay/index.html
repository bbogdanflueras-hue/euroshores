Planning your visit to San Blas Bay

Sand beaches are usually gentle underfoot and easy for swimmers and children, though they can be exposed to wind and waves, so check conditions on the day. Bring a hat and extra water, as shade can be scarce away from facilities.

Access is rated challenging and involves a steep hike. Take plenty of water, start early and allow enough daylight for the walk back.

The usual season runs June–September. Visiting near the start or end of that window usually means fewer people, but the sea is cooler and some facilities may not be open yet or may have closed for the year. We flag this beach as a hidden gem, so it is quieter than the headline beaches nearby, but it is no secret: arrive early in peak summer.

Best months to visit San Blas Bay

The swimming season here runs from June to September. July, August are the warmest and busiest, with the sea around 25 °C, so arrive early for parking and a good spot. June and September are quieter and cooler: good for walking and photos, though some beach bars and lifeguard posts may not be open yet or may already have closed for the year.

Who San Blas Bay suits

Sand makes it comfortable for sunbathing, building sandcastles and long walks along the water line. Access is demanding, so it suits fit, well-prepared visitors; wear sturdy shoes and carry water. It is flagged as a hidden gem, so it suits travellers who prefer quieter places over the headline beaches. There are few or no facilities on the sand, so bring water, food and shade.

Getting there and nearby

A small parking area before the steep descent, then a walk of about 15 minutes down; do not drive down the track. The beach sits at about 36.057, 14.301 on the map, so use the map link above for directions. If you are planning a day around it, the closest other beaches on our list are Ramla Bay (2 km), Marsalforn Bay, Gozo (4 km), Qbajjar Bay, Gozo (5 km).
