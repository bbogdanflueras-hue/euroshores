Akyaka Beach, Mugla

Akyaka is a village at the head of Gokova Bay, known for traditional wooden houses and a cold river that meets the sea. The beach is calm and sheltered, and the area suits a quiet, low-key stay.

The season at Akyaka Beach, Mugla stretches from May to October, with June, July, August, September the peak (sea about 25 °C). Early and late visits mean quieter sand and cooler water, and seasonal services may not yet be running.

Who Akyaka Beach, Mugla suits

A pebble shore means clear water for swimming and snorkelling; shoes for the water help on the stones. The simple approach makes it a practical choice for families and visitors who prefer not to walk far. It is flagged as a hidden gem, so it suits travellers who prefer quieter places over the headline beaches. Food and drink close by make it easy to stay for the whole day.
