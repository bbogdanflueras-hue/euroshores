Planning your visit to Porto Katsiki

Pebble beaches usually mean clear water but awkward walking. Bring water shoes and a thick towel or mat to sit on.

Access is rated moderate: expect stairs, a short walk or a rough road, and wear proper shoes rather than flip-flops.

The usual season runs June–September. Visiting near the start or end of that window usually means fewer people, but the sea is cooler and some facilities may not be open yet or may have closed for the year.

Frequently asked questions

When is the best time to visit Porto Katsiki?

Porto Katsiki is best visited June to September. The sea is typically around 24 °C in season. Arrive before mid-morning for parking and shade. The cliffs reportedly shade the beach until around midday, and the climb back up is harder than the walk down.

How do I get to Porto Katsiki?

Access is rated moderate. Paid clifftop car park (fills early), then a stairway down to the beach.

What facilities does Porto Katsiki have?

Canteen and restaurant above the beach; little shade, so bring an umbrella.

Where is Porto Katsiki?

Porto Katsiki is in Lefkada, Greece (approx. coordinates 38.6025, 20.5494).

Is Porto Katsiki good for children?

Access is rated moderate, so check that it suits young children before you go. Our family guide lists easier options.

What should I bring to Porto Katsiki?

Facilities: Canteen and restaurant above the beach; little shade, so bring an umbrella. Water shoes help on the pebbles. Bring water and snacks if there is no kiosk or restaurant.
