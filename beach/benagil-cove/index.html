Planning your visit to Benagil Cove

Rocky coves often have clear, deeper water that suits snorkelling, but the entry can be slippery. Wear water shoes, enter slowly and avoid swimming when there is a swell.

Access is rated moderate: expect stairs, a short walk or a rough road, and wear proper shoes rather than flip-flops.

The usual season runs May–October. Visiting near the start or end of that window usually means fewer people, but the sea is cooler and some facilities may not be open yet or may have closed for the year. We flag this beach as a hidden gem, so it is quieter than the headline beaches nearby, but it is no secret: arrive early in peak summer.

Best months to visit Benagil Cove

The swimming season here runs from May to October. June, July, August, September are the warmest and busiest, with the sea around 22 °C, so arrive early for parking and a good spot. May and October are quieter and cooler: good for walking and photos, though some beach bars and lifeguard posts may not be open yet or may already have closed for the year.

Who Benagil Cove suits

A rocky shore suits confident swimmers and snorkellers more than families with small children; bring water shoes. Expect a short walk or uneven ground, so it suits most visitors with sensible footwear. It is flagged as a hidden gem, so it suits travellers who prefer quieter places over the headline beaches.

Getting there and nearby

Village car park, short walk. The beach sits at about 37.087, -8.422 on the map, so use the map link above for directions. If you are planning a day around it, the closest other beaches on our list are Praia da Marinha (1 km), Praia do Carvalho (1 km), Praia da Albandeira (2 km).
