Benagil Cove

4.7 / 5 Rocky Cove in Algarve, Portugal · Hidden gem

Frequently asked questions

When is the best time to visit Benagil Cove?

Benagil Cove is best visited May to October. The sea is typically around 22 °C in season. Calm mornings are best for kayaking into the cave.

How do I get to Benagil Cove?

Access is rated moderate. Village car park, short walk.

What facilities does Benagil Cove have?

Kayak and boat tours from the beach.

Where is Benagil Cove?

Benagil Cove is in Algarve, Portugal (approx. coordinates 37.087, -8.4217).
