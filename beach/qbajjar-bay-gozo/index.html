Qbajjar Bay is a rocky bay on the north coast of Gozo near Marsalforn, next to the old salt pans. Swimming is from the flat rocks into clear water and there are a few restaurants nearby. It is a quiet spot, but there is little sand.

Planning your visit to Qbajjar Bay, Gozo

Rocky coves often have clear, deeper water that suits snorkelling, but the entry can be slippery. Wear water shoes, enter slowly and avoid swimming when there is a swell.

Access is rated easy, so most visitors can reach the beach without a long walk or special equipment.

The usual season runs June–September. Visiting near the start or end of that window usually means fewer people, but the sea is cooler and some facilities may not be open yet or may have closed for the year.

Best months to visit Qbajjar Bay, Gozo

The swimming season here runs from June to September. July, August are the warmest and busiest, with the sea around 25 °C, so arrive early for parking and a good spot. June and September are quieter and cooler: good for walking and photos, though some beach bars and lifeguard posts may not be open yet or may already have closed for the year.

Who Qbajjar Bay, Gozo suits

A rocky shore suits confident swimmers and snorkellers more than families with small children; bring water shoes. Easy access makes it practical for families, older visitors and anyone with a pushchair. Food and drink close by make it easy to stay for the whole day.
