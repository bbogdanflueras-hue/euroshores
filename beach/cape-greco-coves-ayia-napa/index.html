Cape Greco Coves, Ayia Napa

4.4 / 5 Rocky coves and clear water in Cape Greco National Forest Park in Cape Greco, Ayia Napa, Cyprus · Hidden gem

Cape Greco is a rocky headland between Ayia Napa and Protaras with small coves, sea caves and very clear water. It is a popular spot for swimming, snorkelling and cliff views, and it is far less built up than the resort beaches.

Best months to visit Cape Greco Coves, Ayia Napa

Most people swim at Cape Greco Coves, Ayia Napa between May and October. The warmest and busiest months are June, July, August, September, when the sea sits around 25 °C. Shoulder months bring cooler water and fewer crowds, although some bars and lifeguard posts may be closed.

Who Cape Greco Coves, Ayia Napa suits

A pebble shore means clear water for swimming and snorkelling; shoes for the water help on the stones. The simple approach makes it a practical choice for families and visitors who prefer not to walk far. It is flagged as a hidden gem, so it suits travellers who prefer quieter places over the headline beaches. There are few or no facilities on the sand, so bring water, food and shade.
