Planning your visit to Praia de Rodas

Sand beaches are usually gentle underfoot and easy for swimmers and children, though they can be exposed to wind and waves, so check conditions on the day. Bring a hat and extra water, as shade can be scarce away from facilities.

Access is rated moderate: expect stairs, a short walk or a rough road, and wear proper shoes rather than flip-flops.

The usual season runs June–September. Visiting near the start or end of that window usually means fewer people, but the sea is cooler and some facilities may not be open yet or may have closed for the year. We flag this beach as a hidden gem, so it is quieter than the headline beaches nearby, but it is no secret: arrive early in peak summer.

Frequently asked questions

When is the best time to visit Praia de Rodas?

Praia de Rodas is best visited June to September. The sea is typically around 17 °C in season. Summer ferries can sell out days ahead. The Atlantic water is cold, so expect a short swim, and check the current permit rules before booking.

How do I get to Praia de Rodas?

Access is rated moderate. Reached only by ferry from Vigo, Cangas or Baiona, which take under an hour and run from mid May to mid September in most guides. Daily visitor numbers are capped and a Xunta de Galicia permit is needed, so book the permit and ferry ahead.

What facilities does Praia de Rodas have?

Minimal on the island, so bring your own water, food and shade.

Where is Praia de Rodas?

Praia de Rodas is in Cíes Islands, Galicia, Spain (approx. coordinates 42.2258, -8.9015).

Is Praia de Rodas good for children?

Access is rated moderate, so check that it suits young children before you go. Our family guide lists easier options.

What should I bring to Praia de Rodas?

Facilities: Minimal on the island, so bring your own water, food and shade. A hat and sun protection are useful. Bring water and snacks if there is no kiosk or restaurant.
