Elafonissi Beach

4.8 / 5 Pink Sand in Crete, Greece

A rare pink-sand lagoon at the southwestern tip of Crete, where shallow crystal-clear waters meet rose-tinted sands.

AccessEasy

Frequently asked questions

When is the best time to visit Elafonissi Beach?

Elafonissi Beach is best visited May to October. The sea is typically around 25 °C in season. Walk across the shallow lagoon to the islet for the pinkest sand.

How do I get to Elafonissi Beach?

Access is rated easy. Large car park near the beach.

What facilities does Elafonissi Beach have?

Sunbeds, tavernas, toilets.

Where is Elafonissi Beach?

Elafonissi Beach is in Crete, Greece (approx. coordinates 35.2711, 23.5388).
