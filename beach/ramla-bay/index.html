Planning your visit to Ramla Bay

Sand beaches are usually gentle underfoot and easy for swimmers and children, though they can be exposed to wind and waves, so check conditions on the day. Bring a hat and extra water, as shade can be scarce away from facilities.

Access is rated easy, so most visitors can reach the beach without a long walk or special equipment.

The usual season runs June–September. Visiting near the start or end of that window usually means fewer people, but the sea is cooler and some facilities may not be open yet or may have closed for the year.

Frequently asked questions

When is the best time to visit Ramla Bay?

Ramla Bay is best visited June to September. The sea is typically around 25 °C in season. Calypso's Cave is currently closed to visitors, but you can see it from the beach. Go early in July and August, when the car parking along the road fills up.

How do I get to Ramla Bay?

Access is rated easy. Parking along the road to the beach; bus 302 stops about a 5-minute walk away.

What facilities does Ramla Bay have?

A bar and snack bars, equipment hire, public toilets, water sports and summer lifeguards.

Where is Ramla Bay?

Ramla Bay is in Xaghra, Gozo, Malta (approx. coordinates 36.0623, 14.2838).

Is Ramla Bay good for children?

Access is rated easy and the beach is sandy, which usually suits families. Check water and weather conditions on the day and keep children in sight near the water.

What should I bring to Ramla Bay?

Facilities: A bar and snack bars, equipment hire, public toilets, water sports and summer lifeguards. A hat and sun protection are useful. Bring water and snacks if there is no kiosk or restaurant.
