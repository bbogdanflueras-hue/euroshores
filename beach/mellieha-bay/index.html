Planning your visit to Mellieha Bay

Sand beaches are usually gentle underfoot and easy for swimmers and children, though they can be exposed to wind and waves, so check conditions on the day. Bring a hat and extra water, as shade can be scarce away from facilities.

Access is rated easy, so most visitors can reach the beach without a long walk or special equipment.

The usual season runs May–October. Visiting near the start or end of that window usually means fewer people, but the sea is cooler and some facilities may not be open yet or may have closed for the year.

Frequently asked questions

When is the best time to visit Mellieha Bay?

Mellieha Bay is best visited May to October. The sea is typically around 24 °C in season. It gets crowded on summer weekends, so go on a weekday morning for more space.

How do I get to Mellieha Bay?

Access is rated easy. Roadside and nearby car parks; busy at weekends.

What facilities does Mellieha Bay have?

Sunbeds, umbrellas and beach cafes in season.

Where is Mellieha Bay?

Mellieha Bay is in Mellieha, Malta (approx. coordinates 35.969, 14.3506).

Is Mellieha Bay good for children?

Access is rated easy and the beach is sandy, which usually suits families. Check water and weather conditions on the day and keep children in sight near the water.

What should I bring to Mellieha Bay?

Facilities: Sunbeds, umbrellas and beach cafes in season. A hat and sun protection are useful. Bring water and snacks if there is no kiosk or restaurant.
