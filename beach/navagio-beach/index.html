Frequently asked questions

When is the best time to visit Navagio Beach?

Navagio Beach is best visited June to September. The sea is typically around 26 °C in season. Go early or late to avoid the peak-hour boat crowds.

How do I get to Navagio Beach?

Access is rated moderate. Boat access only (from Porto Vromi or Zakynthos town).

What facilities does Navagio Beach have?

None on the beach — bring water and shade.

Where is Navagio Beach?

Navagio Beach is in Zakynthos, Greece (approx. coordinates 37.8594, 20.6244).
