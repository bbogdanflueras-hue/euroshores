Planning your visit to Dubovica

Pebble beaches usually mean clear water but awkward walking. Bring water shoes and a thick towel or mat to sit on.

Access is rated moderate: expect stairs, a short walk or a rough road, and wear proper shoes rather than flip-flops.

The usual season runs June–September. Visiting near the start or end of that window usually means fewer people, but the sea is cooler and some facilities may not be open yet or may have closed for the year. We flag this beach as a hidden gem, so it is quieter than the headline beaches nearby, but it is no secret: arrive early in peak summer.

Frequently asked questions

When is the best time to visit Dubovica?

Dubovica is best visited June to September. The sea is typically around 25 °C in season. Arrive early or come by taxi, wear sturdy shoes, bring cash for the beach bars and allow extra time for the climb back up. The path is not suitable for people with limited mobility.

How do I get to Dubovica?

Access is rated moderate. Roadside parking only, just off the Stari Grad–Hvar road, then a steep walk of 10–15 minutes down; the strip can be full by about 09:00 in July and August.

What facilities does Dubovica have?

A few small seasonal beach bars; limited shade, so bring cash and an umbrella.

Where is Dubovica?

Dubovica is in Hvar, Croatia (approx. coordinates 43.1459, 16.5349).

Is Dubovica good for children?

Access is rated moderate, so check that it suits young children before you go. Our family guide lists easier options.

What should I bring to Dubovica?

Facilities: A few small seasonal beach bars; limited shade, so bring cash and an umbrella. Water shoes help on the pebbles. Bring water and snacks if there is no kiosk or restaurant.
