Sveti Stefan

Frequently asked questions

When is the best time to visit Sveti Stefan?

Sveti Stefan is best visited June to September. The sea is typically around 25 °C in season. Views from the road above are free and superb at sunset.

How do I get to Sveti Stefan?

Access is rated easy. Roadside and paid parking above the beach.

What facilities does Sveti Stefan have?

Sunbeds, cafes nearby.

Where is Sveti Stefan?

Sveti Stefan is in Budva Riviera, Montenegro (approx. coordinates 42.2557, 18.8918).
