Marsaskala Bay, Malta

4.0 / 5 Quiet fishing bay with rocky shore and a promenade in south-east Malta in Marsaskala, Southern Harbour, Malta

Marsaskala is a fishing village on a long narrow bay in the south-east of Malta, with colourful boats in the water, a promenade and a small town beach. The shore is mainly rocky, and swimmers use steps and a small sandy area. Cafes and restaurants line the waterfront.

Planning your visit to Marsaskala Bay, Malta

Rocky coves often have clear, deeper water that suits snorkelling, but the entry can be slippery. Wear water shoes, enter slowly and avoid swimming when there is a swell.

Access is rated easy, so most visitors can reach the beach without a long walk or special equipment.

The usual season runs June–September. Visiting near the start or end of that window usually means fewer people, but the sea is cooler and some facilities may not be open yet or may have closed for the year.

Best months to visit Marsaskala Bay, Malta

The swimming season here runs from June to September. July, August are the warmest and busiest, with the sea around 26 °C, so arrive early for parking and a good spot. June and September are quieter and cooler: good for walking and photos, though some beach bars and lifeguard posts may not be open yet or may already have closed for the year.

Who Marsaskala Bay, Malta suits

A rocky shore suits confident swimmers and snorkellers more than families with small children; bring water shoes. Easy access makes it practical for families, older visitors and anyone with a pushchair. Food and drink close by make it easy to stay for the whole day.
