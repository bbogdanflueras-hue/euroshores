Zanjice Beach, Lustica

4.4 / 5 Pebble beach facing Mamula island on the Lustica peninsula in Lustica, Herceg Novi · Hidden gem

Zanjice is a pebble bay on the Lustica peninsula at the mouth of the Bay of Kotor, with a view of Mamula island. The water is clear and boat trips run to the nearby Blue Cave. A restaurant and beach bar sit behind the shore, and the road down is narrow.

Planning your visit to Zanjice Beach, Lustica

Pebble beaches usually mean clear water but awkward walking. Bring water shoes and a thick towel or mat to sit on.

Access is rated easy, so most visitors can reach the beach without a long walk or special equipment.

The usual season runs June–September. Visiting near the start or end of that window usually means fewer people, but the sea is cooler and some facilities may not be open yet or may have closed for the year. We flag this beach as a hidden gem, so it is quieter than the headline beaches nearby, but it is no secret: arrive early in peak summer.

Best months to visit Zanjice Beach, Lustica

The swimming season here runs from June to September. July, August are the warmest and busiest, with the sea around 24 °C, so arrive early for parking and a good spot. June and September are quieter and cooler: good for walking and photos, though some beach bars and lifeguard posts may not be open yet or may already have closed for the year.

Who Zanjice Beach, Lustica suits

Pebbles and clear water suit swimmers and snorkellers; bring water shoes for an easier entry. Easy access makes it practical for families, older visitors and anyone with a pushchair. It is flagged as a hidden gem, so it suits travellers who prefer quieter places over the headline beaches. Food and drink close by make it easy to stay for the whole day.

Getting there and nearby

Small car park at the bay, limited in summer. The beach sits at about 42.405, 18.587 on the map, so use the map link above for directions.
