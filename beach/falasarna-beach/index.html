Falasarna Beach

A broad golden beach on the far west coast of Crete, with clear turquoise water and views toward the Gramvousa peninsula. It is known for its sunsets and has room for everyone even in summer.

Planning your visit to Falasarna Beach

Sand beaches are usually gentle underfoot and easy for swimmers and children, though they can be exposed to wind and waves, so check conditions on the day. Bring a hat and extra water, as shade can be scarce away from facilities.

Access is rated easy, so most visitors can reach the beach without a long walk or special equipment.

The usual season runs May–October. Visiting near the start or end of that window usually means fewer people, but the sea is cooler and some facilities may not be open yet or may have closed for the year.

Best months to visit Falasarna Beach

The swimming season here runs from May to October. June, July, August, September are the warmest and busiest, with the sea around 23 °C, so arrive early for parking and a good spot. May and October are quieter and cooler: good for walking and photos, though some beach bars and lifeguard posts may not be open yet or may already have closed for the year.

Who Falasarna Beach suits

Sand makes it comfortable for sunbathing, building sandcastles and long walks along the water line. Easy access makes it practical for families, older visitors and anyone with a pushchair. Food and drink close by make it easy to stay for the whole day.

Getting there and nearby

Free parking near the beach and along the access road. The beach sits at about 35.502, 23.580 on the map, so use the map link above for directions.
