Planning your visit to Playa de las Teresitas

Sand beaches are usually gentle underfoot and easy for swimmers and children, though they can be exposed to wind and waves, so check conditions on the day. Bring a hat and extra water, as shade can be scarce away from facilities.

Access is rated easy, so most visitors can reach the beach without a long walk or special equipment.

The usual season runs Year-round. Visiting near the start or end of that window usually means fewer people, but the sea is cooler and some facilities may not be open yet or may have closed for the year.

Frequently asked questions

When is the best time to visit Playa de las Teresitas?

Playa de las Teresitas is best visited Year-round. The sea is typically around 21 °C in season. Weekends and holidays are busy with local visitors, so a weekday morning is calmer.

How do I get to Playa de las Teresitas?

Access is rated easy. Parking near the beach; busy at weekends and holidays.

What facilities does Playa de las Teresitas have?

Showers, toilets, lifeguards, sunbeds and cafes.

Where is Playa de las Teresitas?

Playa de las Teresitas is in San Andres, Tenerife, Canary Islands, Spain (approx. coordinates 28.5077, -16.1875).

Is Playa de las Teresitas good for children?

Access is rated easy and the beach is sandy, which usually suits families. Check water and weather conditions on the day and keep children in sight near the water.

What should I bring to Playa de las Teresitas?

Facilities: Showers, toilets, lifeguards, sunbeds and cafes. A hat and sun protection are useful. Bring water and snacks if there is no kiosk or restaurant.
