Planning your visit to Banje Beach

Pebble beaches usually mean clear water but awkward walking. Bring water shoes and a thick towel or mat to sit on.

Access is rated easy, so most visitors can reach the beach without a long walk or special equipment.

The usual season runs June–September. Visiting near the start or end of that window usually means fewer people, but the sea is cooler and some facilities may not be open yet or may have closed for the year.

Frequently asked questions

Banje Beach is best visited June to September. The sea is typically around 25 °C in season. Entry is free, so bring a towel instead of paying for a sunbed, which costs roughly 15 to 25 euros each depending on the season. Wear water shoes for the pebbles.

How do I get to Banje Beach?

Access is rated easy. A short walk east of the Old Town; there is little parking nearby.

What facilities does Banje Beach have?

Showers, changing rooms, toilets, sunbed hire, nearby bars and water-sports rental.

Where is Banje Beach?
