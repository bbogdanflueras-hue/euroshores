Planning your visit to Sarakiniko

Rocky coves often have clear, deeper water that suits snorkelling, but the entry can be slippery. Wear water shoes, enter slowly and avoid swimming when there is a swell.

Access is rated easy, so most visitors can reach the beach without a long walk or special equipment.

The usual season runs May–October. Visiting near the start or end of that window usually means fewer people, but the sea is cooler and some facilities may not be open yet or may have closed for the year.

Best months to visit Sarakiniko

The swimming season here runs from May to October. June, July, August, September are the warmest and busiest, with the sea around 24 °C, so arrive early for parking and a good spot. May and October are quieter and cooler: good for walking and photos, though some beach bars and lifeguard posts may not be open yet or may already have closed for the year.

Who Sarakiniko suits

A rocky shore suits confident swimmers and snorkellers more than families with small children; bring water shoes. Easy access makes it practical for families, older visitors and anyone with a pushchair.

Getting there and nearby

Parking near the beach (fills early in summer); local buses also run from Adamas. The beach sits at about 36.743, 24.458 on the map, so use the map link above for directions. If you are planning a day around it, the closest other beaches on our list are Firiplaka Beach (9 km), Tsigrado Beach (9 km), Paleochori Beach (9 km).
