Unspoiled Beaches Without Sunbeds in Malta and Gozo

We rank the 8 unspoiled beaches without sunbeds in Malta and Gozo. St Peter's Pool (Malta) comes first with 4.5 / 5, followed by Wied il-Ghasri, Gozo, Fomm ir-Rih Bay.

The sea reaches about 24 degrees C in season, and most of these beaches are at their best from May to October. 3 of the 8 have easy access, and 6 are hidden gems.

Save on Pinterest

1St Peter's Pool

Marsaxlokk, Malta · 4.5 / 5 · Moderate access · about 24 °C

A natural rock pool on the Delimara peninsula, with clear water, pale limestone ledges and no sand.

2Wied il-Ghasri, Gozo

Gharb, Gozo · 4.5 / 5 · Moderate access · about 25 °C

Wied il-Ghasri is a narrow inlet cut into the cliffs on the north-west coast of Gozo, reached by steps down the gorge.

3Fomm ir-Rih Bay

Mgarr, Malta · 4.4 / 5 · Challenging access · about 23 °C

A remote pebble bay at the foot of steep cliffs on the west coast of Malta, with a rugged setting and no development.

4Ghar Lapsi, Siggiewi

Siggiewi, Malta · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Ghar Lapsi is a small rocky inlet on the south-west coast of Malta near Siggiewi, with ladders and flat rock platforms for swimming and no sand.

5Gnejna Bay

Mgarr, Malta · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

A sandy bay on the north-west coast of Malta, framed by low cliffs and known for its traditional boathouses.

6Mgarr ix-Xini

Gozo, Malta · 4.4 / 5 · Moderate access · about 24 °C

A narrow, sheltered inlet on the south coast of Gozo with a small pebble beach, clear water and a historic watchtower above.

7Imgiebah Bay

Selmun, Malta · 4.3 / 5 · Moderate access · about 23 °C

A small sandy bay on the north coast of Malta, reached by a path down from Selmun, with clear water and rocky headlands.

8Qbajjar Bay, Gozo

Gozo · 4.1 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Qbajjar Bay is a rocky bay on the north coast of Gozo near Marsalforn, next to the old salt pans.

Frequently asked questions

Which are the top-rated unspoiled beaches without sunbeds in Malta and Gozo?

Our top three are St Peter's Pool (4.5 out of 5), Wied il-Ghasri, Gozo (4.5 out of 5), Fomm ir-Rih Bay (4.4 out of 5). Scores are editorial ratings from the EuroShores team.

When is the best time to visit these beaches?

Most are at their best from May to October. Water temperatures average about 24 degrees C in season. Each beach page lists its exact season.

Which of these beaches are easiest to reach?

Ghar Lapsi, Siggiewi, Gnejna Bay, Qbajjar Bay, Gozo have easy access. The others need a walk, stairs or a rougher road, so check the access note on each page.
