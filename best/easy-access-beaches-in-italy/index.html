Easiest Beaches to Reach in Italy

We rank the top 40 of 64 easy-access beaches in Italy. Cala Brandinchi (Italy) comes first with 4.8 / 5, followed by La Cinta Beach, La Pelosa.

The sea reaches about 24 degrees C in season, and most of these beaches are at their best from June to September. 40 of the 40 have easy access, and 6 are hidden gems.

1Cala Brandinchi

San Teodoro, Sardinia, Italy · 4.8 / 5 · Easy access · about 23 °C

A long bay of fine pale sand and very shallow, clear water backed by stone pines, with Tavolara island rising on the horizon.

2La Cinta Beach

San Teodoro, Sardinia · 4.7 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

A long strip of soft white sand and shallow turquoise water just outside San Teodoro, with a lagoon behind it.

3La Pelosa

Stintino, Sardinia, Italy · 4.7 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

A shallow white-sand lagoon facing the Torre della Pelosa at the northwest tip of Sardinia.

4Porto Giunco

Villasimius, Sardinia · 4.7 / 5 · Easy access · about 23 °C

A wide sandy beach in the south-east of Sardinia, with an old watchtower on the headland and a lagoon behind.

5Spiaggia Grande

Amalfi Coast, Italy · 4.7 / 5 · Easy access · about 23 °C

Nestled beneath the pastel-coloured cliffside village of Positano — one of the most dramatic and romantic settings in Europe.

6Tuerredda Beach

Teulada, Sardinia, Italy · 4.7 / 5 · Easy access · about 23 °C

A crescent of fine pale sand and shallow turquoise water on the south coast of Sardinia, facing a small island just offshore.

7Lama Monachile

Polignano a Mare, Puglia · 4.6 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

A small pebble cove at the foot of Polignano a Mare, framed by tall limestone cliffs and houses built right at the edge.

8Punta Molentis

Villasimius, Sardinia · 4.6 / 5 · Easy access · about 23 °C

A small beach of pale sand and clear shallow water near Villasimius, between low granite rocks.

9San Vito Lo Capo

San Vito Lo Capo, Sicily, Italy · 4.6 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

A broad curve of pale sand and clear shallow water at the tip of north-west Sicily, backed by the town and a rocky headland.

10Scala dei Turchi

Realmonte, Sicily, Italy · 4.6 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

A dazzling white marl cliff carved into giant steps above a small sandy beach on the south coast of Sicily, near Agrigento.

11Torre dell'Orso Beach

Melendugno, Puglia · 4.6 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

A wide crescent of pale sand and shallow turquoise water in Salento, guarded by a watchtower and the famous Two Sisters rock stacks.

12Baia del Silenzio

Sestri Levante, Liguria, Italy · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 22 °C

A small sheltered bay of sand and pebbles beside the old town of Sestri Levante, with pastel houses and calm water.

13Cala Cipolla

Chia, Sardinia · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

A pretty bay on the south coast of Sardinia near Chia, with soft sand, clear water and a rocky headland.

14Costa Rei

Costa Rei, Castiadas, Sardinia · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

A long beach of fine white sand and clear shallow water on the south-east coast of Sardinia.

15Fetovaia Beach

Fetovaia, Elba · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 23 °C

A curved bay of golden sand and granite grains on Elba south-west coast, with calm clear water and a pine-scented hillside behind.

16Is Arutas

Cabras, Sinis Peninsula, Sardinia · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

A beach on the Sinis peninsula in west Sardinia, known for sand made of tiny quartz grains in many colours that give the shallow water a clear, bright look.

17Pizzomunno Beach

Vieste, Puglia · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

The beach of Pizzomunno is a curve of pale sand in Vieste, dominated by a white limestone stack that gives it its name.

18Punta Prosciutto

Porto Cesareo, Salento, Puglia · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

A long sandy beach on the Ionian coast of Salento, backed by dunes and part of a protected area within the Porto Cesareo marine reserve.

19Sa Colonia Beach

Chia, Domus de Maria, Sardinia · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

A sandy beach at Chia on the south coast of Sardinia, with a coastal lagoon behind it where pink flamingos sometimes feed.

20Spiaggia di Punta Aderci, Vasto

Vasto, Abruzzo · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

Punta Aderci is a protected coastal reserve near Vasto, with beaches of sand and pebbles below low cliffs and wild grass.

21Spiaggia di Sirolo

Sirolo, Marche · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

Sirolo is a hill town on the Conero Riviera, and its beaches are white pebble coves under steep green cliffs.

22Alimini Beach

Alimini, Otranto, Puglia · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

A stretch of fine sand and clear, gently sloping water on the Salento coast near Otranto, backed by pine woods and dunes.

23Cala Sinzias

Castiadas, Sardinia · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

A wide, gently shelving bay of fine pale sand and shallow turquoise water in south-east Sardinia, with granite rocks and pines on both sides.

24Camogli Beach

Camogli, Liguria · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

A curved pebble beach beneath the tall pastel houses and basilica of Camogli.

25Fiascherino Beach

Fiascherino, Lerici, Liguria · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 22 °C

A small cove on the Gulf of La Spezia between Lerici and Tellaro.

26Rena Bianca

Santa Teresa Gallura, Sardinia, Italy · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 22 °C

A pale sand beach beside the town of Santa Teresa Gallura, with clear water and granite rocks at the end of the bay.

27San Pietro in Bevagna Beach

Manduria, Puglia · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

A long sandy beach on the Ionian coast of Puglia, near Manduria, with a wide open shoreline and gently shelving water.

28Scilla Marina Grande

Scilla, Calabria · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

A long town beach in the old fishing town of Scilla, with the Ruffo castle rising above one end and views across to Sicily.

29Sperlonga Beach

Sperlonga, Lazio, Italy · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

Long stretches of sand either side of a whitewashed hill town on the Lazio coast, between Rome and Naples.

30Spiaggia dei Maronti

Ischia, Campania · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

A long beach of dark volcanic sand on the south coast of Ischia, backed by cliffs, with views along the coast and plenty of space.

31Spiaggia della Purita

Gallipoli, Puglia · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Spiaggia della Purita is a small sandy beach just outside the old town of Gallipoli on the Ionian coast.

32Spiaggia di Alassio

Alassio, Liguria · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 22 °C

Alassio has a long beach of golden sand, one of the widest on the Ligurian coast, below a promenade of cafes and shops.

33Spiaggia di Finale Ligure

Finale Ligure, Liguria · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 23 °C

Finale Ligure has a long beach of sand and fine pebbles below the white limestone cliffs of the Ligurian hills.

34Spiaggia di Peschici

Peschici, Puglia · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

Peschici is a whitewashed hill village on the Gargano coast, and its beach is a long stretch of fine sand below the old town.

35Spiaggia di Punta Ala

Punta Ala, Tuscany · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

Punta Ala is a quiet resort in the Tuscan Maremma, with a wide sandy beach edged by pine woods and a small marina.

36Spiaggia di Sabaudia

Sabaudia, Lazio · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

The beach of Sabaudia is a long, wide stretch of fine sand backed by dunes and the Circeo national park.

37Spiaggia Grande, Diamante

Diamante, Calabria · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Spiaggia Grande is the main beach of Diamante, a pebble and coarse sand bay on the Riviera dei Cedri.

38Spiaggia Guidi, Carloforte

Carloforte, Sardinia · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 23 °C

Spiaggia Guidi is a small sandy cove on the island of San Pietro, off the south-west coast of Sardinia.

39Balai Beach

Porto Torres, Sardinia · 4.3 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

A small pebble and sand cove on the north-west coast of Sardinia near Porto Torres, with clear water and a rocky shoreline.

40Bibione Beach

Bibione, Veneto · 4.3 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

Bibione has a wide beach of fine sand stretching along the Adriatic coast between the Tagliamento and Livenza rivers.

Frequently asked questions

Which are the top-rated easy-access beaches in Italy?

Our top three are Cala Brandinchi (4.8 out of 5), La Cinta Beach (4.7 out of 5), La Pelosa (4.7 out of 5). Scores are editorial ratings from the EuroShores team.

When is the best time to visit these beaches?

Most are at their best from June to September. Water temperatures average about 24 degrees C in season. Each beach page lists its exact season.

Which of these beaches are easiest to reach?

Cala Brandinchi, La Cinta Beach, La Pelosa, Porto Giunco, Spiaggia Grande and 35 more have easy access. The others need a walk, stairs or a rougher road, so check the access note on each page.
