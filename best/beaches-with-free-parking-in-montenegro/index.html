Best Beaches with Free Parking in Montenegro

We rank the 4 beaches with free parking in Montenegro. Sveti Stefan (Montenegro) comes first with 4.5 / 5, followed by Valdanos Bay, Jaz Beach.

The sea reaches about 24 degrees C in season, and most of these beaches are at their best from June to September. 3 of the 4 have easy access, and 1 are hidden gems.

1Sveti Stefan

Budva Riviera, Montenegro · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

A fairytale island-village connected to the mainland by a narrow isthmus of pink pebbles and crystal-clear Adriatic water.

2Valdanos Bay

Ulcinj, Montenegro · 4.5 / 5 · Moderate access · about 24 °C

A crescent-shaped bay of rounded pebbles, roughly 300 metres long, tucked below old olive groves on the south coast of Montenegro near Ulcinj.

3Jaz Beach

Budva, Montenegro · 4.3 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

4Kamenovo

Budva Riviera, Montenegro · 4.3 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

Frequently asked questions

Which are the top-rated beaches with free parking in Montenegro?

Our top three are Sveti Stefan (4.5 out of 5), Valdanos Bay (4.5 out of 5), Jaz Beach (4.3 out of 5). Scores are editorial ratings from the EuroShores team.

When is the best time to visit these beaches?

Most are at their best from June to September. Water temperatures average about 24 degrees C in season. Each beach page lists its exact season.

Which of these beaches are easiest to reach?

Sveti Stefan, Jaz Beach, Kamenovo have easy access. The others need a walk, stairs or a rougher road, so check the access note on each page.
