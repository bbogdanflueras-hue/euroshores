Best Beaches in the Cyclades in June

We rank the 34 beaches to visit in the Cyclades in June. Kolona Beach (Greece) comes first with 4.7 / 5, followed by Sarakiniko, Katergo Beach.

The sea reaches about 24 degrees C in season, and most of these beaches are at their best from June to September. 27 of the 34 have easy access, and 7 are hidden gems.

1Kolona Beach

Kythnos, Cyclades · 4.7 / 5 · Moderate access · about 24 °C

A narrow strip of fine sand joining the main island of Kythnos to the islet of Agios Loukas, with water on both sides.

2Sarakiniko

Milos, Greece · 4.7 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

A lunar shoreline of smooth white volcanic rock sculpted by wind and sea, with deep clear water for swimming from the rocks.

3Katergo Beach

Katergo, Folegandros · 4.6 / 5 · Challenging access · about 23 °C

A wild, unspoilt cove on the south-east side of Folegandros, backed by tall cliffs and with deep blue water.

4Kleftiko

Milos, Cyclades, Greece · 4.6 / 5 · Challenging access · about 24 °C

A cluster of white rock arches, caves and sheltered coves on the south-west coast of Milos, once used as a pirate hideout.

5Agia Anna Beach, Naxos

Agia Anna, Naxos, Cyclades · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 26 °C

Agia Anna is a village beach on the south-west coast of Naxos, with a long strip of golden sand, clear water and a row of tavernas and small hotels behind the shore.

6Agios Prokopios Beach

Agios Prokopios, Naxos, Greece · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

A long sandy beach on the west coast of Naxos, with shallow turquoise water and views across to Paros.

7Agios Sostis Beach

Mykonos · 4.5 / 5 · Moderate access · about 23 °C

A quiet sandy beach on the north coast of Mykonos, far from the busy clubs of the south.

8Golden Beach Paros

Paros · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

A long sandy beach on the east coast of Paros, open to the wind and popular with windsurfers and kitesurfers in summer.

9Kolymbithres Beach

Naoussa, Paros, Cyclades · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

A beach near Naoussa on Paros, known for its smooth, sculpted rock formations that separate small sandy coves.

10Mikri Vigla Beach

Mikri Vigla, Naxos · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 23 °C

Long, pale beaches on the south-west coast of Naxos, split into two bays by a small rocky headland.

11Mikro Alyko Beach

Naxos · 4.5 / 5 · Moderate access · about 24 °C

A small sandy cove on the south-west coast of Naxos, with clear turquoise water and a rocky headland on one side.

12Mylopotas Beach

Mylopotas, Ios, Cyclades · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

A wide horseshoe bay of golden sand, about 2 km from Chora on Ios.

13Plaka Beach

Naxos, Cyclades, Greece · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 23 °C

A very long sand beach on the southwest coast of Naxos, backed by dunes and open to the Aegean.

14Psili Ammos Beach, Serifos

Psili Ammos, Serifos, Cyclades · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Psili Ammos is a quiet cove of fine pale sand on the south-east coast of Serifos, with shallow turquoise water and a line of tamarisk trees behind the beach.

15Red Beach Santorini

Akrotiri, Santorini · 4.5 / 5 · Moderate access · about 23 °C

A pebble beach on the south coast of Santorini near Akrotiri, set below tall red volcanic cliffs with deep blue water.

16Santa Maria Beach

Naoussa, Paros · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

A sandy bay on the north coast of Paros near Naoussa, with clear water and a relaxed summer atmosphere.

17Elia Beach

Elia, Mykonos, Greece · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

One of the longer sandy beaches on Mykonos, a wide strip of golden sand and clear water on the south coast.

18Firiplaka Beach

Milos · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

A sandy beach on Milos below colourful volcanic cliffs, with clear turquoise water.

19Kalo Livadi Beach

Mykonos · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

A long sandy bay on the south-east coast of Mykonos with turquoise water.

20Paleochori Beach

Milos · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

A sandy bay on the south coast of Milos, with a small settlement along the shore and blue water.

21Platis Gialos Beach

Mykonos · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

A sheltered sandy bay on the south coast of Mykonos with clear water and a lively waterfront behind the beach.

22Tsigrado Beach

Milos · 4.4 / 5 · Challenging access · about 24 °C

A small sandy cove on Milos reached by a steep cliff path, with clear turquoise water.

23Vlychada Beach, Santorini

Vlychada, Santorini, Cyclades · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 26 °C

Vlychada is a quiet beach on the south coast of Santorini, with dark volcanic sand and pebbles below pale eroded cliffs.

24Agios Georgios Beach, Naxos

Agios Georgios, Naxos Town, Cyclades · 4.3 / 5 · Easy access · about 26 °C

Agios Georgios is the town beach of Naxos, a long strip of sand with shallow, calm water just a short walk south of the old harbour.

25Fasolou Beach, Sifnos

Fasolou, Faros, Sifnos · 4.3 / 5 · Easy access · about 26 °C

Fasolou is a small sandy bay close to the fishing hamlet of Faros on Sifnos, with clear shallow water and low dry hills around it.

26Kalafatis Beach

Kalafatis, Mykonos · 4.3 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

A long golden bay on the east coast of Mykonos, with a more relaxed feel than the famous party beaches.

27Paradise Beach

Mykonos, Cyclades · 4.3 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

A long sandy bay on the south coast of Mykonos, known for its beach clubs and lively atmosphere from morning to night.

28Perivolos Beach, Santorini

Perivolos, Santorini · 4.3 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Perivolos is a long beach of dark volcanic sand and fine pebbles on the south coast of Santorini, lined with relaxed beach bars and sunbeds.

29Psarou Beach, Mykonos

Psarou, Mykonos, Cyclades · 4.3 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Psarou is a sheltered sandy bay on the south coast of Mykonos, and one of the best known beaches on the island.

30Spiaggia di Giardini Naxos

Giardini Naxos, Sicily · 4.3 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Giardini Naxos is a bay of grey pebbles and warm clear water at the foot of Taormina, with Mount Etna smoking in the distance.

31Super Paradise Beach

Plintri, Mykonos · 4.3 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

A sandy cove on the south coast of Mykonos, known for its beach clubs and lively atmosphere.

32Kamari Beach

Kamari, Santorini, Cyclades, Greece · 4.2 / 5 · Easy access · about 23 °C

A long beach of dark volcanic sand and pebbles on the east coast of Santorini, backed by a seafront strip of hotels, cafes and tavernas.

33Perissa Beach

Perissa, Santorini · 4.2 / 5 · Easy access · about 23 °C

Perissa is a long beach of black volcanic sand and fine pebbles on the southeast coast of Santorini, beneath the rocky ridge of Mesa Vouno.

34Pollonia Beach, Milos

Pollonia, Milos, Cyclades · 4.2 / 5 · Easy access · about 26 °C

Pollonia is a small fishing village on the north-east tip of Milos, with a calm sandy beach beside the harbour and shallow water that suits children.

Frequently asked questions

Which are the top-rated beaches to visit in the Cyclades in June?

Our top three are Kolona Beach (4.7 out of 5), Sarakiniko (4.7 out of 5), Katergo Beach (4.6 out of 5). Scores are editorial ratings from the EuroShores team.

When is the best time to visit these beaches?

Most are at their best from June to September. Water temperatures average about 24 degrees C in season. Each beach page lists its exact season.

Which of these beaches are easiest to reach?

Sarakiniko, Agia Anna Beach, Naxos, Agios Prokopios Beach, Golden Beach Paros, Kolymbithres Beach and 22 more have easy access. The others need a walk, stairs or a rougher road, so check the access note on each page.
