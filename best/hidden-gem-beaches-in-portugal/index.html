Hidden Gem Beaches in Portugal

We rank the 35 hidden gem beaches in Portugal. Benagil Cove (Portugal) comes first with 4.7 / 5, followed by Portinho da Arrabida, Praia da Bordeira.

The sea reaches about 21 degrees C in season, and most of these beaches are at their best from June to September. 24 of the 35 have easy access, and 35 are hidden gems.

1Benagil Cove

Algarve, Portugal · 4.7 / 5 · Moderate access · about 22 °C

A small cove leading to a sea cave with a skylight in its roof — best seen by kayak or paddleboard.

2Portinho da Arrabida

Portinho da Arrabida, Setubal · 4.7 / 5 · Moderate access · about 23 °C

Portinho da Arrabida is a small sheltered bay in the Arrabida Natural Park, with white sand, calm turquoise water and a green mountain rising behind.

3Praia da Bordeira

Carrapateira, Algarve · 4.7 / 5 · Easy access · about 21 °C

A wide, wild Atlantic beach on the west Algarve coast with dunes, cliffs and a shallow lagoon that forms behind the sand.

4Praia da Ursa

Sintra-Cascais Natural Park, Portugal · 4.7 / 5 · Challenging access · about 17 °C

A small, wild sand cove below steep cliffs near Cabo da Roca, known for its tall rock stacks and rough Atlantic surf.

5Praia do Carvalho

Carvoeiro, Algarve, Portugal · 4.7 / 5 · Moderate access · about 19 °C

A small golden cove enclosed by tall Algarve cliffs, reached through a short tunnel cut into the rock and down a flight of steps.

6Ilha de Tavira

Tavira, Algarve · 4.6 / 5 · Moderate access · about 23 °C

A long barrier island off the eastern Algarve with wide sandy beaches, calm lagoon water on one side and the open Atlantic on the other.

7Praia da Albandeira

Lagoa, Algarve · 4.6 / 5 · Moderate access · about 22 °C

A small, sheltered cove at the foot of golden limestone cliffs in the Algarve, with a natural rock arch and clear water.

8Praia da Figueirinha, Setubal

Arrabida, Setubal · 4.6 / 5 · Easy access · about 22 °C

Praia da Figueirinha is a calm sand beach in the Arrabida natural park, with turquoise water and green hills behind.

9Praia de Cacela Velha

Cacela Velha, Algarve · 4.6 / 5 · Moderate access · about 23 °C

Cacela Velha is a tiny whitewashed village on a bluff in the eastern Algarve, with a beach on a sandbar facing the Ria Formosa lagoon.

10Praia de Galapos, Arrabida

Galapos, Setubal, Arrabida · 4.6 / 5 · Moderate access · about 22 °C

Praia de Galapos is a sheltered bay of pale sand and fine pebbles below the green Arrabida mountains south of Lisbon.

11Praia do Beliche

Sagres, Vila do Bispo, Algarve · 4.6 / 5 · Moderate access · about 20 °C

A sheltered sandy cove near Sagres on the far south-west tip of Portugal, backed by tall cliffs and facing the open Atlantic with golden sand and clear water.

12Praia Atlantica, Troia

Troia, Setubal · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 22 °C

Troia is a long sand peninsula south of Lisbon, with wide Atlantic beaches, white dunes and clear water.

13Praia da Cordoama, Vila do Bispo

Cordoama, Vila do Bispo, Algarve · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 20 °C

Praia da Cordoama is a long, open Atlantic beach near Vila do Bispo in the western Algarve, backed by cliffs and dunes.

14Praia da Prainha, Alvor

Prainha, Alvor, Portimao, Algarve · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 22 °C

Praia da Prainha is a small cove of golden sand between limestone cliffs near Alvor in the Algarve.

15Praia de Melides, Alentejo

Melides, Grandola, Alentejo · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 21 °C

Praia de Melides is a wide, wild beach of pale sand on the Alentejo coast, with a freshwater lagoon behind the dunes and almost no development.

16Praia do Martinhal, Sagres

Sagres, Algarve · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 21 °C

Praia do Martinhal is a sheltered bay of fine sand near Sagres on the far west of the Algarve, with rock pools at low tide and calmer water than the Atlantic coast.

17Praia do Vale Figueiras

Vila do Bispo, Algarve · 4.5 / 5 · Moderate access · about 21 °C

A quiet Atlantic beach on the west Algarve coast with golden sand, low cliffs and room to spread out away from the busier south-coast resorts.

18Praia do Zavial

Raposeira, Vila do Bispo, Algarve · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 21 °C

A broad sandy bay on the western Algarve, backed by low cliffs and a beachfront restaurant.

19Praia da Fonte da Telha, Costa da Caparica

Fonte da Telha, Almada, Setubal · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 20 °C

Praia da Fonte da Telha is a long and wide beach of golden sand at the southern end of the Costa da Caparica, backed by sandy cliffs and a protected landscape.

20Praia da Fuseta, Algarve

Fuseta, Olhao, Algarve · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 23 °C

Praia da Fuseta is a long, quiet beach of fine sand on a barrier island in the Ria Formosa lagoon of the eastern Algarve, reached by a short ferry or footbridge from the fishing village of Fuseta.

21Praia da Ingrina

Raposeira, Vila do Bispo, Algarve · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 21 °C

A small sandy cove on the western Algarve near Raposeira, sheltered by rocks.

22Praia de Santa Barbara

Ribeira Grande, Azores · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 20 °C

A long, dark volcanic sand beach on the north coast of Sao Miguel, popular with surfers.

23Praia do Castelejo

Vila do Bispo, Algarve · 4.4 / 5 · Moderate access · about 19 °C

A wide Atlantic beach on the wild western Algarve, set below tilted dark schist cliffs and rolling hills.

24Praia do Magoito, Sintra

Magoito, Sintra, Lisbon Coast · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 19 °C

Praia do Magoito is a small sandy cove between golden sandstone cliffs on the Sintra coast, west of Lisbon.

25Praia do Seixal

Seixal, Madeira · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 21 °C

A small beach of dark volcanic sand on the north coast of Madeira, framed by steep green cliffs.

26Praia dos Salgados, Albufeira

Salgados, Gale, Albufeira, Algarve · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 23 °C

Praia dos Salgados is a long beach of golden sand in the central Algarve, beside a protected coastal lagoon known for its birdlife.

27Vila Nova de Milfontes Beach

Vila Nova de Milfontes, Alentejo Coast · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 19 °C

Vila Nova de Milfontes is a white village at the mouth of the River Mira on the Alentejo coast.

28Praia da Alagoa, Altura

Altura, Eastern Algarve · 4.3 / 5 · Easy access · about 22 °C

Praia da Alagoa is a long beach of fine golden sand near Altura in the eastern Algarve, with shallow water and a quiet local feel.

29Praia da Consolacao, Peniche

Consolacao, Peniche, Leiria · 4.3 / 5 · Easy access · about 19 °C

Praia da Consolacao is a long beach of fine sand on the coast near Peniche, popular with surfers and with families.

30Praia da Lagoa de Albufeira, Sesimbra

Lagoa de Albufeira, Sesimbra, Setubal · 4.3 / 5 · Easy access · about 20 °C

Praia da Lagoa de Albufeira is a wide beach on the Atlantic coast south of Lisbon, beside a lagoon that opens to the sea in winter.

31Praia de Afife, Viana do Castelo

Afife, Viana do Castelo, Minho · 4.3 / 5 · Easy access · about 19 °C

Praia de Afife is a wide Atlantic beach with golden sand and dunes on the Minho coast, a short drive north of Viana do Castelo.

32Praia de Caminha, Minho

Caminha, Viana do Castelo, Minho · 4.3 / 5 · Easy access · about 19 °C

Praia de Caminha lies at the mouth of the Minho river on the northern coast of Portugal, facing Spain across the water.

33Praia de Santa Rita, Torres Vedras

Santa Rita, Torres Vedras, Lisbon Region · 4.3 / 5 · Easy access · about 19 °C

Praia de Santa Rita is a wide beach of golden sand on the Silver Coast, below low cliffs and fields near Torres Vedras.

34Praia Verde, Castro Marim

Praia Verde, Castro Marim, Algarve · 4.3 / 5 · Easy access · about 23 °C

Praia Verde is a quiet beach on the eastern Algarve, with fine sand and a pine forest behind the shore, between Monte Gordo and Manta Rota.

35Praia da Vagueira

Vagueira, Centro · 4.2 / 5 · Easy access · about 19 °C

Praia da Vagueira is a long, wide Atlantic beach of golden sand backed by dunes, near the lagoon of Aveiro.

Frequently asked questions

Which are the top-rated hidden gem beaches in Portugal?

Our top three are Benagil Cove (4.7 out of 5), Portinho da Arrabida (4.7 out of 5), Praia da Bordeira (4.7 out of 5). Scores are editorial ratings from the EuroShores team.

When is the best time to visit these beaches?

Most are at their best from June to September. Water temperatures average about 21 degrees C in season. Each beach page lists its exact season.

Which of these beaches are easiest to reach?

Praia da Bordeira, Praia da Figueirinha, Setubal, Praia Atlantica, Troia, Praia da Cordoama, Vila do Bispo, Praia da Prainha, Alvor and 19 more have easy access. The others need a walk, stairs or a rougher road, so check the access note on each page.
