Easiest Beaches to Reach in Montenegro

We rank the 16 easy-access beaches in Montenegro. Ada Bojana Beach, Montenegro (Montenegro) comes first with 4.5 / 5, followed by Queens Beach, Sveti Stefan.

The sea reaches about 24 degrees C in season, and most of these beaches are at their best from June to September. 16 of the 16 have easy access, and 2 are hidden gems.

Save on Pinterest

1Ada Bojana Beach, Montenegro

Ada Bojana, Ulcinj, Montenegro · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 26 °C

Ada Bojana is a triangular island formed by the mouth of the Bojana river at the border with Albania, with a long beach of dark golden sand on the sea side.

2Queens Beach

Milocer, Budva Riviera, Montenegro · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

Two small coves of pink-tinged pebbles and sand below a wooded park on the Budva Riviera, with calm clear water.

3Sveti Stefan

Budva Riviera, Montenegro · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

A fairytale island-village connected to the mainland by a narrow isthmus of pink pebbles and crystal-clear Adriatic water.

4Becici Beach

Budva Riviera, Montenegro · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

A long, curving beach on the Budva Riviera with mountains rising behind the resort town.

5Mogren Beach

Budva, Montenegro · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 23 °C

Mogren is a pair of small coves just beside the old town of Budva, joined by a footpath cut into the cliffs.

6Zanjice Beach, Lustica

Lustica, Herceg Novi · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

Zanjice is a pebble bay on the Lustica peninsula at the mouth of the Bay of Kotor, with a view of Mamula island.

7Buljarica Beach

Petrovac, Budva Municipality, Montenegro · 4.3 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

A long pebble beach near Petrovac with green hills behind it and a quieter feel than the busier Budva beaches.

8Jaz Beach

Budva, Montenegro · 4.3 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

One of the longest beaches in Montenegro, a wide pebble sweep about 2.

9Kamenovo

Budva Riviera, Montenegro · 4.3 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

A long pebble-and-sand beach on the Budva Riviera, framed by green hills and calm, clear water.

10Petrovac Beach

Petrovac, Budva Riviera, Montenegro · 4.3 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

A long curve of fine pebbles and sand in front of the town of Petrovac, with two small islets offshore and hills behind.

11Plavi Horizonti Beach, Lustica

Lustica Peninsula · 4.3 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

Plavi Horizonti is a wide, clean beach on the Lustica peninsula, with fine pebbles, some sand and very clear water.

12Rafailovici Beach, Budva Riviera

Rafailovici, Budva, Montenegro · 4.3 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Rafailovici is a seaside village on the Budva Riviera with a long beach of small pebbles and a promenade that leads to the neighbouring village of Becici.

13Sutomore Beach

Bar, Montenegro · 4.3 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

A pebble beach town and rocky coast between Bar and Petrovac, with clear water below steep hills.

14Ulcinj Mala Plaza

Ulcinj, Montenegro · 4.3 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

A small town beach below the old town of Ulcinj, with a sandy shore and views of the rocky headland.

15Przno Beach

Przno, Budva Riviera, Montenegro · 4.2 / 5 · Easy access · about 23 °C

A small sheltered bay on the Budva Riviera with sunbeds along a shore of pebbles and sand, and the village of Przno behind.

16Veliki Pijesak Beach, Ulcinj

Ulcinj · 4.1 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Veliki Pijesak is a sandy beach near Ulcinj on the south Montenegrin coast, with shallow warm water that suits families.

Frequently asked questions

Which are the top-rated easy-access beaches in Montenegro?

Our top three are Ada Bojana Beach, Montenegro (4.5 out of 5), Queens Beach (4.5 out of 5), Sveti Stefan (4.5 out of 5). Scores are editorial ratings from the EuroShores team.

When is the best time to visit these beaches?

Most are at their best from June to September. Water temperatures average about 24 degrees C in season. Each beach page lists its exact season.

Which of these beaches are easiest to reach?

Ada Bojana Beach, Montenegro, Queens Beach, Sveti Stefan, Becici Beach, Mogren Beach and 11 more have easy access. The others need a walk, stairs or a rougher road, so check the access note on each page.
