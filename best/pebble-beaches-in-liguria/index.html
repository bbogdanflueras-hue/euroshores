Best Pebble Beaches in Liguria

We rank the 9 pebble beaches in Liguria. Paraggi Beach (Italy) comes first with 4.5 / 5, followed by Bonassola Beach, Liguria, Camogli Beach.

The sea reaches about 24 degrees C in season, and most of these beaches are at their best from June to September. 8 of the 9 have easy access, and 3 are hidden gems.

Save on Pinterest

1Paraggi Beach

Paraggi, Santa Margherita Ligure, Liguria · 4.5 / 5 · Moderate access · about 24 °C

A small sheltered bay on the Portofino coast, framed by pastel houses and wooded hills.

2Bonassola Beach, Liguria

Bonassola, La Spezia, Liguria · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

Bonassola is a small seaside village on the Ligurian coast, a short drive from the Cinque Terre, with a beach of pebbles and coarse sand.

3Camogli Beach

Camogli, Liguria · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

A curved pebble beach beneath the tall pastel houses and basilica of Camogli.

4Fiascherino Beach

Fiascherino, Lerici, Liguria · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 22 °C

A small cove on the Gulf of La Spezia between Lerici and Tellaro.

5Bergeggi Beach, Liguria

Bergeggi, Savona, Liguria · 4.3 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

Bergeggi is a small village on the Ligurian coast with a pebble beach facing the island of Bergeggi, a protected marine reserve with clear water for snorkelling.

6Lerici Beach, Liguria

Lerici, La Spezia, Liguria · 4.3 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

Lerici is a colourful harbour town on the Gulf of La Spezia, known as the Gulf of Poets, with a small beach of sand and pebbles below a medieval castle.

7Noli Beach, Liguria

Noli, Savona, Liguria · 4.3 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

Noli is a small medieval seaside town on the Ligurian Riviera, with a beach of sand and pebbles below the old walls and towers.

8Monterosso al Mare

Monterosso al Mare, Cinque Terre, Liguria · 4.2 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

The main beach of the Cinque Terre, at Fegina below the village of Monterosso.

9Spiaggia di Sanremo

Sanremo, Liguria · 4.1 / 5 · Easy access · about 22 °C

Sanremo has several public beaches of pebbles and sand along its Riviera coast, with calm water and palm trees on the promenade.
