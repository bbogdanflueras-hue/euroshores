Best Beaches with Sunbeds in Sicily

We rank the 9 beaches with sunbeds and umbrellas in Sicily. San Vito Lo Capo (Italy) comes first with 4.6 / 5, followed by Tonnara di Scopello, Isola Bella.

The sea reaches about 25 degrees C in season, and most of these beaches are at their best from June to September. 8 of the 9 have easy access, and 3 are hidden gems.

Save on Pinterest

1San Vito Lo Capo

San Vito Lo Capo, Sicily, Italy · 4.6 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

A broad curve of pale sand and clear shallow water at the tip of north-west Sicily, backed by the town and a rocky headland.

2Tonnara di Scopello

Scopello, Castellammare del Golfo, Sicily · 4.6 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Tonnara di Scopello is a small cove on the north-west coast of Sicily, with an old tuna fishery building, tall sea stacks called faraglioni just offshore and very clear water.

3Isola Bella

Taormina, Sicily · 4.4 / 5 · Moderate access · about 25 °C

A small island and pebble bay below Taormina, linked to the shore by a narrow strip.

4Spiaggia di Fontane Bianche

Fontane Bianche, Siracusa, Sicily · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Fontane Bianche is a seaside village south of Syracuse, with a long beach of soft pale sand and clear turquoise water.

5Cefalu Beach

Cefalu, Sicily, Italy · 4.3 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

A sandy beach right below the old houses of Cefalu, with rocks at the edges and the town and cliffs behind.

6Mazzaro Beach, Taormina

Mazzaro, Taormina, Sicily · 4.3 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Mazzaro is a pebble bay on the Ionian coast of Sicily, below the hill town of Taormina and connected to it by a cable car.

7Mondello Beach

Mondello, Palermo, Sicily, Italy · 4.3 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

A long curve of pale sand with shallow turquoise water, set between two headlands with a mountain behind the bay.

8Punta Secca Beach, Sicily

Punta Secca, Santa Croce Camerina, Sicily · 4.3 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Punta Secca is a small fishing village on the south coast of Sicily, with a sandy beach beside whitewashed houses and a lighthouse.

9Spiaggia di Marzamemi

Marzamemi, Sicily · 4.3 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

Marzamemi is an old tuna-fishing village on the south-east tip of Sicily, and the beaches around it are sandy and shallow, with very clear water.

Frequently asked questions

Which are the top-rated beaches with sunbeds and umbrellas in Sicily?

Our top three are San Vito Lo Capo (4.6 out of 5), Tonnara di Scopello (4.6 out of 5), Isola Bella (4.4 out of 5). Scores are editorial ratings from the EuroShores team.

When is the best time to visit these beaches?

Most are at their best from June to September. Water temperatures average about 25 degrees C in season. Each beach page lists its exact season.

Which of these beaches are easiest to reach?

San Vito Lo Capo, Tonnara di Scopello, Spiaggia di Fontane Bianche, Cefalu Beach, Mazzaro Beach, Taormina and 3 more have easy access. The others need a walk, stairs or a rougher road, so check the access note on each page.
