Best Beaches with Free Parking in Spain

We rank the 36 beaches with free parking in Spain. Cala Macarelleta (Spain) comes first with 4.8 / 5, followed by As Catedrais, Cala d'Hort.

The sea reaches about 22 degrees C in season, and most of these beaches are at their best from June to September. 25 of the 36 have easy access, and 12 are hidden gems.

Save on Pinterest

1Cala Macarelleta

Menorca, Spain · 4.8 / 5 · Moderate access · about 24 °C

A tiny turquoise cove tucked below pine-covered cliffs, a short coastal walk from its bigger neighbour.

2As Catedrais

Ribadeo, Galicia, Spain · 4.7 / 5 · Moderate access · about 18 °C

Towering sea arches and caves carved by the Atlantic along the coast of Galicia, walkable only at low tide.

3Cala d'Hort

Sant Josep, Ibiza · 4.7 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

A small, beautifully framed cove in south-west Ibiza where the dramatic rock of Es Vedra rises from the sea just offshore.

4Playa de Sotavento

Jandia, Fuerteventura · 4.7 / 5 · Moderate access · about 22 °C

A very long beach of pale sand on the south of Fuerteventura, with a shallow turquoise lagoon and a sandbar that changes with the tides.

5Playa de Torimbia

Llanes, Asturias · 4.7 / 5 · Moderate access · about 19 °C

A beautiful, unspoilt cove of golden sand backed by green meadows and cliffs on the Asturian coast.

6Cala Galdana

Cala Galdana, Ferreries, Menorca · 4.6 / 5 · Easy access · about 23 °C

A half-crescent cove of white sand and calm, shallow water on the south coast of Menorca, ringed by pine-covered cliffs.

7Cala Mitjana

Ciutadella, Menorca · 4.6 / 5 · Moderate access · about 24 °C

A small, sheltered cove of white sand and clear turquoise water, framed by pine trees and low cliffs on the south coast of Menorca.

8Cala Santanyi

Santanyi, Mallorca · 4.6 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

A small sandy cove tucked between pine-covered cliffs on the south-east coast of Mallorca, with calm turquoise water.

9Calo des Moro

Santanyi, Mallorca · 4.6 / 5 · Challenging access · about 24 °C

A very small sandy cove squeezed between rocky walls on Mallorca south-east coast, known for its turquoise water.

10Playa de Calblanque

Cartagena, Murcia · 4.6 / 5 · Moderate access · about 22 °C

A string of sandy coves inside Calblanque Regional Park on the Murcia coast, with dunes and low hills and no buildings behind the beach.

11Playa de los Muertos, Carboneras

Carboneras, Almeria, Andalusia · 4.6 / 5 · Moderate access · about 23 °C

Playa de los Muertos is a wild beach of pale pebbles and sand near the Cabo de Gata-Nijar natural park, reached down a steep path from a car park.

12Cala del Moraig

Benitatxell, Costa Blanca · 4.5 / 5 · Moderate access · about 24 °C

A pebble cove on the Costa Blanca between steep rocky cliffs, with clear blue water and a rugged, dramatic shoreline.

13Cala Llombards

Santanyi, Mallorca · 4.5 / 5 · Moderate access · about 24 °C

A small sandy cove on the south-east coast of Mallorca, set between low rocky cliffs and pine trees with clear shallow water.

14Itzurun Beach

Zumaia, Gipuzkoa, Basque Country · 4.5 / 5 · Moderate access · about 20 °C

A golden-sand beach of about 270 m in Zumaia, backed by the layered rock cliffs of the Basque flysch coast.

15Platja de Cadaques

Cadaques, Costa Brava · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 22 °C

Cadaques is a whitewashed fishing village at the far northeast of the Costa Brava, with a small pebble and sand beach on a sheltered bay.

16Playa de Amadores, Gran Canaria

Amadores, Mogan, Gran Canaria, Canary Islands · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

Playa de Amadores is a curved bay of white sand on the south-west coast of Gran Canaria, protected by breakwaters that keep the water calm and clear.

17Playa de Berria

Santona, Cantabria · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 20 °C

A broad, golden beach of about 2 km with dunes, a nature reserve behind it and views to the Monte Buciero cliffs.

18Playa de Carnota

Carnota, A Coruna, Galicia · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 20 °C

Playa de Carnota is one of the longest beaches in Galicia, a wide stretch of fine pale sand on the Costa da Morte, with the granite slopes of Monte Pindo rising behind.

19Playa de Famara

Caleta de Famara, Teguise, Lanzarote · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 22 °C

A long, wide sandy beach on the north-west coast of Lanzarote, framed by the dramatic Famara cliffs and popular with surfers and kitesurfers.

20Playa de la Barrosa

Chiclana de la Frontera, Andalusia · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 21 °C

An 8 km sweep of fine golden sand on the Costa de la Luz, backed by pine woods and a low red cliff at the north end.

21Playazo de Rodalquilar

Rodalquilar, Cabo de Gata-Nijar, Almeria · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 22 °C

El Playazo is a long beach of sand and fine gravel near Rodalquilar, in the Cabo de Gata-Nijar natural park in Almeria, with the ruined Castillo de San Ramon at the north end.

22Praia da Lanzada

Sanxenxo, Galicia · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 19 °C

One of the longest beaches in Galicia, with about 2 km of fine white sand backed by dunes.

23Praia de Pantin, Valdovino

Pantin, Valdovino, A Coruna, Galicia · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 19 °C

Praia de Pantin is a long beach of golden sand in Valdovino on the north coast of Galicia, known for its waves and its surf competitions.

24Platja de Muro

Muro, Mallorca · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

A long beach of fine sand in the Bay of Alcudia, backed by dunes and an old watchtower.

25Platja de Sa Riera, Begur

Sa Riera, Begur, Costa Brava · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

Sa Riera is a quiet cove below the village of Begur on the Costa Brava, with a small beach of coarse sand, clear water and pine-covered headlands.

26Playa de Cabopino

Marbella, Costa del Sol · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 22 °C

A sandy beach on the Costa del Sol beside the small Cabopino marina and an old watchtower, backed by pine woods and dunes.

27Ses Salines

Sant Josep, Ibiza, Balearic Islands · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

A long sandy beach at the southern tip of Ibiza, inside the Ses Salines natural park with dunes and pine woods behind.

28Platja de Roses

Roses, Costa Brava · 4.3 / 5 · Easy access · about 23 °C

Roses sits on a big, sweeping bay at the top of the Costa Brava, with a long beach of fine sand and calm water.

29Playa de Poniente

Benidorm, Costa Blanca · 4.3 / 5 · Easy access · about 22 °C

Poniente is the calmer of the two big city beaches of Benidorm, with fine golden sand and a long promenade.

30Playa de Santa Ponsa, Mallorca

Santa Ponsa, Calvia, Mallorca · 4.3 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Santa Ponsa is a sheltered bay on the south-west coast of Mallorca with a wide beach of fine sand and calm, shallow water.

31Sant Elm Beach

Sant Elm, Andratx, Mallorca · 4.3 / 5 · Easy access · about 23 °C

A small sandy bay in the village of Sant Elm at the south-west tip of Mallorca, with the island of Sa Dragonera offshore.

32Son Bou Beach, Menorca

Son Bou, Alaior, Menorca · 4.3 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

Son Bou is the longest beach on Menorca, a wide strip of golden sand on the south coast backed by dunes and a hotel strip.

33Playa de Castelldefels, Barcelona

Castelldefels, Barcelona, Catalonia · 4.2 / 5 · Easy access · about 23 °C

Castelldefels is one of the longest sandy beaches near Barcelona, with a wide strip of golden sand and a seafront of beach bars.

34Platja de Pineda de Mar

Pineda de Mar, Costa del Maresme · 4.1 / 5 · Easy access · about 22 °C

Pineda de Mar has a long beach of coarse golden sand, with green parkland and pine trees behind and a promenade that runs for kilometres.

35Platja de Portals Nous, Mallorca

Portals Nous, Calvia, Mallorca · 4.1 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Platja de Portals Nous is a small sandy beach on the south-west coast of Mallorca beside a large marina with luxury yachts, a short drive from Palma.

36Platja de Malgrat de Mar

Malgrat de Mar, Costa del Maresme · 4.0 / 5 · Easy access · about 22 °C

Malgrat de Mar has a long beach of coarse sand at the northern end of the Maresme coast, near the mouth of the Tordera river.

Frequently asked questions

Which are the top-rated beaches with free parking in Spain?

Our top three are Cala Macarelleta (4.8 out of 5), As Catedrais (4.7 out of 5), Cala d'Hort (4.7 out of 5). Scores are editorial ratings from the EuroShores team.

When is the best time to visit these beaches?

Most are at their best from June to September. Water temperatures average about 22 degrees C in season. Each beach page lists its exact season.

Which of these beaches are easiest to reach?

Cala d'Hort, Cala Galdana, Cala Santanyi, Platja de Cadaques, Playa de Amadores, Gran Canaria and 20 more have easy access. The others need a walk, stairs or a rougher road, so check the access note on each page.
