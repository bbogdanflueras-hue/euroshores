Easiest Beaches to Reach in Albania

We rank the 16 easy-access beaches in Albania. Palasa Beach, Albanian Riviera (Albania) comes first with 4.6 / 5, followed by Dhërmi, Ksamil Beaches.

The sea reaches about 25 degrees C in season, and most of these beaches are at their best from June to September. 16 of the 16 have easy access, and 7 are hidden gems.

Save on Pinterest

1Palasa Beach, Albanian Riviera

Palase, Himare, Vlore, Albania · 4.6 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

Palasa is a long beach of white pebbles at the foot of the Llogara mountains on the Albanian Riviera, facing the Ionian Sea.

2Dhërmi

Albanian Riviera, Albania · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

A beach of about 1 km of white and grey pebbles below the mountains, with bars and sunbeds at the south end and free open space elsewhere.

3Ksamil Beaches

Albanian Riviera, Albania · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Small islands and shallow turquoise coves on the Albanian Riviera, facing Corfu.

4Borsh

Albanian Riviera, Albania · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

About seven kilometres of pebble beach, often described as the longest in Albania, backed by olive groves and a hilltop castle.

5Drymades

Albanian Riviera, Albania · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

A long stretch of coast beside Dhërmi, now lined with beach clubs but with free open areas still available.

6Livadhi Beach

Himare, Albanian Riviera, Albania · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

A long pebble beach on the Albanian Riviera near Himare, with clear water and green mountains rising behind the shore.

7Porto Palermo Beach, Himara

Himara · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Porto Palermo is a sheltered bay on the Albanian Riviera near Himara, with a pebble beach and a small Ali Pasha castle on the peninsula.

8Qeparo

Albanian Riviera, Albania · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

A curved beach of pebble over sand with very clear water, backed by a stone promenade, with a semi-abandoned old village on the hill above.

9Potami Beach

Himara, Albania · 4.3 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

A long pebble curve about 2 km south of Himara town, with clear water fed by freshwater springs and a quieter feel than the town beach.

10Velipoja Beach

Shkoder, Albania · 4.3 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

A wide, gently shelving beach of dark sand on the northern coast of Albania, with mountains behind.

11Jonufer Beach

Vlora, Albanian Riviera · 4.1 / 5 · Easy access · about 23 °C

Jonufer is a quiet stretch of coast just north of Vlora, where the Adriatic meets the Ionian Sea.

12Lalzit Bay Beach, Albania

Lalzit Bay, Durres, Albania · 4.1 / 5 · Easy access · about 26 °C

Lalzit Bay is a long curve of sandy beach on the Adriatic coast north of Durres, with calm shallow water and a view of the hills behind the bay.

13Saranda Beach

Saranda, Albanian Riviera · 4.1 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

Saranda is a lively port town on the Ionian coast, and its waterfront pebble beach looks across the strait to Corfu.

14Shengjin Beach, Lezhe

Shengjin, Lezhe · 4.1 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Shengjin is a small port town on the Adriatic coast of northern Albania with a long sandy beach, a promenade and many cafes and hotels.

15Durres Beach

Durres, Albanian Adriatic · 4.0 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

Durres has a long beach of grey sand along the Adriatic coast, close to Albania largest port and the ruins of the Roman amphitheatre.

16Golem Beach, Albania

Golem, Kavaje, Durres, Albania · 4.0 / 5 · Easy access · about 26 °C

Golem is a wide beach of dark golden sand on the Adriatic coast of Albania between Durres and Kavaje, with shallow water and a long gentle slope into the sea.

Frequently asked questions

Which are the top-rated easy-access beaches in Albania?

Our top three are Palasa Beach, Albanian Riviera (4.6 out of 5), Dhërmi (4.5 out of 5), Ksamil Beaches (4.5 out of 5). Scores are editorial ratings from the EuroShores team.

When is the best time to visit these beaches?

Most are at their best from June to September. Water temperatures average about 25 degrees C in season. Each beach page lists its exact season.

Which of these beaches are easiest to reach?

Palasa Beach, Albanian Riviera, Dhërmi, Ksamil Beaches, Borsh, Drymades and 11 more have easy access. The others need a walk, stairs or a rougher road, so check the access note on each page.
