Best Family Beaches in the Lisbon coast

We rank the 16 family-friendly beaches in the Lisbon coast. Praia da Figueirinha, Setubal (Portugal) comes first with 4.6 / 5, followed by Praia Atlantica, Troia, Praia da Adraga.

The sea reaches about 20 degrees C in season, and most of these beaches are at their best from June to September. 16 of the 16 have easy access, and 6 are hidden gems.

Save on Pinterest

1Praia da Figueirinha, Setubal

Arrabida, Setubal · 4.6 / 5 · Easy access · about 22 °C

Praia da Figueirinha is a calm sand beach in the Arrabida natural park, with turquoise water and green hills behind.

2Praia Atlantica, Troia

Troia, Setubal · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 22 °C

Troia is a long sand peninsula south of Lisbon, with wide Atlantic beaches, white dunes and clear water.

3Praia da Adraga

Sintra-Cascais Natural Park, Portugal · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 17 °C

A golden Atlantic beach below eroded cliffs and rock arches on the Sintra coast, with strong surf and a beach restaurant.

4Praia da Costa da Caparica

Costa da Caparica, Lisbon · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 21 °C

Costa da Caparica is a long stretch of wide Atlantic beach just across the river from Lisbon, with fine sand, big waves and beach bars.

5Praia da Fonte da Telha, Costa da Caparica

Fonte da Telha, Almada, Setubal · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 20 °C

Praia da Fonte da Telha is a long and wide beach of golden sand at the southern end of the Costa da Caparica, backed by sandy cliffs and a protected landscape.

6Praia do Guincho

Cascais, Portugal · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 18 °C

A long, wind-blown Atlantic beach west of Cascais, backed by dunes and the Sintra hills and known as one of the best windsurfing and kitesurfing spots in Europe.

7Praia do Magoito, Sintra

Magoito, Sintra, Lisbon Coast · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 19 °C

Praia do Magoito is a small sandy cove between golden sandstone cliffs on the Sintra coast, west of Lisbon.

8Praia da Conceicao, Cascais

Cascais, Lisbon · 4.3 / 5 · Easy access · about 19 °C

Praia da Conceicao is a small, sheltered sand beach in the centre of Cascais, with a promenade and an old chapel nearby.

9Praia da Lagoa de Albufeira, Sesimbra

Lagoa de Albufeira, Sesimbra, Setubal · 4.3 / 5 · Easy access · about 20 °C

Praia da Lagoa de Albufeira is a wide beach on the Atlantic coast south of Lisbon, beside a lagoon that opens to the sea in winter.

10Praia de Carcavelos

Carcavelos, Cascais, Lisbon coast · 4.3 / 5 · Easy access · about 19 °C

A wide sandy beach on the Lisbon-Cascais coast, backed by a promenade.

11Praia de Santa Rita, Torres Vedras

Santa Rita, Torres Vedras, Lisbon Region · 4.3 / 5 · Easy access · about 19 °C

Praia de Santa Rita is a wide beach of golden sand on the Silver Coast, below low cliffs and fields near Torres Vedras.

12Praia de Sesimbra

Sesimbra, Setubal, Lisbon coast · 4.3 / 5 · Easy access · about 22 °C

Sesimbra is a fishing town south of Lisbon, with a wide golden beach in a sheltered bay and a Moorish castle on the hill behind.

13Praia do Algodio, Ericeira

Ericeira, Mafra, Lisbon coast · 4.3 / 5 · Easy access · about 18 °C

Praia do Algodio is a small sand and rock beach on the coast of Ericeira, a white surf village north-west of Lisbon.

14Praia do Meco

Sesimbra, Lisbon coast · 4.3 / 5 · Easy access · about 20 °C

A long, open Atlantic beach on the coast south of Lisbon, backed by low cliffs, with golden sand, strong surf and a relaxed local feel.

15Praia do Tamariz, Estoril

Estoril, Cascais, Lisbon coast · 4.2 / 5 · Easy access · about 19 °C

Praia do Tamariz is the main beach of Estoril on the Lisbon coast, a curve of golden sand sheltered by a breakwater, with the casino gardens and a seafront promenade behind.

16Praia de Sao Pedro do Estoril

Sao Pedro do Estoril, Cascais, Lisbon Region · 4.1 / 5 · Easy access · about 19 °C

Praia de Sao Pedro do Estoril is a small beach of sand and rocks on the coast between Lisbon and Cascais, beside the seaside railway.

Frequently asked questions

Which are the top-rated family-friendly beaches in the Lisbon coast?

Our top three are Praia da Figueirinha, Setubal (4.6 out of 5), Praia Atlantica, Troia (4.5 out of 5), Praia da Adraga (4.5 out of 5). Scores are editorial ratings from the EuroShores team.

When is the best time to visit these beaches?

Most are at their best from June to September. Water temperatures average about 20 degrees C in season. Each beach page lists its exact season.

Which of these beaches are easiest to reach?

Praia da Figueirinha, Setubal, Praia Atlantica, Troia, Praia da Adraga, Praia da Costa da Caparica, Praia da Fonte da Telha, Costa da Caparica and 11 more have easy access. The others need a walk, stairs or a rougher road, so check the access note on each page.
