Best Pebble Beaches in Turkey

We rank the 15 pebble beaches in Turkey. Oludeniz Beach, Fethiye (Turkey) comes first with 4.6 / 5, followed by Butterfly Valley Beach, Faralya, Cirali Beach, Kemer.

The sea reaches about 25 degrees C in season, and most of these beaches are at their best from May to October. 12 of the 15 have easy access, and 7 are hidden gems.

1Oludeniz Beach, Fethiye

Oludeniz, Fethiye, Turkey · 4.6 / 5 · Easy access · about 26 °C

Oludeniz is a famous turquoise lagoon and beach near Fethiye, backed by pine-covered mountains.

2Butterfly Valley Beach, Faralya

Butterfly Valley, Faralya, Fethiye, Turkey · 4.5 / 5 · Challenging access · about 26 °C

Butterfly Valley is a hidden cove at the foot of steep cliffs and a forested canyon near Faralya.

3Cirali Beach, Kemer

Cirali, Kemer, Antalya, Turkey · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 26 °C

Cirali is a long pebble beach in a small protected village near the ancient city of Olympos.

4Kabak Bay, Fethiye

Kabak, Fethiye, Mugla, Turkey · 4.5 / 5 · Challenging access · about 26 °C

Kabak is a quiet bay at the bottom of a forested valley near Faralya, on the Lycian Way walking route.

5Phaselis Bay, Kemer

Phaselis, Kemer, Antalya, Turkey · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Phaselis is a ruined ancient harbour city on a pine-covered peninsula near Kemer, with three small bays beside the ruins.

6Adrasan Beach, Kumluca

Adrasan, Kumluca, Antalya, Turkey · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Adrasan is a calm bay in a green valley on the Lycian coast, with a pebble beach and a few small pensions and restaurants.

7Akyaka Beach, Mugla

Akyaka, Mugla, Turkey · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Akyaka is a village at the head of Gokova Bay, known for traditional wooden houses and a cold river that meets the sea.

8Assos Beach, Behramkale

Assos, Behramkale, Canakkale, Turkey · 4.4 / 5 · Moderate access · about 25 °C

Assos is a stone village on a hill above the northern Aegean, with the ruins of a Temple of Athena at the top and a small harbour below.

9Konyaalti Beach, Antalya

Konyaalti, Antalya, Turkey · 4.3 / 5 · Easy access · about 26 °C

Konyaalti is a long pebble beach on the western side of Antalya, with the Taurus Mountains rising behind the sea.

10Turunc Beach, Marmaris

Turunc, Marmaris, Mugla, Turkey · 4.3 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Turunc is a small village on a green bay south-west of Marmaris, with a pebble and sand beach and hills rising behind the seafront.

11Beldibi Beach, Kemer

Beldibi, Kemer, Antalya, Turkey · 4.2 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Beldibi is a pebble beach between Antalya and Kemer, with the Taurus Mountains rising steeply behind the coast.

12Datca Beach, Mugla

Datca, Mugla, Turkey · 4.2 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Datca is a laid-back town at the western end of a long peninsula between the Aegean and the Mediterranean.

13Kemer Beach, Antalya

Kemer, Antalya, Turkey · 4.2 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Kemer Beach is a long pebble beach beside the resort town of Kemer, with the Taurus Mountains rising behind the coast.

14Calis Beach, Fethiye

Calis, Fethiye, Mugla, Turkey · 4.1 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Calis Beach is a long stretch of coarse sand and pebbles beside Fethiye with a seafront promenade.

15Marmaris Beach, Mugla

Marmaris, Mugla, Turkey · 4.1 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Marmaris Beach lies along the sheltered bay at Marmaris, with a long seafront of sand and pebbles.

Frequently asked questions

Which are the top-rated pebble beaches in Turkey?

Our top three are Oludeniz Beach, Fethiye (4.6 out of 5), Butterfly Valley Beach, Faralya (4.5 out of 5), Cirali Beach, Kemer (4.5 out of 5). Scores are editorial ratings from the EuroShores team.

When is the best time to visit these beaches?

Most are at their best from May to October. Water temperatures average about 25 degrees C in season. Each beach page lists its exact season.

Which of these beaches are easiest to reach?

Oludeniz Beach, Fethiye, Cirali Beach, Kemer, Phaselis Bay, Kemer, Adrasan Beach, Kumluca, Akyaka Beach, Mugla and 7 more have easy access. The others need a walk, stairs or a rougher road, so check the access note on each page.
