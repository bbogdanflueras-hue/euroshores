Best Beaches in Sicily in June

We rank the 15 beaches to visit in Sicily in June. Spiaggia dei Conigli (Italy) comes first with 4.8 / 5, followed by Cala Azzurra, Favignana, Calamosche Beach.

The sea reaches about 25 degrees C in season, and most of these beaches are at their best from June to September. 10 of the 15 have easy access, and 7 are hidden gems.

1Spiaggia dei Conigli

Lampedusa, Sicily, Italy · 4.8 / 5 · Moderate access · about 25 °C

A sweep of pale sand and shallow turquoise water on Lampedusa, set inside a nature reserve and often counted among the most beautiful beaches in the Mediterranean.

2Cala Azzurra, Favignana

Favignana, Egadi Islands, Sicily · 4.6 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Cala Azzurra is a small cove on the island of Favignana in the Egadi archipelago off west Sicily, with a narrow sand and pebble beach, pale rock and very clear turquoise water.

3Calamosche Beach

Vendicari, Noto, Sicily · 4.6 / 5 · Moderate access · about 24 °C

A small sandy cove between rocky headlands inside the Vendicari nature reserve on the south-east coast of Sicily.

4San Vito Lo Capo

San Vito Lo Capo, Sicily, Italy · 4.6 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

A broad curve of pale sand and clear shallow water at the tip of north-west Sicily, backed by the town and a rocky headland.

5Scala dei Turchi

Realmonte, Sicily, Italy · 4.6 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

A dazzling white marl cliff carved into giant steps above a small sandy beach on the south coast of Sicily, near Agrigento.

6Tonnara di Scopello

Scopello, Castellammare del Golfo, Sicily · 4.6 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Tonnara di Scopello is a small cove on the north-west coast of Sicily, with an old tuna fishery building, tall sea stacks called faraglioni just offshore and very clear water.

7Spiaggia di Vendicari

Vendicari Reserve, Noto, Sicily · 4.5 / 5 · Moderate access · about 26 °C

Vendicari is a nature reserve on the south-east coast of Sicily, with several long beaches of pale sand, salt marshes that attract flamingos in spring and autumn, and an old tuna fishery.

8Cala Rossa

Favignana, Egadi Islands, Sicily · 4.4 / 5 · Moderate access · about 25 °C

A rocky cove on the island of Favignana in the Egadi Islands, with pale tufa cliffs and clear turquoise water.

9Isola Bella

Taormina, Sicily · 4.4 / 5 · Moderate access · about 25 °C

A small island and pebble bay below Taormina, linked to the shore by a narrow strip.

10Spiaggia di Fontane Bianche

Fontane Bianche, Siracusa, Sicily · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Fontane Bianche is a seaside village south of Syracuse, with a long beach of soft pale sand and clear turquoise water.

11Cefalu Beach

Cefalu, Sicily, Italy · 4.3 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

A sandy beach right below the old houses of Cefalu, with rocks at the edges and the town and cliffs behind.

12Mazzaro Beach, Taormina

Mazzaro, Taormina, Sicily · 4.3 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Mazzaro is a pebble bay on the Ionian coast of Sicily, below the hill town of Taormina and connected to it by a cable car.

13Mondello Beach

Mondello, Palermo, Sicily, Italy · 4.3 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

A long curve of pale sand with shallow turquoise water, set between two headlands with a mountain behind the bay.

14Punta Secca Beach, Sicily

Punta Secca, Santa Croce Camerina, Sicily · 4.3 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Punta Secca is a small fishing village on the south coast of Sicily, with a sandy beach beside whitewashed houses and a lighthouse.

15Spiaggia di Marzamemi

Marzamemi, Sicily · 4.3 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

Marzamemi is an old tuna-fishing village on the south-east tip of Sicily, and the beaches around it are sandy and shallow, with very clear water.

Frequently asked questions

Which are the top-rated beaches to visit in Sicily in June?

Our top three are Spiaggia dei Conigli (4.8 out of 5), Cala Azzurra, Favignana (4.6 out of 5), Calamosche Beach (4.6 out of 5). Scores are editorial ratings from the EuroShores team.

When is the best time to visit these beaches?

Most are at their best from June to September. Water temperatures average about 25 degrees C in season. Each beach page lists its exact season.

Which of these beaches are easiest to reach?

Cala Azzurra, Favignana, San Vito Lo Capo, Scala dei Turchi, Tonnara di Scopello, Spiaggia di Fontane Bianche and 5 more have easy access. The others need a walk, stairs or a rougher road, so check the access note on each page.
