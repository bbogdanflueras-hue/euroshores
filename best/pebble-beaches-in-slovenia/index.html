Best Pebble Beaches in Slovenia

We rank the 4 pebble beaches in Slovenia. Moon Bay (Mesecev Zaliv), Strunjan (Slovenia) comes first with 4.4 / 5, followed by Debeli Rtic Beach, Fiesa Beach.

The sea reaches about 23 degrees C in season, and most of these beaches are at their best from June to September. 1 of the 4 have easy access, and 3 are hidden gems.

Save on Pinterest

1Moon Bay (Mesecev Zaliv), Strunjan

Strunjan Landscape Park, Piran, Slovenia · 4.4 / 5 · Moderate access · about 23 °C

Moon Bay is a crescent-shaped pebble cove below the Strunjan cliff, the highest on the Slovenian coast, inside Strunjan Landscape Park.

2Debeli Rtic Beach

Debeli Rtic, Ankaran, Slovenia · 4.2 / 5 · Moderate access · about 22 °C

Debeli rtic is a protected headland at the north-western tip of the Slovenian coast, near the Italian border, with low cliffs, woodland and small pebble coves.

3Fiesa Beach

Fiesa, Piran, Slovenia · 4.2 / 5 · Moderate access · about 23 °C

Fiesa is a small pebble bay below green slopes between Piran and Strunjan, next to two small lakes that are a protected natural area.

4Simonov Zaliv Beach, Izola

Izola, Slovenia · 4.1 / 5 · Easy access · about 23 °C

Simonov zaliv is a bay just west of Izola with a grassy lawn, a pebble and concrete shore and clear water, close to the remains of the Roman port of Haliaetum.

Frequently asked questions

Which are the top-rated pebble beaches in Slovenia?

Our top three are Moon Bay (Mesecev Zaliv), Strunjan (4.4 out of 5), Debeli Rtic Beach (4.2 out of 5), Fiesa Beach (4.2 out of 5). Scores are editorial ratings from the EuroShores team.

When is the best time to visit these beaches?

Most are at their best from June to September. Water temperatures average about 23 degrees C in season. Each beach page lists its exact season.

Which of these beaches are easiest to reach?

Simonov Zaliv Beach, Izola have easy access. The others need a walk, stairs or a rougher road, so check the access note on each page.
