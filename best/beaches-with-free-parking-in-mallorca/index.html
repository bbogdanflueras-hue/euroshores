Best Beaches with Free Parking in Mallorca

We rank the 7 beaches with free parking in Mallorca. Cala Santanyi (Spain) comes first with 4.6 / 5, followed by Calo des Moro, Cala Llombards.

The sea reaches about 24 degrees C in season, and most of these beaches are at their best from May to October. 5 of the 7 have easy access.

Save on Pinterest

1Cala Santanyi

Santanyi, Mallorca · 4.6 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

A small sandy cove tucked between pine-covered cliffs on the south-east coast of Mallorca, with calm turquoise water.

2Calo des Moro

Santanyi, Mallorca · 4.6 / 5 · Challenging access · about 24 °C

A very small sandy cove squeezed between rocky walls on Mallorca south-east coast, known for its turquoise water.

3Cala Llombards

Santanyi, Mallorca · 4.5 / 5 · Moderate access · about 24 °C

A small sandy cove on the south-east coast of Mallorca, set between low rocky cliffs and pine trees with clear shallow water.

4Platja de Muro

Muro, Mallorca · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

A long beach of fine sand in the Bay of Alcudia, backed by dunes and an old watchtower.

5Playa de Santa Ponsa, Mallorca

Santa Ponsa, Calvia, Mallorca · 4.3 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Santa Ponsa is a sheltered bay on the south-west coast of Mallorca with a wide beach of fine sand and calm, shallow water.

6Sant Elm Beach

Sant Elm, Andratx, Mallorca · 4.3 / 5 · Easy access · about 23 °C

A small sandy bay in the village of Sant Elm at the south-west tip of Mallorca, with the island of Sa Dragonera offshore.

7Platja de Portals Nous, Mallorca

Portals Nous, Calvia, Mallorca · 4.1 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Platja de Portals Nous is a small sandy beach on the south-west coast of Mallorca beside a large marina with luxury yachts, a short drive from Palma.

Frequently asked questions

Which are the top-rated beaches with free parking in Mallorca?

Our top three are Cala Santanyi (4.6 out of 5), Calo des Moro (4.6 out of 5), Cala Llombards (4.5 out of 5). Scores are editorial ratings from the EuroShores team.

When is the best time to visit these beaches?

Most are at their best from May to October. Water temperatures average about 24 degrees C in season. Each beach page lists its exact season.

Which of these beaches are easiest to reach?

Cala Santanyi, Platja de Muro, Playa de Santa Ponsa, Mallorca, Sant Elm Beach, Platja de Portals Nous, Mallorca have easy access. The others need a walk, stairs or a rougher road, so check the access note on each page.
