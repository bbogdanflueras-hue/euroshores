Easiest Beaches to Reach in France

We rank the 33 easy-access beaches in France. Palombaggia (France) comes first with 4.8 / 5, followed by Rondinara Beach, Santa Giulia.

The sea reaches about 21 degrees C in season, and most of these beaches are at their best from June to September. 33 of the 33 have easy access, and 2 are hidden gems.

1Palombaggia

Corsica, France · 4.8 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

Red granite boulders, parasol pines and soft pale sand — Corsica's most photographed shoreline.

2Rondinara Beach

Bonifacio, Corsica, France · 4.7 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

A horseshoe-shaped bay of pale sand and shallow, turquoise water on the south-east coast of Corsica, backed by low scrub and quiet hills.

3Santa Giulia

Porto-Vecchio, Corsica, France · 4.6 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

A long white-sand beach on a sheltered bay with shallow, calm water, south of Porto-Vecchio.

4Gigaro Beach

La Croix-Valmer, Var, France · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 22 °C

A long sandy beach on the Var coast near Cap Lardier, backed by pine woods and protected coastline.

5Pampelonne Beach

Ramatuelle, Provence, France · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 23 °C

A long arc of fine sand, about 4.

6Pointe de la Torche

Plomeur, Finistere, Brittany · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 17 °C

A wide sandy beach beside the Pointe de la Torche in south Finistere, one of the best-known surf spots in Brittany.

7Aregno Beach

Balagne, Corsica · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 23 °C

A long sandy beach on the Balagne coast of north-west Corsica, with clear water and mountains in the background.

8Etretat Beach

Etretat, Normandy, France · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 17 °C

A steep pebble beach beneath the famous white chalk cliffs and arches of the Normandy coast.

9La Baule Beach

La Baule-Escoublac, Pays de la Loire · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 19 °C

A sweeping semicircular bay of fine pale sand, about 9 km long between Pornichet and Le Pouliguen.

10Lacanau Ocean Beach

Lacanau, Gironde · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 19 °C

A broad sandy Atlantic beach backed by dunes and pines, with strong waves that have made Lacanau one of France best-known surf spots.

11Lozari Beach

Belgodere, Corsica · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 23 °C

A sandy beach on the Balagne coast of Corsica near Belgodere, with clear water and views of the coastal hills.

12Plage de Deauville

Deauville, Normandy · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 17 °C

The beach of Deauville is a broad stretch of fine sand with a famous wooden boardwalk, Les Planches, and rows of colourful parasols.

13Plage de la Garoupe

Antibes, French Riviera · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 22 °C

Plage de la Garoupe is a sheltered crescent of sand on the cape of Antibes, with calm clear water and a green backdrop of pines.

14Plage de Piemanson

Salin-de-Giraud, Camargue · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 23 °C

A long, wild sandy beach on the Camargue coast near the mouth of the Rhone, with dunes and few buildings.

15Plage de Saint-Jean-de-Luz

Saint-Jean-de-Luz, Basque Coast · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 19 °C

Saint-Jean-de-Luz has a calm, curved bay of fine sand protected by sea walls, with a lively promenade and Basque houses behind.

16Plage de Tahiti

Ramatuelle, Var, Provence · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 23 °C

The northern end of the long Pampelonne bay near Ramatuelle on the French Riviera, with fine sand and a line of beach restaurants.

17Arromanches Gold Beach

Arromanches-les-Bains, Normandy · 4.3 / 5 · Easy access · about 18 °C

A broad sandy beach below chalk cliffs, known as Gold Beach in the 1944 landings.

18Biarritz Grande Plage

Biarritz, Basque Coast, France · 4.3 / 5 · Easy access · about 19 °C

A wide Atlantic sand beach in the centre of Biarritz, with the town and promenade behind and cliffs and rocks along the coast.

19Bidart Beach

Bidart, Basque Coast, Nouvelle-Aquitaine · 4.3 / 5 · Easy access · about 20 °C

The central beach of Bidart, one of the village beaches on the Basque coast between Biarritz and Saint-Jean-de-Luz.

20Calvi Beach

Calvi, Corsica, France · 4.3 / 5 · Easy access · about 22 °C

A long sandy beach on the Calvi bay in northwest Corsica, with a view across the water to the mountains.

21Dinard Beach

Dinard, Brittany · 4.3 / 5 · Easy access · about 18 °C

The main beach of Dinard is a long sandy bay below Belle Epoque villas on the Emerald Coast of Brittany.

22Grande Plage de Quiberon

Quiberon, Brittany · 4.3 / 5 · Easy access · about 17 °C

The Grande Plage of Quiberon is a sheltered curve of sand facing the bay, with calm water and a seafront lined with cafes.

23Hossegor Lake Beach

Hossegor, Landes · 4.3 / 5 · Easy access · about 21 °C

A calm sandy beach on the lake of Hossegor, ringed by pines and villas, offering a gentler alternative to the surf on the nearby ocean beaches.

24Plage de Cavalaire

Cavalaire-sur-Mer, Var · 4.3 / 5 · Easy access · about 21 °C

The main beach of Cavalaire-sur-Mer is a long arc of fine sand with a gentle slope into the water.

25Plage de la Grande Conche

Royan, Charente-Maritime · 4.3 / 5 · Easy access · about 18 °C

The Grande Conche is the main beach of Royan, a wide curve of fine sand backed by a promenade and 1950s architecture.

26Plage de Sainte-Maxime

Sainte-Maxime, Var · 4.3 / 5 · Easy access · about 22 °C

The main beach of Sainte-Maxime is a long stretch of fine sand on the gulf of Saint-Tropez, with calm shallow water and a view across the bay to the old town of Saint-Tropez.

27Plage du Bon-Secours, Saint-Malo

Saint-Malo, Brittany · 4.3 / 5 · Easy access · about 18 °C

Plage du Bon-Secours lies just below the granite walls of Saint-Malo, with a famous tidal swimming pool built on the rocks.

28Omaha Beach

Saint-Laurent-sur-Mer, Normandy · 4.2 / 5 · Easy access · about 17 °C

A long, flat, sandy shoreline in Normandy, best known as one of the D-Day landing beaches of 1944.

29Plage de la Croisette

Cannes, French Riviera · 4.2 / 5 · Easy access · about 23 °C

The sandy beaches along the famous Boulevard de la Croisette in Cannes, a mix of private beach clubs and free public sections.

30Plage de la Salis

Antibes, French Riviera · 4.2 / 5 · Easy access · about 23 °C

A sheltered sandy beach at Antibes with calm, shallow water that suits families, and views across the bay.

31Plage des Sablettes, Menton

Menton, French Riviera · 4.2 / 5 · Easy access · about 22 °C

Plage des Sablettes is the favourite beach of Menton, a short stretch of grey pebbles with calm water and the pastel houses of the old town rising behind.

32Plage de la Gravette

Antibes, French Riviera · 4.1 / 5 · Easy access · about 23 °C

A small sandy beach right beside the old town and port of Antibes, with calm shallow water and a walk to the ramparts.

33Plage des Catalans

Marseille, Provence · 4.1 / 5 · Easy access · about 21 °C

Plage des Catalans is the closest sandy beach to the Old Port of Marseille, below the Pharo gardens.

Frequently asked questions

Which are the top-rated easy-access beaches in France?

Our top three are Palombaggia (4.8 out of 5), Rondinara Beach (4.7 out of 5), Santa Giulia (4.6 out of 5). Scores are editorial ratings from the EuroShores team.

When is the best time to visit these beaches?

Most are at their best from June to September. Water temperatures average about 21 degrees C in season. Each beach page lists its exact season.

Which of these beaches are easiest to reach?

Palombaggia, Rondinara Beach, Santa Giulia, Gigaro Beach, Pampelonne Beach and 28 more have easy access. The others need a walk, stairs or a rougher road, so check the access note on each page.
