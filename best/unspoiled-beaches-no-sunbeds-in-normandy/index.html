Unspoiled Beaches Without Sunbeds in Normandy

We rank the 4 unspoiled beaches without sunbeds in Normandy. Etretat Beach (France) comes first with 4.4 / 5, followed by Arromanches Gold Beach, Omaha Beach.

The sea reaches about 18 degrees C in season, and most of these beaches are at their best from June to September. 4 of the 4 have easy access.

Save on Pinterest

1Etretat Beach

Etretat, Normandy, France · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 17 °C

A steep pebble beach beneath the famous white chalk cliffs and arches of the Normandy coast.

2Arromanches Gold Beach

Arromanches-les-Bains, Normandy · 4.3 / 5 · Easy access · about 18 °C

A broad sandy beach below chalk cliffs, known as Gold Beach in the 1944 landings.

3Omaha Beach

Saint-Laurent-sur-Mer, Normandy · 4.2 / 5 · Easy access · about 17 °C

A long, flat, sandy shoreline in Normandy, best known as one of the D-Day landing beaches of 1944.

4Utah Beach, Normandy

Sainte-Marie-du-Mont, Normandy · 4.2 / 5 · Easy access · about 18 °C

Utah Beach is a wide, flat beach on the east coast of the Cotentin peninsula in Normandy, one of the D-Day landing beaches.

Frequently asked questions

Which are the top-rated unspoiled beaches without sunbeds in Normandy?

Our top three are Etretat Beach (4.4 out of 5), Arromanches Gold Beach (4.3 out of 5), Omaha Beach (4.2 out of 5). Scores are editorial ratings from the EuroShores team.

When is the best time to visit these beaches?

Most are at their best from June to September. Water temperatures average about 18 degrees C in season. Each beach page lists its exact season.

Which of these beaches are easiest to reach?

Etretat Beach, Arromanches Gold Beach, Omaha Beach, Utah Beach, Normandy have easy access. The others need a walk, stairs or a rougher road, so check the access note on each page.
