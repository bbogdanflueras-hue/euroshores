Best Beaches with Sunbeds in Puglia

We rank the 12 beaches with sunbeds and umbrellas in Puglia. Vignanotica Beach, Gargano (Italy) comes first with 4.7 / 5, followed by Torre dell'Orso Beach, Baia dei Turchi.

The sea reaches about 25 degrees C in season, and most of these beaches are at their best from June to September. 10 of the 12 have easy access, and 2 are hidden gems.

Save on Pinterest

1Vignanotica Beach, Gargano

Vignanotica, Vieste, Puglia · 4.7 / 5 · Moderate access · about 25 °C

Vignanotica is a cove of white pebbles at the foot of tall white limestone cliffs on the Gargano coast, south of Vieste.

2Torre dell'Orso Beach

Melendugno, Puglia · 4.6 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

A wide crescent of pale sand and shallow turquoise water in Salento, guarded by a watchtower and the famous Two Sisters rock stacks.

3Baia dei Turchi

Otranto, Puglia · 4.5 / 5 · Moderate access · about 25 °C

A natural bay in Salento, a few kilometres north of Otranto, with fine pale sand, pines and clear shallow water.

4Pescoluse Beach, Salento

Pescoluse, Salve, Puglia · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Pescoluse is a long beach of fine white sand on the Ionian coast of Salento, often called the Maldives of Salento for its shallow, clear water and pale dunes.

5Pizzomunno Beach

Vieste, Puglia · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

The beach of Pizzomunno is a curve of pale sand in Vieste, dominated by a white limestone stack that gives it its name.

6Punta Prosciutto

Porto Cesareo, Salento, Puglia · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

A long sandy beach on the Ionian coast of Salento, backed by dunes and part of a protected area within the Porto Cesareo marine reserve.

7Alimini Beach

Alimini, Otranto, Puglia · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

A stretch of fine sand and clear, gently sloping water on the Salento coast near Otranto, backed by pine woods and dunes.

8San Pietro in Bevagna Beach

Manduria, Puglia · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

A long sandy beach on the Ionian coast of Puglia, near Manduria, with a wide open shoreline and gently shelving water.

9Spiaggia della Purita

Gallipoli, Puglia · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Spiaggia della Purita is a small sandy beach just outside the old town of Gallipoli on the Ionian coast.

10Spiaggia di Peschici

Peschici, Puglia · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

Peschici is a whitewashed hill village on the Gargano coast, and its beach is a long stretch of fine sand below the old town.

11Piana di Mattinata Beach, Puglia

Mattinata, Foggia, Gargano · 4.3 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Piana di Mattinata is a long beach of sand and fine pebbles below the town of Mattinata on the Gargano coast of Puglia.

12Baia Verde, Gallipoli

Baia Verde, Gallipoli, Puglia · 4.2 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Baia Verde is the beach strip south of the old town of Gallipoli in Puglia, with pale sand, patches of rock and shallow clear water.

Frequently asked questions

Which are the top-rated beaches with sunbeds and umbrellas in Puglia?

Our top three are Vignanotica Beach, Gargano (4.7 out of 5), Torre dell'Orso Beach (4.6 out of 5), Baia dei Turchi (4.5 out of 5). Scores are editorial ratings from the EuroShores team.

When is the best time to visit these beaches?

Most are at their best from June to September. Water temperatures average about 25 degrees C in season. Each beach page lists its exact season.

Which of these beaches are easiest to reach?

Torre dell'Orso Beach, Pescoluse Beach, Salento, Pizzomunno Beach, Punta Prosciutto, Alimini Beach and 5 more have easy access. The others need a walk, stairs or a rougher road, so check the access note on each page.
