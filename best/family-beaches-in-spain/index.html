Best Family Beaches in Spain

We rank the top 40 of 118 family-friendly beaches in Spain. Ses Illetes (Spain) comes first with 4.8 / 5, followed by Es Trenc, Playa de Bolonia.

The sea reaches about 22 degrees C in season, and most of these beaches are at their best from June to September. 40 of the 40 have easy access, and 12 are hidden gems.

Save on Pinterest

1Ses Illetes

Formentera, Spain · 4.8 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

A long strip of white sand and shallow, almost Caribbean-coloured water on the northern tip of Formentera.

2Es Trenc

Mallorca, Spain · 4.7 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

Mallorca's finest untouched beach — miles of pristine white sand, turquoise shallow water, and no high-rise hotels in sight.

3Playa de Bolonia

Tarifa, Andalusia, Spain · 4.7 / 5 · Easy access · about 20 °C

A wide, wind-swept Atlantic bay on the Costa de la Luz with a huge sand dune at one end and the Roman ruins of Baelo Claudia just behind the beach.

4Cala Galdana

Cala Galdana, Ferreries, Menorca · 4.6 / 5 · Easy access · about 23 °C

A half-crescent cove of white sand and calm, shallow water on the south coast of Menorca, ringed by pine-covered cliffs.

5Cala Santanyi

Santanyi, Mallorca · 4.6 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

A small sandy cove tucked between pine-covered cliffs on the south-east coast of Mallorca, with calm turquoise water.

6Cala Saona

Formentera, Balearic Islands, Spain · 4.6 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

A sheltered sandy cove on the west coast of Formentera with clear shallow water and traditional boat houses on the rocks.

7Formentor Beach

Pollensa, Mallorca · 4.6 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

A sheltered bay of pale sand and shallow turquoise water at the foot of the Formentor peninsula in northern Mallorca, backed by pine trees.

8Los Genoveses Beach

Cabo de Gata, Almeria, Spain · 4.6 / 5 · Easy access · about 22 °C

A broad, undeveloped sandy bay inside the Cabo de Gata-Nijar natural park, backed by dunes and low hills.

9Playa de Barayo, Valdes

Barayo, Valdes, Asturias · 4.6 / 5 · Easy access · about 19 °C

Playa de Barayo is a wide beach of fine sand on the western coast of Asturias, with a river mouth, dunes and wetlands behind it.

10Playa de la Concha

San Sebastian, Basque Country, Spain · 4.6 / 5 · Easy access · about 20 °C

A sheltered, shell-shaped city beach with fine golden sand, a long promenade and a small island in the bay, a short walk from the old town of San Sebastian.

11Playa de Liencres, Cantabria

Liencres, Pielagos, Cantabria · 4.6 / 5 · Easy access · about 19 °C

Playa de Liencres is a long beach of golden sand near Santander, backed by one of the best preserved dune systems on the Cantabrian coast and a pine forest.

12Playa de Trafalgar

Los Canos de Meca, Cadiz · 4.6 / 5 · Easy access · about 21 °C

Playa de Trafalgar is a long beach of golden sand and rocks near Los Canos de Meca, with the Cape Trafalgar lighthouse on the headland.

13Playa de Valdevaqueros

Tarifa, Cadiz, Andalusia · 4.6 / 5 · Easy access · about 22 °C

A long open beach west of Tarifa, backed by the large Punta Paloma dune.

14Sa Tuna Beach, Begur

Sa Tuna, Begur, Girona · 4.6 / 5 · Easy access · about 22 °C

Sa Tuna is a tiny cove near Begur on the Costa Brava, with a small beach of coarse sand and a row of whitewashed fishermen houses along the shore.

15Cala Comte

Sant Josep, Ibiza, Spain · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

Small sandy coves with very clear water and low islets offshore, on the west coast of Ibiza.

16Cala en Porter, Menorca

Cala en Porter, Menorca · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

Cala en Porter is a cove of fine white sand in a narrow ravine on the south coast of Menorca, with turquoise water and tall cliffs on both sides.

17Cala Mesquida

Capdepera, Mallorca · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

A wide sandy bay in the north-east of Mallorca, backed by dunes and low hills.

18Cala Mondragó

Mallorca, Spain · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

A sheltered turquoise cove backed by pines inside a natural park, with the sandy bay of S'Amarador alongside.

19El Cotillo Beach

El Cotillo, Fuerteventura · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 22 °C

A wide sandy beach near the fishing village of El Cotillo on the north-west coast of Fuerteventura, known for clear turquoise water, steady wind and windsurfers.

20Los Canos de Meca Beach

Los Canos de Meca, Barbate, Cadiz · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

Los Canos de Meca is a laid-back beach village in Barbate, Cadiz, with sandy beaches backed by cliffs and pine woods.

21Platja de Calella de Palafrugell

Calella de Palafrugell, Costa Brava · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 22 °C

Calella de Palafrugell is a charming fishing village with small sand and pebble coves between rocks, backed by white arcades.

22Platja de Llafranc

Llafranc, Palafrugell, Costa Brava · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

Llafranc is a small former fishing village on the Costa Brava, with a crescent of coarse golden sand in a sheltered bay, pine-covered hills behind and a lighthouse above.

23Platja Gran, Tossa de Mar

Tossa de Mar, Costa Brava · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 22 °C

Platja Gran is the main beach of Tossa de Mar, a crescent of golden sand overlooked by the medieval walls and towers of the Vila Vella.

24Playa de Benijo, Tenerife

Benijo, Anaga, Tenerife · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 22 °C

Playa de Benijo is a beach of dark sand and rocks below the Anaga mountains on the north-east coast of Tenerife, with sea stacks called the Roques de Anaga rising from the water.

25Playa de Berria

Santona, Cantabria · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 20 °C

A broad, golden beach of about 2 km with dunes, a nature reserve behind it and views to the Monte Buciero cliffs.

26Playa de Carnota

Carnota, A Coruna, Galicia · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 20 °C

Playa de Carnota is one of the longest beaches in Galicia, a wide stretch of fine pale sand on the Costa da Morte, with the granite slopes of Monte Pindo rising behind.

27Playa de Famara

Caleta de Famara, Teguise, Lanzarote · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 22 °C

A long, wide sandy beach on the north-west coast of Lanzarote, framed by the dramatic Famara cliffs and popular with surfers and kitesurfers.

28Playa de la Ballota, Llanes

Ballota, Llanes, Asturias · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 19 °C

Playa de la Ballota is a wild beach of sand and small stones near Llanes in Asturias, reached by a steep path through meadows and cliffs.

29Playa de la Barrosa

Chiclana de la Frontera, Andalusia · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 21 °C

An 8 km sweep of fine golden sand on the Costa de la Luz, backed by pine woods and a low red cliff at the north end.

30Playa de la Fontanilla

Conil de la Frontera, Cadiz · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 21 °C

La Fontanilla is the main beach of Conil de la Frontera, a wide sweep of fine golden sand with shallow Atlantic water and a promenade.

31Playa de las Canteras

Las Palmas, Gran Canaria · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 22 °C

A long, sheltered city beach in Las Palmas, protected by a natural offshore reef that keeps the water calm for most of the year.

32Playa de las Teresitas

San Andres, Tenerife, Canary Islands, Spain · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 21 °C

A long curved golden beach on the northeast coast of Tenerife, with the Anaga Mountains behind and a breakwater that calms the water.

33Playa de Ondarreta

Ondarreta, San Sebastian, Basque Country · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 22 °C

Ondarreta is the quieter of the two city beaches of San Sebastian, set at the western end of the bay below Monte Igueldo.

34Playa de Ris

Noja, Cantabria · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 20 °C

A broad golden beach in Noja, Cantabria, with a long gentle shore, good swimming and dunes and green hills behind.

35Playa de Sancti Petri, Chiclana

Sancti Petri, Chiclana de la Frontera, Cadiz · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 22 °C

Playa de Sancti Petri is a long beach of fine golden sand on the Costa de la Luz, facing a small islet with the ruins of a castle.

36Playa de Zahara de los Atunes

Zahara de los Atunes, Costa de la Luz · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 22 °C

A long, wide beach of fine pale sand on Spain's Atlantic coast, with big skies and strong evening light.

37Playazo de Rodalquilar

Rodalquilar, Cabo de Gata-Nijar, Almeria · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 22 °C

El Playazo is a long beach of sand and fine gravel near Rodalquilar, in the Cabo de Gata-Nijar natural park in Almeria, with the ruined Castillo de San Ramon at the north end.

38Praia da Lanzada

Sanxenxo, Galicia · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 19 °C

One of the longest beaches in Galicia, with about 2 km of fine white sand backed by dunes.

39Praia de Pantin, Valdovino

Pantin, Valdovino, A Coruna, Galicia · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 19 °C

Praia de Pantin is a long beach of golden sand in Valdovino on the north coast of Galicia, known for its waves and its surf competitions.

40Praia do Rostro, Fisterra

Rostro, Cee, Costa da Morte, Galicia · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 18 °C

Praia do Rostro is a long beach of fine pale sand on the Costa da Morte in Galicia, near Fisterra and Cee, with a small lagoon behind and mountains on each side.

Frequently asked questions

Which are the top-rated family-friendly beaches in Spain?

Our top three are Ses Illetes (4.8 out of 5), Es Trenc (4.7 out of 5), Playa de Bolonia (4.7 out of 5). Scores are editorial ratings from the EuroShores team.

When is the best time to visit these beaches?

Most are at their best from June to September. Water temperatures average about 22 degrees C in season. Each beach page lists its exact season.

Which of these beaches are easiest to reach?

Ses Illetes, Es Trenc, Playa de Bolonia, Cala Galdana, Cala Santanyi and 35 more have easy access. The others need a walk, stairs or a rougher road, so check the access note on each page.
