Best Pebble Beaches in Greece

We rank the top 40 of 44 pebble beaches in Greece. Myrtos Beach (Greece) comes first with 4.9 / 5, followed by Porto Katsiki, Lalaria Beach.

The sea reaches about 24 degrees C in season, and most of these beaches are at their best from June to September. 19 of the 40 have easy access, and 23 are hidden gems.

Save on Pinterest

1Myrtos Beach

Kefalonia, Greece · 4.9 / 5 · Moderate access · about 27 °C

Consistently ranked among Europe's most beautiful beaches — gleaming white pebbles meet the most intense shade of turquoise blue imaginable, framed by towering cliffs.

2Porto Katsiki

Lefkada, Greece · 4.8 / 5 · Moderate access · about 24 °C

Tall white cliffs plunge into bright turquoise water on the Ionian coast of Lefkada, reached by a long flight of steps from the clifftop.

3Lalaria Beach

Skiathos · 4.7 / 5 · Challenging access · about 24 °C

A striking beach of smooth white pebbles beneath tall cliffs on the north coast of Skiathos, with sea arches and caves nearby.

4Porto Timoni

Afionas, Corfu · 4.7 / 5 · Challenging access · about 24 °C

Two back-to-back coves on the north-west coast of Corfu, separated by a narrow strip of land.

5Apella Beach

Apella, Karpathos · 4.6 / 5 · Challenging access · about 23 °C

A pretty bay of white pebbles and very clear water on the east coast of Karpathos, set under green hills.

6Damouchari Beach, Pelion

Damouchari, Pelion, Magnesia · 4.6 / 5 · Moderate access · about 25 °C

Damouchari is a small bay on the Pelion peninsula, with a beach of pebbles, a few old stone houses and a tiny harbour.

7Glyka Nera Beach

Sfakia, Crete · 4.6 / 5 · Challenging access · about 24 °C

A pebble cove on the south coast of Crete beneath steep cliffs, known for cool freshwater springs that mix with the clear sea.

8Katergo Beach

Katergo, Folegandros · 4.6 / 5 · Challenging access · about 23 °C

A wild, unspoilt cove on the south-east side of Folegandros, backed by tall cliffs and with deep blue water.

9Mylos Beach

Agios Nikitas, Lefkada · 4.6 / 5 · Moderate access · about 24 °C

A wild, mostly pebbly beach south of Agios Nikitas, framed by steep green hills and bright blue water.

10Agiofarago Beach, Crete

Agiofarago, Heraklion, south Crete · 4.5 / 5 · Challenging access · about 24 °C

Agiofarago is a small beach at the foot of a narrow limestone gorge on the south coast of Crete, in the Heraklion region near Matala.

11Agios Nikitas Beach, Lefkada

Agios Nikitas, Lefkada, Ionian Islands · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Agios Nikitas is a small village on the north-west coast of Lefkada, with a beach of pebbles and clear turquoise water.

12Dafnoudi Beach

Fiskardo area, Kefalonia · 4.5 / 5 · Moderate access · about 24 °C

A small, sheltered pebble cove on the north tip of Kefalonia near Fiskardo, with calm turquoise water and a pine forest behind it.

13Gidaki Beach

Ithaca, Ionian Islands · 4.5 / 5 · Challenging access · about 24 °C

A remote pebble cove on the east coast of Ithaca, backed by pale cliffs and clear blue water.

14Kalamitsi Beach

Kalamitsi, Lefkada · 4.5 / 5 · Challenging access · about 23 °C

A string of pebble coves on the west coast of Lefkada below the village of Kalamitsi, with clear water under steep cliffs.

15Kokkinokastro Beach

Alonnisos, Greece · 4.5 / 5 · Moderate access · about 24 °C

A pebble beach on the east coast of Alonnisos, named for its red cliffs, with rocky islets just offshore.

16Paleokastritsa Beach

Paleokastritsa, Corfu, Greece · 4.5 / 5 · Moderate access · about 23 °C

A cluster of small coves on the north-west coast of Corfu, backed by green hills and cliffs.

17Red Beach Santorini

Akrotiri, Santorini · 4.5 / 5 · Moderate access · about 23 °C

A pebble beach on the south coast of Santorini near Akrotiri, set below tall red volcanic cliffs with deep blue water.

18Agia Anna Beach, Amorgos

Agia Anna, Amorgos · 4.4 / 5 · Moderate access · about 23 °C

Agia Anna is a small pebble beach on the east coast of Amorgos, directly below the white Chozoviotissa monastery clinging to the cliff.

19Antisamos Beach

Kefalonia · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

A long bay near Sami on Kefalonia with green hills behind and clear blue water.

20Arvanitia Beach, Nafplio

Nafplio, Peloponnese · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Arvanitia is a small pebble beach at the foot of the Nafplio fortress hill, reached by a path around the rocks.

21Elounda Beach, Crete

Elounda, Lasithi, Crete · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

Elounda has several small beaches of sand and gravel along a calm, sheltered bay on the northeast coast of Crete.

22Fakistra Beach, Pelion

Fakistra, Zagora, Pelion · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Fakistra is a small pebble beach on the Aegean coast of Pelion, reached by a steep road down through chestnut and olive trees.

23Milia Beach

Skopelos, Greece · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

A long beach of fine pebbles and sand on the west coast of Skopelos, with pine-covered hills behind.

24Nikolaos Beach

Hydra · 4.4 / 5 · Moderate access · about 24 °C

A pebble cove on the car-free island of Hydra with sunbeds along the shore and clear, sheltered-looking water.

25Rousoum Gialos Beach

Alonnisos · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 23 °C

A small beach beside the port town of Patitiri on Alonnisos, with turquoise water below the houses.

26Sougia Beach, Crete

Sougia, Chania, Crete · 4.4 / 5 · Moderate access · about 24 °C

Sougia is a small village on the south-west coast of Crete with a long pebble beach in front of its tavernas.

27Tzortzi Gialos Beach

Alonnisos · 4.4 / 5 · Moderate access · about 23 °C

A curved bay on Alonnisos with pine-covered hills behind and turquoise water, often visited by boats.

28Vasiliki Beach, Lefkada

Vasiliki, Lefkada, Ionian Islands · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Vasiliki is a village at the head of a long bay on the south of Lefkada, famous as one of the best windsurfing spots in Greece.

29Vlychada Beach, Santorini

Vlychada, Santorini, Cyclades · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 26 °C

Vlychada is a quiet beach on the south coast of Santorini, with dark volcanic sand and pebbles below pale eroded cliffs.

30Agia Roumeli Beach, Crete

Agia Roumeli, Sfakia, Crete · 4.3 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Agia Roumeli is a small village on the south coast of Crete with a long pebble beach, reached on foot through the Samaria Gorge or by ferry.

31Agios Dimitrios Beach

Alonnisos · 4.3 / 5 · Easy access · about 23 °C

A long pale pebble beach on the east coast of Alonnisos, with deep clear water and a road close behind the shore.

32Agios Ioannis Beach

Agios Ioannis, Pelion · 4.3 / 5 · Moderate access · about 24 °C

A long pebble beach on the Aegean side of Pelion, with forested slopes behind and calm, clear water.

33Agnontas Beach, Skopelos

Agnontas, Skopelos · 4.3 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

Agnontas is a small fishing hamlet on a narrow green bay on the south-west coast of Skopelos.

34Kokkari Beach, Samos

Kokkari, Samos · 4.3 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Kokkari is a former fishing village on the north coast of Samos, with white houses climbing a small hill and pebble beaches on both sides of a rocky point.

35Liapades Beach, Corfu

Liapades, Paleokastritsa, Corfu · 4.3 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

Liapades is a small village beach on the north-west coast of Corfu, just south of Paleokastritsa.

36Livadi Beach, Astypalaia

Livadi, Astypalaia, Dodecanese · 4.3 / 5 · Easy access · about 26 °C

Livadi is the main beach of Astypalaia, a green valley bay with pebbles and sand below the whitewashed Chora and its Venetian castle.

37Nikiana Beach, Lefkada

Nikiana, Lefkada, Ionian Islands · 4.3 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Nikiana is a small seaside village on the east coast of Lefkada, with a long beach of pebbles, calm water and a view of the mainland hills.

38Perivolos Beach, Santorini

Perivolos, Santorini · 4.3 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Perivolos is a long beach of dark volcanic sand and fine pebbles on the south coast of Santorini, lined with relaxed beach bars and sunbeds.

39Acharavi Beach, Corfu

Acharavi, North Corfu, Ionian Islands · 4.2 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Acharavi is a village on the north coast of Corfu with one of the longest beaches on the island, a strip of sand and fine pebbles with shallow water.

40Kamari Beach

Kamari, Santorini, Cyclades, Greece · 4.2 / 5 · Easy access · about 23 °C

A long beach of dark volcanic sand and pebbles on the east coast of Santorini, backed by a seafront strip of hotels, cafes and tavernas.

Frequently asked questions

Which are the top-rated pebble beaches in Greece?

Our top three are Myrtos Beach (4.9 out of 5), Porto Katsiki (4.8 out of 5), Lalaria Beach (4.7 out of 5). Scores are editorial ratings from the EuroShores team.

When is the best time to visit these beaches?

Most are at their best from June to September. Water temperatures average about 24 degrees C in season. Each beach page lists its exact season.

Which of these beaches are easiest to reach?

Agios Nikitas Beach, Lefkada, Antisamos Beach, Arvanitia Beach, Nafplio, Elounda Beach, Crete, Fakistra Beach, Pelion and 14 more have easy access. The others need a walk, stairs or a rougher road, so check the access note on each page.
