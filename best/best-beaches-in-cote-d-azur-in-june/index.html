Best Beaches in the French Riviera in June

We rank the 11 beaches to visit in the French Riviera in June. Plage des Marinieres, Villefranche (France) comes first with 4.5 / 5, followed by Brouis Beach, Plage de la Garoupe.

The sea reaches about 23 degrees C in season, and most of these beaches are at their best from June to September. 10 of the 11 have easy access, and 1 are hidden gems.

Save on Pinterest

1Plage des Marinieres, Villefranche

Villefranche-sur-Mer, Cote d Azur · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

Plage des Marinieres is a small pebble beach in the deep natural harbour of Villefranche-sur-Mer, between Nice and Monaco.

2Brouis Beach

La Croix-Valmer, French Riviera · 4.4 / 5 · Moderate access · about 21 °C

A small sandy cove on the coast of La Croix-Valmer, with pine-covered hills behind and open blue water in front.

3Plage de la Garoupe

Antibes, French Riviera · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 22 °C

Plage de la Garoupe is a sheltered crescent of sand on the cape of Antibes, with calm clear water and a green backdrop of pines.

4Almanarre Beach, Hyeres

Almanarre, Hyeres, Var · 4.2 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

Almanarre is a long, wind-swept beach of fine sand on the Giens peninsula near Hyeres, famous for windsurfing and kitesurfing.

5La Capte Beach, Hyeres

La Capte, Hyeres, Var · 4.2 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

La Capte is a long beach of fine sand on the Giens peninsula near Hyeres, with pine trees behind and calm, shallow water.

6Plage de la Croisette

Cannes, French Riviera · 4.2 / 5 · Easy access · about 23 °C

The sandy beaches along the famous Boulevard de la Croisette in Cannes, a mix of private beach clubs and free public sections.

7Plage de la Salis

Antibes, French Riviera · 4.2 / 5 · Easy access · about 23 °C

A sheltered sandy beach at Antibes with calm, shallow water that suits families, and views across the bay.

8Plage de Nice, Baie des Anges

Nice, French Riviera · 4.2 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

The beach of Nice is a long curve of smooth pebbles along the Bay of Angels, backed by the Promenade des Anglais.

9Plage des Sablettes, Menton

Menton, French Riviera · 4.2 / 5 · Easy access · about 22 °C

Plage des Sablettes is the favourite beach of Menton, a short stretch of grey pebbles with calm water and the pastel houses of the old town rising behind.

10Plage de la Gravette

Antibes, French Riviera · 4.1 / 5 · Easy access · about 23 °C

A small sandy beach right beside the old town and port of Antibes, with calm shallow water and a walk to the ramparts.

11Plage de Cannes, La Croisette

Cannes, French Riviera · 4.0 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

The beaches of Cannes line the Boulevard de la Croisette, with fine imported sand, shallow clear water and private beach clubs.
