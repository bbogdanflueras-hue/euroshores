Easiest Beaches to Reach in Cyprus

We rank the 28 easy-access beaches in Cyprus. Konnos Bay, Cape Greco (Cyprus) comes first with 4.7 / 5, followed by Fig Tree Bay, Protaras, Nissi Beach, Ayia Napa.

The sea reaches about 25 degrees C in season, and most of these beaches are at their best from May to October. 28 of the 28 have easy access, and 9 are hidden gems.

Save on Pinterest

1Konnos Bay, Cape Greco

Cape Greco, Protaras, Cyprus · 4.7 / 5 · Easy access · about 26 °C

Konnos Bay is a small sandy cove at the foot of pine-covered cliffs near Cape Greco, with clear turquoise water that is good for snorkelling.

2Fig Tree Bay, Protaras

Protaras, Cyprus · 4.6 / 5 · Easy access · about 26 °C

Fig Tree Bay is a sheltered sandy bay in Protaras with shallow, calm water that suits children and beginners.

3Nissi Beach, Ayia Napa

Ayia Napa, Cyprus · 4.6 / 5 · Easy access · about 26 °C

Nissi Beach is a wide white-sand beach on the edge of Ayia Napa, with shallow turquoise water and a small islet that can be reached by wading in calm weather.

4Ayia Thekla Beach

Ayia Thekla, near Ayia Napa, Cyprus · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 26 °C

Ayia Thekla Beach lies on the coast west of Ayia Napa beside a small seaside chapel.

5Cape Greco Coves, Ayia Napa

Cape Greco, Ayia Napa, Cyprus · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Cape Greco is a rocky headland between Ayia Napa and Protaras with small coves, sea caves and very clear water.

6Coral Bay, Peyia

Peyia, Paphos, Cyprus · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Coral Bay near Peyia is a sandy bay sheltered between two headlands, with calm, shallow water and a well-developed resort strip behind it.

7Glyki Nero Beach, Ayia Napa

Glyki Nero, Ayia Napa, Cyprus · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Glyki Nero is a small sandy cove on the coast between Ayia Napa and Cape Greco.

8Landa Beach, Ayia Napa

Ayia Napa, Cyprus · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 26 °C

Landa Beach is a golden sand beach a short walk west of Nissi Beach in Ayia Napa, with shallow, clear water and a calmer feel than its busier neighbour.

9Makronissos Beach, Ayia Napa

Ayia Napa, Cyprus · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 26 °C

Makronissos Beach is a long sandy beach on the east side of Ayia Napa with calm, shallow water and a more relaxed feel than Nissi Beach.

10Governor's Beach, Limassol

Between Limassol and Larnaca, Cyprus · 4.3 / 5 · Easy access · about 26 °C

Governor's Beach lies below white chalk cliffs between Limassol and Larnaca, with dark grey sand and pebbles that contrast with the pale rock.

11Sunrise Beach, Protaras

Protaras, Cyprus · 4.3 / 5 · Easy access · about 26 °C

Sunrise Beach is a sandy beach on the Protaras coast with calm, shallow water and a line of hotels behind it.

12Toxeftra Beach, Pegeia

Toxeftra, Pegeia, Paphos, Cyprus · 4.3 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Toxeftra Beach is a quiet sandy bay on the coast near Pegeia, north of Coral Bay.

13Kalamies Beach, Protaras

Kalamies, Protaras, Cyprus · 4.2 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Kalamies Beach is a sandy cove in Protaras with clear shallow water.

14Latchi Beach, Polis

Latchi, Polis, Cyprus · 4.2 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Latchi, or Latsi, is a small fishing harbour near Polis with a pebble and coarse sand beach and a line of seafront tavernas serving fresh fish.

15Pernera Beach, Protaras

Pernera, Protaras, Cyprus · 4.2 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Pernera Beach is a sandy resort beach in Protaras with shallow, clear water.

16Pissouri Bay Beach

Pissouri, Limassol, Cyprus · 4.2 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Pissouri Bay Beach is a long beach of pebbles and coarse sand on the south coast between Limassol and Paphos, with a quieter feel than the larger resorts.

17Venus Beach, Paphos

Paphos, Cyprus · 4.2 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Venus Beach is a sandy beach on the coastal road north of Kato Paphos, close to the Tombs of the Kings.

18Finikoudes Beach, Larnaca

Larnaca, Cyprus · 4.1 / 5 · Easy access · about 26 °C

Finikoudes is a sandy city beach with a palm-lined promenade in the centre of Larnaca, with shallow water and cafes just behind the sand.

19Kefalos Beach, Paphos

Paphos, Cyprus · 4.1 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Kefalos Beach is a sandy beach near the Paphos lighthouse on the west edge of Kato Paphos.

20Mackenzie Beach, Larnaca

Larnaca, Cyprus · 4.1 / 5 · Easy access · about 26 °C

Mackenzie Beach is a sandy beach just south of Larnaca with a row of bars and tavernas along the sand.

21Polis Chrysochous Beach

Polis, Cyprus · 4.1 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Polis Chrysochous Beach is the town beach of Polis on the north-west coast.

22Polyxenia Beach, Protaras

Polyxenia, Protaras, Cyprus · 4.1 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Polyxenia Beach is a short sandy beach in Protaras, close to Pernera.

23Agios Georgios Beach, Pegeia

Agios Georgios, Pegeia, Paphos, Cyprus · 4.0 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Agios Georgios is a small harbour area on the Pegeia coast with a church, a few tavernas and a pebble and rock shoreline.

24Alykes Beach, Kato Paphos

Kato Paphos, Cyprus · 4.0 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Alykes Beach is a small town beach in Kato Paphos, close to the harbour, the castle and the archaeological sites.

25Faros Beach, Pervolia

Pervolia, Larnaca, Cyprus · 4.0 / 5 · Easy access · about 26 °C

Faros Beach is a quiet local sandy beach near the village of Pervolia on the coast south-west of Larnaca.

26Kato Pyrgos Beach, Tillyria

Kato Pyrgos, Tillyria, Cyprus · 4.0 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Kato Pyrgos is a remote pebble coast in the Tillyria region, far from the big resorts.

27Kissonerga Beach, Paphos

Kissonerga, Paphos, Cyprus · 4.0 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Kissonerga Beach is a quiet stretch of pebbles and rock on the coast north of Paphos, below the village of Kissonerga.

28Pomos Beach, Tillyria

Pomos, Tillyria, Cyprus · 4.0 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Pomos is a small fishing village on the Tillyria coast with a pebble shoreline and a quiet atmosphere.

Frequently asked questions

Which are the top-rated easy-access beaches in Cyprus?

Our top three are Konnos Bay, Cape Greco (4.7 out of 5), Fig Tree Bay, Protaras (4.6 out of 5), Nissi Beach, Ayia Napa (4.6 out of 5). Scores are editorial ratings from the EuroShores team.

When is the best time to visit these beaches?

Most are at their best from May to October. Water temperatures average about 25 degrees C in season. Each beach page lists its exact season.

Which of these beaches are easiest to reach?

Konnos Bay, Cape Greco, Fig Tree Bay, Protaras, Nissi Beach, Ayia Napa, Ayia Thekla Beach, Cape Greco Coves, Ayia Napa and 23 more have easy access. The others need a walk, stairs or a rougher road, so check the access note on each page.
