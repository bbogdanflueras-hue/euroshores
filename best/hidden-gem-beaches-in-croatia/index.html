Hidden Gem Beaches in Croatia

We rank the 29 hidden gem beaches in Croatia. Stiniva (Croatia) comes first with 4.7 / 5, followed by Dubovica, Nugal Beach.

The sea reaches about 24 degrees C in season, and most of these beaches are at their best from June to September. 17 of the 29 have easy access, and 29 are hidden gems.

Save on Pinterest

1Stiniva

Vis, Croatia · 4.7 / 5 · Challenging access · about 24 °C

A hidden pebble cove squeezed between sheer cliffs, with a narrow sea entrance framing the turquoise water.

2Dubovica

Hvar, Croatia · 4.6 / 5 · Moderate access · about 25 °C

A small pebble cove on the south coast of Hvar, reached by a steep path from the road or by boat.

3Nugal Beach

Makarska, Dalmatia · 4.6 / 5 · Challenging access · about 24 °C

A small, secluded pebble cove with clear turquoise water below limestone cliffs and pines, reached on foot along a path from the Makarska coast.

4Pasjaca Beach

Popovici, Konavle, Croatia · 4.6 / 5 · Challenging access · about 23 °C

A wild pebble beach below a steep cliff in Konavle, south-east of Dubrovnik, known for its dramatic setting.

5Pupnatska Luka Beach, Korcula

Pupnatska Luka, Pupnat, Korcula · 4.6 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

Pupnatska Luka is a long pebble bay on the south coast of Korcula, below the village of Pupnat, with clear turquoise water and forested hills on both sides.

6Sakarun Beach

Dugi Otok, Croatia · 4.6 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

A long, shallow, white-sand bay in the Kornati archipelago — rare for the Adriatic and ideal for families.

7Saplunara

Mljet, Croatia · 4.6 / 5 · Moderate access · about 25 °C

A rare sandy bay at the southeast end of Mljet, relatively quiet and set in protected surroundings.

8Kamenjak Beach, Premantura

Premantura, Istria · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

Kamenjak is a protected peninsula at the southern tip of Istria, with rocky coves, small pebble beaches and low pine scrub.

9Porat Beach

Bisevo, Croatia · 4.5 / 5 · Moderate access · about 24 °C

A small sandy bay on the island of Bisevo, with very clear water, a few houses and terraced fields behind.

10Sveti Jakov Beach

Dubrovnik, Dalmatia · 4.5 / 5 · Moderate access · about 24 °C

A small pebble and shingle cove east of Dubrovnik old town, with clear, deep blue water and wooded cliffs behind.

11Donje Celo Beach, Kolocep

Donje Celo, Kolocep, Elaphiti Islands · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

Donje Celo is a small village on Kolocep, the smallest inhabited Elaphiti island near Dubrovnik, with a small sandy beach in a sheltered bay.

12Drvenik Beach, Dalmatia

Drvenik, Gradac, Split-Dalmatia, Croatia · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Drvenik is a village at the southern end of the Makarska Riviera in Dalmatia, with a long pebble beach below pine trees and a view of the island of Hvar and the Peljesac peninsula.

13Jagodna Beach

Hvar, Dalmatia · 4.4 / 5 · Moderate access · about 25 °C

A pebble beach in a clear bay on the south coast of Hvar, east of Hvar Town, backed by pine forest.

14Lovrecina Bay, Brac

Postira, Brac · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

Lovrecina is a sheltered bay near Postira on the north coast of Brac, with fine pebbles and sand, shallow clear water and pine trees behind the beach.

15Pinizule Beach

Premantura, Istria, Croatia · 4.4 / 5 · Moderate access · about 23 °C

A small rocky cove on the Istrian coast with clear water, flat stone slabs and pine trees reaching down to the shore.

16Sjekirica Beach, Brsecine

Brsecine, Dubrovnik coast · 4.4 / 5 · Challenging access · about 24 °C

Sjekirica is a small pebble beach on the Adriatic coast north of Dubrovnik, close to the village of Brsecine, with a rocky islet just offshore and green hills behind.

17Stoncica Beach

Vis Island, Croatia · 4.4 / 5 · Moderate access · about 24 °C

A sheltered pebble bay on the island of Vis, with clear water and pine-covered hills around it.

18Trstenica Beach

Orebic, Peljesac · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 23 °C

Trstenica is a long beach of fine shingle and sand beside the town of Orebic on the Peljesac peninsula.

19Zavala Beach

Zavala, Hvar · 4.4 / 5 · Moderate access · about 24 °C

A pebble and gravel beach in the village of Zavala on the quiet south coast of Hvar.

20Bilin Zal Beach, Lumbarda

Lumbarda, Korcula · 4.3 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

Bilin Zal is a shallow cove of sand and fine pebbles near the village of Lumbarda at the eastern end of Korcula.

21Igrane Beach, Makarska Riviera

Igrane, Makarska Riviera, Split-Dalmatia · 4.3 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Igrane is a small village on the southern Makarska Riviera, with a pebble beach and a little harbour below the Biokovo mountain.

22Pomena Beach, Mljet

Pomena, Mljet, Dubrovnik-Neretva · 4.3 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Pomena is a small village at the north-western end of Mljet island, beside the entrance to Mljet National Park.

23Trstenik Beach, Peljesac

Trstenik, Peljesac, Dubrovnik-Neretva · 4.3 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Trstenik is a small village on the south coast of the Peljesac peninsula, with a long pebble beach and a little harbour with fishing boats.

24Trstevica Beach, Prvic

Sepurine, Prvic Island, Sibenik archipelago · 4.3 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

Trstevica is a quiet pebble beach beside the village of Sepurine on Prvic, a small island in the Sibenik archipelago.

25Valun Beach, Cres

Valun, Cres · 4.3 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

Valun is a small fishing village on the west coast of Cres, with a pebble beach in a sheltered bay, old stone houses and a quiet harbour.

26Viganj Beach, Peljesac

Viganj, Peljesac, Dubrovnik-Neretva · 4.3 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

Viganj is a small village on the Peljesac peninsula, facing the island of Korcula across a narrow channel.

27Kupari Beach

Zupa dubrovacka, near Dubrovnik · 4.2 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

A pebble beach in Zupa dubrovacka, about 10 km south of Dubrovnik, beside the shell of a military holiday resort built for Yugoslav army officers.

28Stobrec Beach, Split

Stobrec, Split-Dalmatia · 4.2 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Stobrec is a former fishing village on the eastern edge of Split, with a pebble beach and a small harbour.

29Trpanj Beach, Peljesac

Trpanj, Peljesac, Dubrovnik-Neretva · 4.2 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Trpanj is a small fishing village at the northern end of the Peljesac peninsula, with a quiet pebble beach beside the harbour.

Frequently asked questions

Which are the top-rated hidden gem beaches in Croatia?

Our top three are Stiniva (4.7 out of 5), Dubovica (4.6 out of 5), Nugal Beach (4.6 out of 5). Scores are editorial ratings from the EuroShores team.

When is the best time to visit these beaches?

Most are at their best from June to September. Water temperatures average about 24 degrees C in season. Each beach page lists its exact season.

Which of these beaches are easiest to reach?

Pupnatska Luka Beach, Korcula, Sakarun Beach, Kamenjak Beach, Premantura, Donje Celo Beach, Kolocep, Drvenik Beach, Dalmatia and 12 more have easy access. The others need a walk, stairs or a rougher road, so check the access note on each page.
