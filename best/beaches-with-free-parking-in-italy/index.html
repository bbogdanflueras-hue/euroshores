Best Beaches with Free Parking in Italy

We rank the top 40 of 44 beaches with free parking in Italy. Cala Goloritzé (Italy) comes first with 4.8 / 5, followed by Cala Mariolu, La Cinta Beach.

The sea reaches about 24 degrees C in season, and most of these beaches are at their best from June to September. 35 of the 40 have easy access, and 16 are hidden gems.

1Cala Goloritzé

Sardinia, Italy · 4.8 / 5 · Challenging access · about 24 °C

A secluded cove under a limestone needle, reached only on foot or by boat — worth every step.

2Cala Mariolu

Gulf of Orosei, Sardinia, Italy · 4.8 / 5 · Challenging access · about 23 °C

A pebble cove with vivid turquoise water below steep cliffs, reachable only by boat or on foot.

3La Cinta Beach

San Teodoro, Sardinia · 4.7 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

A long strip of soft white sand and shallow turquoise water just outside San Teodoro, with a lagoon behind it.

Scivu, Arbus, Costa Verde · 4.7 / 5 · Moderate access · about 24 °C

Scivu is a wild beach on the Costa Verde of south-west Sardinia, with tall golden dunes that reach down to a long sweep of sand.

Capriccioli, Costa Smeralda, Sardinia · 4.6 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Frequently asked questions

Which are the top-rated beaches with free parking in Italy?

Our top three are Cala Goloritzé (4.8 out of 5), Cala Mariolu (4.8 out of 5), La Cinta Beach (4.7 out of 5). Scores are editorial ratings from the EuroShores team.

When is the best time to visit these beaches?

Most are at their best from June to September. Water temperatures average about 24 degrees C in season. Each beach page lists its exact season.

Which of these beaches are easiest to reach?

La Cinta Beach, Spiaggia di Capriccioli, Spiaggia di Portonovo, Cala Cipolla, Cala Mari Pintau, Sardinia and 30 more have easy access. The others need a walk, stairs or a rougher road, so check the access note on each page.
