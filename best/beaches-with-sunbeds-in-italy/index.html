Best Beaches with Sunbeds in Italy

We rank the top 40 of 112 beaches with sunbeds and umbrellas in Italy. Cala Luna (Italy) comes first with 4.7 / 5, followed by La Cinta Beach, Porto Giunco.

The sea reaches about 24 degrees C in season, and most of these beaches are at their best from June to September. 28 of the 40 have easy access, and 14 are hidden gems.

Save on Pinterest

1Cala Luna

Gulf of Orosei, Sardinia, Italy · 4.7 / 5 · Challenging access · about 22 °C

A crescent of pale pebbles and coarse sand between limestone cliffs, with caves along the back and a freshwater pond behind the beach.

2La Cinta Beach

San Teodoro, Sardinia · 4.7 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

A long strip of soft white sand and shallow turquoise water just outside San Teodoro, with a lagoon behind it.

3Porto Giunco

Villasimius, Sardinia · 4.7 / 5 · Easy access · about 23 °C

A wide sandy beach in the south-east of Sardinia, with an old watchtower on the headland and a lagoon behind.

4Scivu Beach, Sardinia

Scivu, Arbus, Costa Verde · 4.7 / 5 · Moderate access · about 24 °C

Scivu is a wild beach on the Costa Verde of south-west Sardinia, with tall golden dunes that reach down to a long sweep of sand.

5Spiaggia Grande

Amalfi Coast, Italy · 4.7 / 5 · Easy access · about 23 °C

Nestled beneath the pastel-coloured cliffside village of Positano — one of the most dramatic and romantic settings in Europe.

6Tuerredda Beach

Teulada, Sardinia, Italy · 4.7 / 5 · Easy access · about 23 °C

A crescent of fine pale sand and shallow turquoise water on the south coast of Sardinia, facing a small island just offshore.

7Vignanotica Beach, Gargano

Vignanotica, Vieste, Puglia · 4.7 / 5 · Moderate access · about 25 °C

Vignanotica is a cove of white pebbles at the foot of tall white limestone cliffs on the Gargano coast, south of Vieste.

8Baia delle Zagare

Mattinata, Gargano, Puglia · 4.6 / 5 · Moderate access · about 24 °C

A pebble cove on the Gargano coast of Puglia, known for its white limestone sea stacks, clear turquoise water and dramatic cliffs backed by pine trees.

9Li Cossi

Costa Paradiso, Sardinia · 4.6 / 5 · Moderate access · about 24 °C

A small sandy cove on the north coast of Sardinia near Costa Paradiso, set between red granite rocks with green-blue water.

10Piscina Rei Beach

Muravera, Sardinia · 4.6 / 5 · Moderate access · about 25 °C

A long beach of fine sand on the south-east coast of Sardinia in the municipality of Muravera, with clear water and dunes behind the shore.

11Punta Molentis

Villasimius, Sardinia · 4.6 / 5 · Easy access · about 23 °C

A small beach of pale sand and clear shallow water near Villasimius, between low granite rocks.

12San Vito Lo Capo

San Vito Lo Capo, Sicily, Italy · 4.6 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

A broad curve of pale sand and clear shallow water at the tip of north-west Sicily, backed by the town and a rocky headland.

13Spiaggia di Notteri, Villasimius

Villasimius, Sardinia · 4.6 / 5 · Easy access · about 26 °C

Notteri is a long beach of fine white sand near Villasimius, on the south-east tip of Sardinia, with a coastal lagoon behind it where flamingos can sometimes be seen.

14Spiaggia di Piscinas

Arbus, Costa Verde, Sardinia · 4.6 / 5 · Moderate access · about 22 °C

A wild, long beach backed by some of the tallest sand dunes in Europe, on the Costa Verde of south-west Sardinia.

15Spiaggia di Portonovo

Portonovo, Conero, Marche · 4.6 / 5 · Easy access · about 23 °C

Portonovo is a bay of white pebbles and clear blue water below the green cliffs of the Conero headland.

16Tonnara di Scopello

Scopello, Castellammare del Golfo, Sicily · 4.6 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Tonnara di Scopello is a small cove on the north-west coast of Sicily, with an old tuna fishery building, tall sea stacks called faraglioni just offshore and very clear water.

17Torre dell'Orso Beach

Melendugno, Puglia · 4.6 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

A wide crescent of pale sand and shallow turquoise water in Salento, guarded by a watchtower and the famous Two Sisters rock stacks.

18Tropea Beach

Calabria, Italy · 4.6 / 5 · Moderate access · about 24 °C

A sweep of pale sand and clear blue water below the cliff-top town of Tropea on the Calabrian coast.

19Baia dei Turchi

Otranto, Puglia · 4.5 / 5 · Moderate access · about 25 °C

A natural bay in Salento, a few kilometres north of Otranto, with fine pale sand, pines and clear shallow water.

20Baia Sardinia Beach

Baia Sardinia, Arzachena, Costa Smeralda, Sardinia · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

Baia Sardinia is a small resort on the Costa Smeralda in north-east Sardinia, with a sheltered bay of coarse sand, granite rocks and clear water.

21Baratti Beach, Tuscany

Golfo di Baratti, Piombino, Tuscany · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

Baratti is a calm crescent bay of fine pale sand near Piombino on the Tuscan coast, with pine trees behind the shore and the island of Elba visible on a clear day.

22Biodola Beach, Elba

Biodola, Portoferraio, Elba · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

Biodola is a wide bay of pale sand and fine gravel on the north coast of Elba, west of Portoferraio, with turquoise water and green hills on both sides.

23Cala Cipolla

Chia, Sardinia · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

A pretty bay on the south coast of Sardinia near Chia, with soft sand, clear water and a rocky headland.

24Cala Mari Pintau, Sardinia

Mari Pintau, Quartu Sant Elena, Cagliari, Sardinia · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Cala Mari Pintau is a small cove of white sand and rocks on the south-east coast of Sardinia, with very clear water and low granite cliffs on either side.

25Costa Rei

Costa Rei, Castiadas, Sardinia · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

A long beach of fine white sand and clear shallow water on the south-east coast of Sardinia.

26Fetovaia Beach

Fetovaia, Elba · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 23 °C

A curved bay of golden sand and granite grains on Elba south-west coast, with calm clear water and a pine-scented hillside behind.

27Is Arutas

Cabras, Sinis Peninsula, Sardinia · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

A beach on the Sinis peninsula in west Sardinia, known for sand made of tiny quartz grains in many colours that give the shallow water a clear, bright look.

28Le Bombarde Beach, Alghero

Le Bombarde, Alghero, Sardinia · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Le Bombarde is a bay of fine white sand north of Alghero on the north-west coast of Sardinia, with clear turquoise water and pine trees behind the shore.

29Marina di Praia

Praiano, Amalfi Coast · 4.5 / 5 · Moderate access · about 24 °C

A small pebbly cove at the bottom of a steep gorge, with a pretty old fishing hamlet, a few restaurants and a sea that is deep blue and very clear.

30Paraggi Beach

Paraggi, Santa Margherita Ligure, Liguria · 4.5 / 5 · Moderate access · about 24 °C

A small sheltered bay on the Portofino coast, framed by pastel houses and wooded hills.

31Pescoluse Beach, Salento

Pescoluse, Salve, Puglia · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Pescoluse is a long beach of fine white sand on the Ionian coast of Salento, often called the Maldives of Salento for its shallow, clear water and pale dunes.

32Pizzomunno Beach

Vieste, Puglia · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

The beach of Pizzomunno is a curve of pale sand in Vieste, dominated by a white limestone stack that gives it its name.

33Punta Prosciutto

Porto Cesareo, Salento, Puglia · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

A long sandy beach on the Ionian coast of Salento, backed by dunes and part of a protected area within the Porto Cesareo marine reserve.

34Sa Colonia Beach

Chia, Domus de Maria, Sardinia · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

A sandy beach at Chia on the south coast of Sardinia, with a coastal lagoon behind it where pink flamingos sometimes feed.

35Sansone Beach

Portoferraio, Elba · 4.5 / 5 · Moderate access · about 23 °C

A small bay of bright white pebbles and clear turquoise water on Elba north coast near Portoferraio.

36Spiaggia di Capo Coda Cavallo

Capo Coda Cavallo, San Teodoro, Sardinia · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Capo Coda Cavallo is a protected headland south of San Teodoro in north-east Sardinia, with a long curve of pale sand, red granite rocks and clear turquoise water.

37Alimini Beach

Alimini, Otranto, Puglia · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

A stretch of fine sand and clear, gently sloping water on the Salento coast near Otranto, backed by pine woods and dunes.

38Bonassola Beach, Liguria

Bonassola, La Spezia, Liguria · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

Bonassola is a small seaside village on the Ligurian coast, a short drive from the Cinque Terre, with a beach of pebbles and coarse sand.

39Cala Liberotto, Orosei

Cala Liberotto, Orosei, Sardinia · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Cala Liberotto is a beach on the Orosei coast of eastern Sardinia, with fine sand and small pebbles, pine and juniper trees behind and rocks at the ends that are good for snorkelling.

40Cala Sinzias

Castiadas, Sardinia · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

A wide, gently shelving bay of fine pale sand and shallow turquoise water in south-east Sardinia, with granite rocks and pines on both sides.

Frequently asked questions

Which are the top-rated beaches with sunbeds and umbrellas in Italy?

Our top three are Cala Luna (4.7 out of 5), La Cinta Beach (4.7 out of 5), Porto Giunco (4.7 out of 5). Scores are editorial ratings from the EuroShores team.

When is the best time to visit these beaches?

Most are at their best from June to September. Water temperatures average about 24 degrees C in season. Each beach page lists its exact season.

Which of these beaches are easiest to reach?

La Cinta Beach, Porto Giunco, Spiaggia Grande, Tuerredda Beach, Punta Molentis and 23 more have easy access. The others need a walk, stairs or a rougher road, so check the access note on each page.
