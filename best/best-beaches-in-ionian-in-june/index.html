Best Beaches in the Ionian Islands in June

We rank the 35 beaches to visit in the Ionian Islands in June. Myrtos Beach (Greece) comes first with 4.9 / 5, followed by Navagio Beach, Egremni Beach.

The sea reaches about 24 degrees C in season, and most of these beaches are at their best from June to September. 20 of the 35 have easy access, and 15 are hidden gems.

Save on Pinterest

1Myrtos Beach

Kefalonia, Greece · 4.9 / 5 · Moderate access · about 27 °C

Consistently ranked among Europe's most beautiful beaches — gleaming white pebbles meet the most intense shade of turquoise blue imaginable, framed by towering cliffs.

2Navagio Beach

Zakynthos, Greece · 4.9 / 5 · Moderate access · about 26 °C

The iconic Shipwreck Beach, enclosed by soaring white limestone cliffs — arguably the most photographed beach in the world.

3Egremni Beach

Lefkada, Ionian Islands, Greece · 4.8 / 5 · Challenging access · about 23 °C

A long sweep of pale sand and vivid turquoise water below white limestone cliffs on the west coast of Lefkada.

4Porto Katsiki

Lefkada, Greece · 4.8 / 5 · Moderate access · about 24 °C

Tall white cliffs plunge into bright turquoise water on the Ionian coast of Lefkada, reached by a long flight of steps from the clifftop.

5Porto Timoni

Afionas, Corfu · 4.7 / 5 · Challenging access · about 24 °C

Two back-to-back coves on the north-west coast of Corfu, separated by a narrow strip of land.

6Mylos Beach

Agios Nikitas, Lefkada · 4.6 / 5 · Moderate access · about 24 °C

A wild, mostly pebbly beach south of Agios Nikitas, framed by steep green hills and bright blue water.

7Myrtiotissa Beach, Corfu

Myrtiotissa, Corfu, Ionian Islands · 4.6 / 5 · Challenging access · about 24 °C

Myrtiotissa is a long golden beach on the west coast of Corfu, backed by steep green cliffs and a small monastery above.

8Agios Nikitas Beach, Lefkada

Agios Nikitas, Lefkada, Ionian Islands · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Agios Nikitas is a small village on the north-west coast of Lefkada, with a beach of pebbles and clear turquoise water.

9Dafnoudi Beach

Fiskardo area, Kefalonia · 4.5 / 5 · Moderate access · about 24 °C

A small, sheltered pebble cove on the north tip of Kefalonia near Fiskardo, with calm turquoise water and a pine forest behind it.

10Gerakas Beach

Zakynthos · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

A sandy bay on the south-east coast of Zakynthos below pale clay cliffs.

11Gidaki Beach

Ithaca, Ionian Islands · 4.5 / 5 · Challenging access · about 24 °C

A remote pebble cove on the east coast of Ithaca, backed by pale cliffs and clear blue water.

12Kalamitsi Beach

Kalamitsi, Lefkada · 4.5 / 5 · Challenging access · about 23 °C

A string of pebble coves on the west coast of Lefkada below the village of Kalamitsi, with clear water under steep cliffs.

13Paleokastritsa Beach

Paleokastritsa, Corfu, Greece · 4.5 / 5 · Moderate access · about 23 °C

A cluster of small coves on the north-west coast of Corfu, backed by green hills and cliffs.

14Pefkoulia Beach, Lefkada

Pefkoulia, Agios Nikitas, Lefkada · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Pefkoulia is a long beach of golden sand and pebbles on the west coast of Lefkada, below steep green hills near Agios Nikitas.

15Petani Beach

Paliki, Kefalonia · 4.5 / 5 · Moderate access · about 23 °C

A long beach of pale sand and pebbles beneath white limestone cliffs on the west coast of the Paliki peninsula, with clear water and a relaxed atmosphere.

16Agiofili Beach

Vasiliki, Lefkada, Greece · 4.4 / 5 · Moderate access · about 23 °C

A beach about 2 km from Vasiliki on the south of Lefkada, with clear water and a sandy shore below dry hills.

17Agios Gordios Beach

Agios Gordios, Corfu, Ionian Islands · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

A Blue Flag beach of about 1 km on the west coast of Corfu, backed by steep green cliffs and a rock offshore.

18Antisamos Beach

Kefalonia · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

A long bay near Sami on Kefalonia with green hills behind and clear blue water.

19Dafni Beach, Zakynthos

Dafni, Vasilikos, Zakynthos · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Dafni is a long sandy beach on the Vasilikos peninsula in the south-east of Zakynthos, close to the Gerakas beach where sea turtles nest.

20Kathisma Beach

Lefkada, Greece · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

A long beach on the west coast of Lefkada with clear blue water, organised sunbeds and beach bars.

21Marathias Beach, Zakynthos

Marathias, Keri, Zakynthos · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Marathias is a small bay near Keri on the south-west coast of Zakynthos, framed by white limestone rocks and shallow turquoise water.

22Porto Limnionas, Zakynthos

Porto Limnionas, Zakynthos · 4.4 / 5 · Moderate access · about 24 °C

Porto Limnionas is a narrow rocky inlet on the north-west coast of Zakynthos, with clear deep water and no sand.

23Sidari Beach

Sidari, Corfu · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

Sidari on the north coast of Corfu has sandy beaches and a maze of golden sandstone cliffs carved by the sea.

24Vasiliki Beach, Lefkada

Vasiliki, Lefkada, Ionian Islands · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Vasiliki is a village at the head of a long bay on the south of Lefkada, famous as one of the best windsurfing spots in Greece.

25Xi Beach, Kefalonia

Xi, Paliki, Kefalonia · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Xi Beach is a long stretch of reddish-gold sand on the Paliki peninsula of Kefalonia, south of Lixouri, backed by low clay cliffs.

26Glyfada Beach

Glyfada, Corfu, Greece · 4.3 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

A broad golden beach on the west coast of Corfu, backed by wooded hills, with gentle water and a lively summer scene.

27Liapades Beach, Corfu

Liapades, Paleokastritsa, Corfu · 4.3 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

Liapades is a small village beach on the north-west coast of Corfu, just south of Paleokastritsa.

28Nikiana Beach, Lefkada

Nikiana, Lefkada, Ionian Islands · 4.3 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Nikiana is a small seaside village on the east coast of Lefkada, with a long beach of pebbles, calm water and a view of the mainland hills.

29Skala Beach, Kefalonia

Skala, Kefalonia · 4.3 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Skala is a long, gently shelving beach of golden sand and fine pebbles on the south coast of Kefalonia.

30Xigia Beach

Zakynthos, Ionian Islands · 4.3 / 5 · Moderate access · about 24 °C

A small sandy and rocky cove on the north-east coast of Zakynthos, known for its sulphur springs, cloudy-turquoise water and pale cliffs.

31Acharavi Beach, Corfu

Acharavi, North Corfu, Ionian Islands · 4.2 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Acharavi is a village on the north coast of Corfu with one of the longest beaches on the island, a strip of sand and fine pebbles with shallow water.

32Kalamaki Beach

Laganas Bay, Zakynthos · 4.2 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

A long stretch of golden sand on Laganas Bay and part of the protected loggerhead nesting area.

33Laganas Beach, Zakynthos

Laganas, Zakynthos · 4.2 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Laganas is a long beach of fine pale sand at the head of Laganas Bay on Zakynthos, inside a national marine park where loggerhead turtles nest.

34Tsilivi Beach

Zakynthos, Ionian Islands · 4.2 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

A long sandy beach north of Zakynthos Town with gently shelving, warm water and a lively strip of beach bars and tavernas.

35Ipsos Beach, Corfu

Ipsos, Corfu, Ionian Islands · 4.1 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Ipsos is a resort on the east coast of Corfu with a long curved beach of pebbles and coarse sand, backed by the road, tavernas and bars.

Frequently asked questions

Which are the top-rated beaches to visit in the Ionian Islands in June?

Our top three are Myrtos Beach (4.9 out of 5), Navagio Beach (4.9 out of 5), Egremni Beach (4.8 out of 5). Scores are editorial ratings from the EuroShores team.

When is the best time to visit these beaches?

Most are at their best from June to September. Water temperatures average about 24 degrees C in season. Each beach page lists its exact season.

Which of these beaches are easiest to reach?
