Best Rocky Coves in Europe

We rank the top 40 of 41 rocky coves in Europe. Baia di Ieranto (Italy) comes first with 4.7 / 5, followed by Benagil Cove, Cala d'Hort.

The sea reaches about 24 degrees C in season, and most of these beaches are at their best from June to September. 24 of the 40 have easy access, and 20 are hidden gems.

Save on Pinterest

1Baia di Ieranto

Massa Lubrense, Campania · 4.7 / 5 · Challenging access · about 24 °C

A secluded cove on the Sorrentine peninsula, set between steep rocky headlands with deep turquoise water and a rugged, unspoilt shoreline.

2Benagil Cove

Algarve, Portugal · 4.7 / 5 · Moderate access · about 22 °C

A small cove leading to a sea cave with a skylight in its roof — best seen by kayak or paddleboard.

3Cala d'Hort

Sant Josep, Ibiza · 4.7 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

A small, beautifully framed cove in south-west Ibiza where the dramatic rock of Es Vedra rises from the sea just offshore.

4Sarakiniko

Milos, Greece · 4.7 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

A lunar shoreline of smooth white volcanic rock sculpted by wind and sea, with deep clear water for swimming from the rocks.

5Cala Azzurra, Favignana

Favignana, Egadi Islands, Sicily · 4.6 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Cala Azzurra is a small cove on the island of Favignana in the Egadi archipelago off west Sicily, with a narrow sand and pebble beach, pale rock and very clear turquoise water.

6Cala degli Inglesi

San Domino, Tremiti Islands · 4.6 / 5 · Moderate access · about 24 °C

A rocky cove on San Domino in the Tremiti Islands, with clear blue water and a rugged shoreline.

7Calanque de Sugiton

Marseille, Calanques, France · 4.6 / 5 · Challenging access · about 20 °C

A small rocky cove set between limestone cliffs in the Calanques National Park, reached on foot from Marseille.

8Kavourotrypes Beach

Sithonia, Halkidiki · 4.6 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

A string of small coves on the east coast of Sithonia, also known as Orange Beach.

9Kleftiko

Milos, Cyclades, Greece · 4.6 / 5 · Challenging access · about 24 °C

A cluster of white rock arches, caves and sheltered coves on the south-west coast of Milos, once used as a pirate hideout.

10Praia da Marinha

Algarve, Portugal · 4.6 / 5 · Moderate access · about 22 °C

Dramatic golden limestone arches frame this hidden gem on the Algarve coast — one of the most beautiful beaches in all of Portugal.

11Praia Dona Ana

Lagos, Algarve, Portugal · 4.6 / 5 · Easy access · about 21 °C

Golden cliffs, sea stacks and a sheltered turquoise cove, a short walk from Lagos town.

12Tonnara di Scopello

Scopello, Castellammare del Golfo, Sicily · 4.6 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Tonnara di Scopello is a small cove on the north-west coast of Sicily, with an old tuna fishery building, tall sea stacks called faraglioni just offshore and very clear water.

13Blue Lagoon

Comino, Malta · 4.5 / 5 · Moderate access · about 25 °C

A sheltered bay of vivid turquoise water between Comino and the islet of Cominotto.

14Cala Deia

Deia, Mallorca · 4.5 / 5 · Moderate access · about 24 °C

A tiny pebble and rock cove at the end of a narrow valley below the village of Deia, with very clear water and a fishing-hut feel.

15Kamenjak Beach, Premantura

Premantura, Istria · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

Kamenjak is a protected peninsula at the southern tip of Istria, with rocky coves, small pebble beaches and low pine scrub.

16Lokrum Island, Dubrovnik

Lokrum, Dubrovnik · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

Lokrum is a small wooded island a short boat ride from the old port of Dubrovnik, with rocky shores, a botanical garden and a ruined monastery.

17Palmizana Beach, Pakleni Islands

Palmizana, Sveti Klement, near Hvar · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

Palmizana is a bay on Sveti Klement, the largest of the Pakleni Islands off Hvar, with a pine-shaded shore of rock slabs and small pebble coves, a marina and a botanical garden.

18St Peter's Pool

Marsaxlokk, Malta · 4.5 / 5 · Moderate access · about 24 °C

A natural rock pool on the Delimara peninsula, with clear water, pale limestone ledges and no sand.

19Wied il-Ghasri, Gozo

Gharb, Gozo · 4.5 / 5 · Moderate access · about 25 °C

Wied il-Ghasri is a narrow inlet cut into the cliffs on the north-west coast of Gozo, reached by steps down the gorge.

20Anthony Quinn Bay

Rhodes, Dodecanese · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 23 °C

A small, sheltered bay of clear turquoise water enclosed by pine-covered rocks near Faliraki on Rhodes.

21Cala Rossa

Favignana, Egadi Islands, Sicily · 4.4 / 5 · Moderate access · about 25 °C

A rocky cove on the island of Favignana in the Egadi Islands, with pale tufa cliffs and clear turquoise water.

22Cikat Bay, Losinj

Cikat, Mali Losinj, Losinj · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

Cikat is a long, sheltered bay beside Mali Losinj, with pine and tamarisk trees casting shade along a shore of rock platforms and small pebble beaches.

23Ghar Lapsi, Siggiewi

Siggiewi, Malta · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Ghar Lapsi is a small rocky inlet on the south-west coast of Malta near Siggiewi, with ladders and flat rock platforms for swimming and no sand.

24Marathias Beach, Zakynthos

Marathias, Keri, Zakynthos · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Marathias is a small bay near Keri on the south-west coast of Zakynthos, framed by white limestone rocks and shallow turquoise water.

25Pinizule Beach

Premantura, Istria, Croatia · 4.4 / 5 · Moderate access · about 23 °C

A small rocky cove on the Istrian coast with clear water, flat stone slabs and pine trees reaching down to the shore.

26Porto Limnionas, Zakynthos

Porto Limnionas, Zakynthos · 4.4 / 5 · Moderate access · about 24 °C

Porto Limnionas is a narrow rocky inlet on the north-west coast of Zakynthos, with clear deep water and no sand.

27Porto Selvaggio

Puglia · 4.4 / 5 · Challenging access · about 25 °C

A rocky cove in a regional park on the Salento coast, with pine woods and turquoise water.

28Xlendi Bay, Gozo

Xlendi, Gozo, Malta · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 26 °C

Xlendi is a narrow bay on the south-west coast of Gozo, with steep golden cliffs on both sides and a small beach at the head.

29Cavtat Beach

Cavtat, Dubrovnik Riviera · 4.3 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

Cavtat is a small old town south of Dubrovnik, with rock platforms, small pebble beaches and a long coastal path.

30Suha Punta Beach, Rab

Suha Punta, Rab · 4.3 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Suha Punta is a pine-covered peninsula on the island of Rab, with rocky and pebbly coves, flat stone platforms and clear water.

31Xigia Beach

Zakynthos, Ionian Islands · 4.3 / 5 · Moderate access · about 24 °C

A small sandy and rocky cove on the north-east coast of Zakynthos, known for its sulphur springs, cloudy-turquoise water and pale cliffs.

32Ambrela Beach, Verudela

Verudela, Pula, Istria · 4.2 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

Ambrela is a beach on the Verudela peninsula, a pine-covered resort area south of Pula.

33Marsalforn Bay, Gozo

Marsalforn, Gozo · 4.2 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Marsalforn is a small town on the north coast of Gozo with a sheltered bay, a small beach of sand and pebbles and a promenade lined with restaurants and cafes.

34Opatija Beach

Opatija, Kvarner · 4.2 / 5 · Easy access · about 23 °C

Opatija is the grandest Habsburg resort on the Adriatic, with swimming from stone platforms, small pebble coves and bathing piers along the Lungomare promenade.

35Zukovica Beach

Montenegro coast · 4.2 / 5 · Moderate access · about 24 °C

A small cove on the Montenegrin coast with a narrow shore of pebbles and rocks, wooded slopes behind and clear water.

36Anchor Bay, Popeye Village

Popeye Village, Mellieha · 4.1 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Anchor Bay is a small pebble and rock bay below Popeye Village on the north-west coast of Malta, framed by cliffs and the colourful film-set village.

37Porec Beach

Porec, Istria · 4.1 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

Porec has stone platforms, pebbles and small concrete lidos around its old town, with clear and calm Adriatic water.

38Qbajjar Bay, Gozo

Gozo · 4.1 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Qbajjar Bay is a rocky bay on the north coast of Gozo near Marsalforn, next to the old salt pans.

39Fond Ghadir, Sliema

Sliema, Malta · 4.0 / 5 · Easy access · about 26 °C

Fond Ghadir is a rocky swim spot on the Sliema seafront in Malta, with flat platforms, ladders and clear water beside the promenade.

40Marsaskala Bay, Malta

Marsaskala, Southern Harbour, Malta · 4.0 / 5 · Easy access · about 26 °C

Marsaskala is a fishing village on a long narrow bay in the south-east of Malta, with colourful boats in the water, a promenade and a small town beach.

Frequently asked questions

Which are the top-rated rocky coves in Europe?

Our top three are Baia di Ieranto (4.7 out of 5), Benagil Cove (4.7 out of 5), Cala d'Hort (4.7 out of 5). Scores are editorial ratings from the EuroShores team.

When is the best time to visit these beaches?

Most are at their best from June to September. Water temperatures average about 24 degrees C in season. Each beach page lists its exact season.

Which of these beaches are easiest to reach?

Cala d'Hort, Sarakiniko, Cala Azzurra, Favignana, Kavourotrypes Beach, Praia Dona Ana and 19 more have easy access. The others need a walk, stairs or a rougher road, so check the access note on each page.
