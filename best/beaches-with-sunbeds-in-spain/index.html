Best Beaches with Sunbeds in Spain

We rank the top 40 of 101 beaches with sunbeds and umbrellas in Spain. Cala Macarella (Spain) comes first with 4.8 / 5, followed by Ses Illetes, Cala d'Hort.

The sea reaches about 23 degrees C in season, and most of these beaches are at their best from June to September. 36 of the 40 have easy access, and 5 are hidden gems.

1Cala Macarella

Ciutadella, Menorca, Spain · 4.8 / 5 · Moderate access · about 23 °C

A pale sand cove with turquoise water, enclosed by pine-covered cliffs on the south coast of Menorca.

2Ses Illetes

Formentera, Spain · 4.8 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

A long strip of white sand and shallow, almost Caribbean-coloured water on the northern tip of Formentera.

3Cala d'Hort

Sant Josep, Ibiza · 4.7 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

A small, beautifully framed cove in south-west Ibiza where the dramatic rock of Es Vedra rises from the sea just offshore.

4Maspalomas Dunes

Maspalomas, Gran Canaria · 4.7 / 5 · Moderate access · about 22 °C

A protected field of golden dunes at the southern tip of Gran Canaria, running down to a wide Atlantic beach.

5Playa de Sotavento

Jandia, Fuerteventura · 4.7 / 5 · Moderate access · about 22 °C

A very long beach of pale sand on the south of Fuerteventura, with a shallow turquoise lagoon and a sandbar that changes with the tides.

6Cala Galdana

Cala Galdana, Ferreries, Menorca · 4.6 / 5 · Easy access · about 23 °C

A half-crescent cove of white sand and calm, shallow water on the south coast of Menorca, ringed by pine-covered cliffs.

7Cala Santanyi

Santanyi, Mallorca · 4.6 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

A small sandy cove tucked between pine-covered cliffs on the south-east coast of Mallorca, with calm turquoise water.

8Cala Saona

Formentera, Balearic Islands, Spain · 4.6 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

A sheltered sandy cove on the west coast of Formentera with clear shallow water and traditional boat houses on the rocks.

9Formentor Beach

Pollensa, Mallorca · 4.6 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

A sheltered bay of pale sand and shallow turquoise water at the foot of the Formentor peninsula in northern Mallorca, backed by pine trees.

10Playa de la Concha

San Sebastian, Basque Country, Spain · 4.6 / 5 · Easy access · about 20 °C

A sheltered, shell-shaped city beach with fine golden sand, a long promenade and a small island in the bay, a short walk from the old town of San Sebastian.

11Sa Tuna Beach, Begur

Sa Tuna, Begur, Girona · 4.6 / 5 · Easy access · about 22 °C

Sa Tuna is a tiny cove near Begur on the Costa Brava, with a small beach of coarse sand and a row of whitewashed fishermen houses along the shore.

12Ses Illetes Beach, Formentera

Ses Illetes, Formentera · 4.6 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Ses Illetes is a narrow peninsula on the north of Formentera, with white sand and shallow water in a Mediterranean turquoise.

13Cala Comte

Sant Josep, Ibiza, Spain · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

Small sandy coves with very clear water and low islets offshore, on the west coast of Ibiza.

14Cala en Porter, Menorca

Cala en Porter, Menorca · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

Cala en Porter is a cove of fine white sand in a narrow ravine on the south coast of Menorca, with turquoise water and tall cliffs on both sides.

15Cala Llombards

Santanyi, Mallorca · 4.5 / 5 · Moderate access · about 24 °C

A small sandy cove on the south-east coast of Mallorca, set between low rocky cliffs and pine trees with clear shallow water.

16Cala Mondragó

Mallorca, Spain · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

A sheltered turquoise cove backed by pines inside a natural park, with the sandy bay of S'Amarador alongside.

17Costa Calma Beach, Fuerteventura

Costa Calma, Pajara, Fuerteventura, Canary Islands · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 23 °C

Costa Calma is a resort on the south of Fuerteventura with a wide beach of golden sand that runs for kilometres along the Sotavento coast.

18El Cotillo Beach

El Cotillo, Fuerteventura · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 22 °C

A wide sandy beach near the fishing village of El Cotillo on the north-west coast of Fuerteventura, known for clear turquoise water, steady wind and windsurfers.

19Platja de Migjorn, Formentera

Migjorn, Formentera · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Platja de Migjorn is a long sandy beach on the south coast of Formentera, running for several kilometres.

20Platja Gran, Tossa de Mar

Tossa de Mar, Costa Brava · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 22 °C

Platja Gran is the main beach of Tossa de Mar, a crescent of golden sand overlooked by the medieval walls and towers of the Vila Vella.

21Playa de Amadores, Gran Canaria

Amadores, Mogan, Gran Canaria, Canary Islands · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

Playa de Amadores is a curved bay of white sand on the south-west coast of Gran Canaria, protected by breakwaters that keep the water calm and clear.

22Playa de la Barrosa

Chiclana de la Frontera, Andalusia · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 21 °C

An 8 km sweep of fine golden sand on the Costa de la Luz, backed by pine woods and a low red cliff at the north end.

23Playa de la Fontanilla

Conil de la Frontera, Cadiz · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 21 °C

La Fontanilla is the main beach of Conil de la Frontera, a wide sweep of fine golden sand with shallow Atlantic water and a promenade.

24Playa de las Canteras

Las Palmas, Gran Canaria · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 22 °C

A long, sheltered city beach in Las Palmas, protected by a natural offshore reef that keeps the water calm for most of the year.

25Playa de las Teresitas

San Andres, Tenerife, Canary Islands, Spain · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 21 °C

A long curved golden beach on the northeast coast of Tenerife, with the Anaga Mountains behind and a breakwater that calms the water.

26Playa de Ondarreta

Ondarreta, San Sebastian, Basque Country · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 22 °C

Ondarreta is the quieter of the two city beaches of San Sebastian, set at the western end of the bay below Monte Igueldo.

27Playa de Ris

Noja, Cantabria · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 20 °C

A broad golden beach in Noja, Cantabria, with a long gentle shore, good swimming and dunes and green hills behind.

28Playa de Zahara de los Atunes

Zahara de los Atunes, Costa de la Luz · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 22 °C

A long, wide beach of fine pale sand on Spain's Atlantic coast, with big skies and strong evening light.

29Cala Agulla

Capdepera, Mallorca · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 23 °C

A wide sandy bay on the north-east coast of Mallorca, backed by pine forest and dunes.

30Cala Bassa

Sant Josep de sa Talaia, Ibiza · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

A wide sandy bay on the west coast of Ibiza with calm, shallow water backed by pine trees.

31Cala Benirras

Sant Joan, Ibiza · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

A sheltered cove on the north coast of Ibiza with a small beach of sand and pebbles, framed by pine-covered hills and the Cap Bernat rock stack.

32Cala Ferrera, Mallorca

Cala Ferrera, Felanitx, Mallorca · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Cala Ferrera is a small cove of fine sand on the east coast of Mallorca, with clear turquoise water and pine trees on the slopes above.

33Cala Salada

Sant Antoni, Ibiza, Spain · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

A sheltered cove on the west coast of Ibiza, with clear green-blue water and pine-covered slopes.

34Cala Tarida

Sant Josep, Ibiza, Balearic Islands · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

A west-facing bay on Ibiza with clear water and a curve of sand, popular for evening sunsets.

35Canyamel Beach

Canyamel, Capdepera, Mallorca · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 23 °C

A small sandy beach of roughly 300 m at the mouth of a green valley on the east coast of Mallorca.

36Platja de Muro

Muro, Mallorca · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

A long beach of fine sand in the Bay of Alcudia, backed by dunes and an old watchtower.

37Platja de Muro, Mallorca

Playa de Muro, Alcudia, Mallorca · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Platja de Muro is a long white-sand beach on Alcudia Bay in north-east Mallorca, with shallow warm water and pine trees behind the dunes.

38Playa Burriana

Nerja, Costa del Sol, Andalusia · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 22 °C

The most popular beach in Nerja, about 800 m long and backed by beach bars and restaurants.

39Playa de Cabopino

Marbella, Costa del Sol · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 22 °C

A sandy beach on the Costa del Sol beside the small Cabopino marina and an old watchtower, backed by pine woods and dunes.

40Playa de El Medano, Tenerife

El Medano, Granadilla de Abona, Tenerife, Canary Islands · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 23 °C

El Medano is a seaside town on the south-east coast of Tenerife, with a long beach of golden sand beside the volcanic cone of Montana Roja.

Frequently asked questions

Which are the top-rated beaches with sunbeds and umbrellas in Spain?

Our top three are Cala Macarella (4.8 out of 5), Ses Illetes (4.8 out of 5), Cala d'Hort (4.7 out of 5). Scores are editorial ratings from the EuroShores team.

When is the best time to visit these beaches?

Most are at their best from June to September. Water temperatures average about 23 degrees C in season. Each beach page lists its exact season.

Which of these beaches are easiest to reach?

Ses Illetes, Cala d'Hort, Cala Galdana, Cala Santanyi, Cala Saona and 31 more have easy access. The others need a walk, stairs or a rougher road, so check the access note on each page.
