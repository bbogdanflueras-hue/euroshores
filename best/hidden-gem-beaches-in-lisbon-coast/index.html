Hidden Gem Beaches in the Lisbon coast

We rank the 9 hidden gem beaches in the Lisbon coast. Portinho da Arrabida (Portugal) comes first with 4.7 / 5, followed by Praia da Ursa, Praia da Figueirinha, Setubal.

The sea reaches about 20 degrees C in season, and most of these beaches are at their best from June to September. 6 of the 9 have easy access, and 9 are hidden gems.

Save on Pinterest

1Portinho da Arrabida

Portinho da Arrabida, Setubal · 4.7 / 5 · Moderate access · about 23 °C

Portinho da Arrabida is a small sheltered bay in the Arrabida Natural Park, with white sand, calm turquoise water and a green mountain rising behind.

2Praia da Ursa

Sintra-Cascais Natural Park, Portugal · 4.7 / 5 · Challenging access · about 17 °C

A small, wild sand cove below steep cliffs near Cabo da Roca, known for its tall rock stacks and rough Atlantic surf.

3Praia da Figueirinha, Setubal

Arrabida, Setubal · 4.6 / 5 · Easy access · about 22 °C

Praia da Figueirinha is a calm sand beach in the Arrabida natural park, with turquoise water and green hills behind.

4Praia de Galapos, Arrabida

Galapos, Setubal, Arrabida · 4.6 / 5 · Moderate access · about 22 °C

Praia de Galapos is a sheltered bay of pale sand and fine pebbles below the green Arrabida mountains south of Lisbon.

5Praia Atlantica, Troia

Troia, Setubal · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 22 °C

Troia is a long sand peninsula south of Lisbon, with wide Atlantic beaches, white dunes and clear water.

6Praia da Fonte da Telha, Costa da Caparica

Fonte da Telha, Almada, Setubal · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 20 °C

Praia da Fonte da Telha is a long and wide beach of golden sand at the southern end of the Costa da Caparica, backed by sandy cliffs and a protected landscape.

7Praia do Magoito, Sintra

Magoito, Sintra, Lisbon Coast · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 19 °C

Praia do Magoito is a small sandy cove between golden sandstone cliffs on the Sintra coast, west of Lisbon.

8Praia da Lagoa de Albufeira, Sesimbra

Lagoa de Albufeira, Sesimbra, Setubal · 4.3 / 5 · Easy access · about 20 °C

Praia da Lagoa de Albufeira is a wide beach on the Atlantic coast south of Lisbon, beside a lagoon that opens to the sea in winter.

9Praia de Santa Rita, Torres Vedras

Santa Rita, Torres Vedras, Lisbon Region · 4.3 / 5 · Easy access · about 19 °C

Praia de Santa Rita is a wide beach of golden sand on the Silver Coast, below low cliffs and fields near Torres Vedras.

Frequently asked questions

Which are the top-rated hidden gem beaches in the Lisbon coast?

Our top three are Portinho da Arrabida (4.7 out of 5), Praia da Ursa (4.7 out of 5), Praia da Figueirinha, Setubal (4.6 out of 5). Scores are editorial ratings from the EuroShores team.

When is the best time to visit these beaches?

Most are at their best from June to September. Water temperatures average about 20 degrees C in season. Each beach page lists its exact season.

Which of these beaches are easiest to reach?

Praia da Figueirinha, Setubal, Praia Atlantica, Troia, Praia da Fonte da Telha, Costa da Caparica, Praia do Magoito, Sintra, Praia da Lagoa de Albufeira, Sesimbra and 1 more have easy access. The others need a walk, stairs or a rougher road, so check the access note on each page.
