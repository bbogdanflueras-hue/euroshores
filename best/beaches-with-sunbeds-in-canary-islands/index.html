Best Beaches with Sunbeds in the Canary Islands

We rank the 12 beaches with sunbeds and umbrellas in the Canary Islands. Maspalomas Dunes (Spain) comes first with 4.7 / 5, followed by Playa de Sotavento, Costa Calma Beach, Fuerteventura.

The sea reaches about 23 degrees C in season, and most of these beaches are at their best from June to September. 10 of the 12 have easy access, and 2 are hidden gems.

Save on Pinterest

1Maspalomas Dunes

Maspalomas, Gran Canaria · 4.7 / 5 · Moderate access · about 22 °C

A protected field of golden dunes at the southern tip of Gran Canaria, running down to a wide Atlantic beach.

2Playa de Sotavento

Jandia, Fuerteventura · 4.7 / 5 · Moderate access · about 22 °C

A very long beach of pale sand on the south of Fuerteventura, with a shallow turquoise lagoon and a sandbar that changes with the tides.

3Costa Calma Beach, Fuerteventura

Costa Calma, Pajara, Fuerteventura, Canary Islands · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 23 °C

Costa Calma is a resort on the south of Fuerteventura with a wide beach of golden sand that runs for kilometres along the Sotavento coast.

4El Cotillo Beach

El Cotillo, Fuerteventura · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 22 °C

A wide sandy beach near the fishing village of El Cotillo on the north-west coast of Fuerteventura, known for clear turquoise water, steady wind and windsurfers.

5Playa de Amadores, Gran Canaria

Amadores, Mogan, Gran Canaria, Canary Islands · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

Playa de Amadores is a curved bay of white sand on the south-west coast of Gran Canaria, protected by breakwaters that keep the water calm and clear.

6Playa de las Canteras

Las Palmas, Gran Canaria · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 22 °C

A long, sheltered city beach in Las Palmas, protected by a natural offshore reef that keeps the water calm for most of the year.

7Playa de las Teresitas

San Andres, Tenerife, Canary Islands, Spain · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 21 °C

A long curved golden beach on the northeast coast of Tenerife, with the Anaga Mountains behind and a breakwater that calms the water.

8Playa de El Medano, Tenerife

El Medano, Granadilla de Abona, Tenerife, Canary Islands · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 23 °C

El Medano is a seaside town on the south-east coast of Tenerife, with a long beach of golden sand beside the volcanic cone of Montana Roja.

9Playa de Mogan, Gran Canaria

Puerto de Mogan, Gran Canaria, Canary Islands · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

Playa de Mogan is a small beach of golden sand beside the harbour of Puerto de Mogan, a village of white houses, flowers and canals on the south-west coast of Gran Canaria.

10Playa del Ingles, Gran Canaria

Playa del Ingles, San Bartolome de Tirajana, Gran Canaria · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

Playa del Ingles is a wide beach of golden sand on the south coast of Gran Canaria, next to the large dune field of Maspalomas.

11Playa de Los Cristianos, Tenerife

Los Cristianos, Arona, Tenerife, Canary Islands · 4.2 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

Playa de Los Cristianos is a sheltered beach of golden sand in the port town of Los Cristianos on the south coast of Tenerife.

12Playa de Las Americas, Tenerife

Playa de las Americas, Adeje, Tenerife, Canary Islands · 4.1 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

Playa de Las Americas is the biggest resort area on the south coast of Tenerife, with several beaches of dark and golden sand, a long promenade and many hotels, bars and restaurants.

Frequently asked questions

Which are the top-rated beaches with sunbeds and umbrellas in the Canary Islands?

Our top three are Maspalomas Dunes (4.7 out of 5), Playa de Sotavento (4.7 out of 5), Costa Calma Beach, Fuerteventura (4.5 out of 5). Scores are editorial ratings from the EuroShores team.

When is the best time to visit these beaches?

Most are at their best from June to September. Water temperatures average about 23 degrees C in season. Each beach page lists its exact season.

Which of these beaches are easiest to reach?

Costa Calma Beach, Fuerteventura, El Cotillo Beach, Playa de Amadores, Gran Canaria, Playa de las Canteras, Playa de las Teresitas and 5 more have easy access. The others need a walk, stairs or a rougher road, so check the access note on each page.
