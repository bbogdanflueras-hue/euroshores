Easiest Beaches to Reach in Portugal

We rank the top 40 of 87 easy-access beaches in Portugal. Portinho da Arrabida (Portugal) comes first with 4.7 / 5, followed by Praia da Bordeira, Praia da Senhora da Rocha, Porches.

The sea reaches about 21 degrees C in season, and most of these beaches are at their best from June to September. 40 of the 40 have easy access, and 15 are hidden gems.

Save on Pinterest

1Portinho da Arrabida

Portinho da Arrabida, Setubal · 4.7 / 5 · Easy access · about 23 °C

Portinho da Arrabida is a small sheltered bay in the Arrabida Natural Park, with white sand, calm turquoise water and a green mountain rising behind.

2Praia da Bordeira

Carrapateira, Algarve · 4.7 / 5 · Easy access · about 21 °C

A wide, wild Atlantic beach on the west Algarve coast with dunes, cliffs and a shallow lagoon that forms behind the sand.

3Praia da Senhora da Rocha, Porches

Senhora da Rocha, Porches, Algarve · 4.7 / 5 · Easy access · about 22 °C

Praia da Senhora da Rocha is a golden cove in the central Algarve, with a small white chapel on the headland above the sand.

4Porto Santo Beach

Porto Santo, Madeira · 4.6 / 5 · Easy access · about 22 °C

A nine-kilometre golden beach on the island of Porto Santo, with calm, shallow, clear water.

5Praia da Comporta

Alentejo coast, Portugal · 4.6 / 5 · Easy access · about 20 °C

A long, quiet stretch of pale sand backed by dunes and pine woods on the Alentejo coast.

6Praia da Figueirinha, Setubal

Arrabida, Setubal · 4.6 / 5 · Easy access · about 22 °C

Praia da Figueirinha is a calm sand beach in the Arrabida natural park, with turquoise water and green hills behind.

7Praia de Cacela Velha

Cacela Velha, Algarve · 4.6 / 5 · Easy access · about 23 °C

Cacela Velha is a tiny whitewashed village on a bluff in the eastern Algarve, with a beach on a sandbar facing the Ria Formosa lagoon.

8Praia de Galapos, Arrabida

Galapos, Setubal, Arrabida · 4.6 / 5 · Easy access · about 22 °C

Praia de Galapos is a sheltered bay of pale sand and fine pebbles below the green Arrabida mountains south of Lisbon.

9Praia de Odeceixe

Odeceixe, Algarve, Portugal · 4.6 / 5 · Easy access · about 18 °C

A horseshoe shaped beach on the west coast of the Algarve where the Ribeira de Seixe river curls behind the sand to meet the Atlantic, with calm river pools on one side and surf on the other.

10Praia do Amado

Carrapateira, Aljezur, Algarve, Portugal · 4.6 / 5 · Easy access · about 19 °C

A broad sandy beach with hills behind it on the west coast of the Algarve, popular with surfers.

11Praia do Monte Clerigo

Monte Clerigo, Aljezur, Algarve · 4.6 / 5 · Easy access · about 19 °C

Monte Clerigo is a small village beach on the Atlantic coast of the Algarve near Aljezur, with a wide stretch of pale sand between rock outcrops and steep green slopes.

12Praia Dona Ana

Lagos, Algarve, Portugal · 4.6 / 5 · Easy access · about 21 °C

Golden cliffs, sea stacks and a sheltered turquoise cove, a short walk from Lagos town.

13Meia Praia

Lagos, Algarve, Portugal · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 20 °C

A very long sandy beach east of Lagos, backed by dunes and reached by wooden walkways.

14Praia Atlantica, Troia

Troia, Setubal · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 22 °C

Troia is a long sand peninsula south of Lisbon, with wide Atlantic beaches, white dunes and clear water.

15Praia da Adraga

Sintra-Cascais Natural Park, Portugal · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 17 °C

A golden Atlantic beach below eroded cliffs and rock arches on the Sintra coast, with strong surf and a beach restaurant.

16Praia da Amoreira, Aljezur

Amoreira, Aljezur, Costa Vicentina · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 18 °C

Praia da Amoreira is a wide beach on the Costa Vicentina near Aljezur, where a river and a lagoon meet the Atlantic below low cliffs.

17Praia da Cordoama, Vila do Bispo

Cordoama, Vila do Bispo, Algarve · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 20 °C

Praia da Cordoama is a long, open Atlantic beach near Vila do Bispo in the western Algarve, backed by cliffs and dunes.

18Praia da Figueira da Foz

Figueira da Foz, Centro · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 19 °C

The beach of Figueira da Foz is famously wide, with a long stretch of fine golden sand beside the mouth of the Mondego river.

19Praia da Foz do Arelho

Foz do Arelho, Silver Coast · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 19 °C

A wide sandy beach on the Silver Coast where the calm Obidos lagoon meets the open Atlantic, so you can pick between gentle lagoon water and ocean waves.

20Praia da Ilha do Pessegueiro

Porto Covo, Alentejo · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 20 °C

A sheltered sandy beach on the Alentejo coast looking out at Pessegueiro Island and its small fort.

21Praia da Luz

Lagos, Algarve, Portugal · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 20 °C

A sandy beach beside a small Algarve village near Lagos, with rocky outcrops and a seafront walk.

22Praia da Prainha, Alvor

Prainha, Alvor, Portimao, Algarve · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 22 °C

Praia da Prainha is a small cove of golden sand between limestone cliffs near Alvor in the Algarve.

23Praia de Melides, Alentejo

Melides, Grandola, Alentejo · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 21 °C

Praia de Melides is a wide, wild beach of pale sand on the Alentejo coast, with a freshwater lagoon behind the dunes and almost no development.

24Praia do Carvoeiro

Lagoa, Algarve · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 22 °C

A small golden beach set in a cove below the whitewashed village of Carvoeiro, with cliffs on each side.

25Praia do Evaristo

Albufeira, Algarve · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 22 °C

A long golden beach with red cliffs on the western side of Albufeira, with good swimming and a natural rock pool at low tide.

26Praia do Martinhal, Sagres

Sagres, Algarve · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 21 °C

Praia do Martinhal is a sheltered bay of fine sand near Sagres on the far west of the Algarve, with rock pools at low tide and calmer water than the Atlantic coast.

27Praia do Zavial

Raposeira, Vila do Bispo, Algarve · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 21 °C

A broad sandy bay on the western Algarve, backed by low cliffs and a beachfront restaurant.

28Praia da Costa da Caparica

Costa da Caparica, Lisbon · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 21 °C

Costa da Caparica is a long stretch of wide Atlantic beach just across the river from Lisbon, with fine sand, big waves and beach bars.

29Praia da Costa Nova

Aveiro · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 19 °C

A long Atlantic beach beside the colourful striped houses of Costa Nova near Aveiro, with wide golden sand and dunes.

30Praia da Fonte da Telha, Costa da Caparica

Fonte da Telha, Almada, Setubal · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 20 °C

Praia da Fonte da Telha is a long and wide beach of golden sand at the southern end of the Costa da Caparica, backed by sandy cliffs and a protected landscape.

31Praia da Fuseta, Algarve

Fuseta, Olhao, Algarve · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 23 °C

Praia da Fuseta is a long, quiet beach of fine sand on a barrier island in the Ria Formosa lagoon of the eastern Algarve, reached by a short ferry or footbridge from the fishing village of Fuseta.

32Praia da Ingrina

Raposeira, Vila do Bispo, Algarve · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 21 °C

A small sandy cove on the western Algarve near Raposeira, sheltered by rocks.

33Praia da Manta Rota

Manta Rota, Vila Real de Santo Antonio, Algarve · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 22 °C

Manta Rota is a long, wide beach on the eastern Algarve, with fine pale sand, dunes behind and shallow water that is warmer than on the west coast.

34Praia da Mareta, Sagres

Mareta, Sagres, Algarve · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 21 °C

Praia da Mareta is a sandy beach below the ochre cliffs of Sagres in the western Algarve, close to the village centre.

35Praia da Rocha

Portimao, Algarve, Portugal · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 20 °C

A very wide sand beach below golden Algarve cliffs and rock formations, with a long promenade behind.

36Praia da Salema

Salema, Budens, Algarve · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 21 °C

A Blue Flag beach beside the fishing village of Salema on the western Algarve, with golden sand between low cliffs and a line of beachfront restaurants.

37Praia da Zambujeira do Mar

Odemira, Alentejo · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 20 °C

A pretty cove in the Alentejo with a golden beach, dark cliffs and a lively little village on top.

38Praia de Armacao de Pera

Armacao de Pera, Algarve · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 20 °C

Armacao de Pera has one of the longest sandy beaches in the Algarve, stretching for several kilometres below a small resort town.

39Praia de Burgau

Burgau, Algarve · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 21 °C

A small sandy beach in the western Algarve, backed by the whitewashed houses of Burgau village and framed by green hills.

40Praia de Santa Barbara

Ribeira Grande, Azores · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 20 °C

A long, dark volcanic sand beach on the north coast of Sao Miguel, popular with surfers.

Frequently asked questions

Which are the top-rated easy-access beaches in Portugal?

Our top three are Portinho da Arrabida (4.7 out of 5), Praia da Bordeira (4.7 out of 5), Praia da Senhora da Rocha, Porches (4.7 out of 5). Scores are editorial ratings from the EuroShores team.

When is the best time to visit these beaches?

Most are at their best from June to September. Water temperatures average about 21 degrees C in season. Each beach page lists its exact season.

Which of these beaches are easiest to reach?

Portinho da Arrabida, Praia da Bordeira, Praia da Senhora da Rocha, Porches, Porto Santo Beach, Praia da Comporta and 35 more have easy access. The others need a walk, stairs or a rougher road, so check the access note on each page.
