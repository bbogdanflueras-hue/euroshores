Easiest Beaches to Reach in Malta and Gozo

We rank the 13 easy-access beaches in Malta and Gozo. Ramla Bay (Malta) comes first with 4.7 / 5, followed by Golden Bay, Ghadira Bay.

The sea reaches about 25 degrees C in season, and most of these beaches are at their best from June to September. 13 of the 13 have easy access, and 2 are hidden gems.

Save on Pinterest

1Ramla Bay

Xaghra, Gozo, Malta · 4.7 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

A wide sandy beach on Gozo known for its reddish-orange sand, with Calypso's Cave in the cliffs above.

2Golden Bay

Mellieha, Malta · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

A wide sandy bay on the northwest coast of Malta, backed by cliffs, with the amenities for a full beach day.

3Ghadira Bay

Mellieha, Malta · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

A long sandy bay that is often described as Malta's largest beach, with shallow water that stays calm well out from shore.

4Ghar Lapsi, Siggiewi

Siggiewi, Malta · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Ghar Lapsi is a small rocky inlet on the south-west coast of Malta near Siggiewi, with ladders and flat rock platforms for swimming and no sand.

5Gnejna Bay

Mgarr, Malta · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

A sandy bay on the north-west coast of Malta, framed by low cliffs and known for its traditional boathouses.

6Xlendi Bay, Gozo

Xlendi, Gozo, Malta · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 26 °C

Xlendi is a narrow bay on the south-west coast of Gozo, with steep golden cliffs on both sides and a small beach at the head.

7Armier Bay, Malta

Armier, Mellieha, Malta · 4.2 / 5 · Easy access · about 26 °C

Armier Bay is a sandy bay in the north-west of Malta near Mellieha, with shallow water and a view of the island of Comino across the channel.

8Marsalforn Bay, Gozo

Marsalforn, Gozo · 4.2 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Marsalforn is a small town on the north coast of Gozo with a sheltered bay, a small beach of sand and pebbles and a promenade lined with restaurants and cafes.

9Mellieha Bay

Mellieha, Malta · 4.2 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

One of the longest sandy beaches in Malta, with shallow water that suits families and a wide bay facing the open sea.

10Qbajjar Bay, Gozo

Gozo · 4.1 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Qbajjar Bay is a rocky bay on the north coast of Gozo near Marsalforn, next to the old salt pans.

11Fond Ghadir, Sliema

Sliema, Malta · 4.0 / 5 · Easy access · about 26 °C

Fond Ghadir is a rocky swim spot on the Sliema seafront in Malta, with flat platforms, ladders and clear water beside the promenade.

12Marsaskala Bay, Malta

Marsaskala, Southern Harbour, Malta · 4.0 / 5 · Easy access · about 26 °C

Marsaskala is a fishing village on a long narrow bay in the south-east of Malta, with colourful boats in the water, a promenade and a small town beach.

13Pretty Bay

Birzebbuga, Malta · 4.0 / 5 · Easy access · about 23 °C

A sandy bay beside the town of Birzebbuga in the south of Malta, with a promenade and views across the harbour.

Frequently asked questions

Which are the top-rated easy-access beaches in Malta and Gozo?

Our top three are Ramla Bay (4.7 out of 5), Golden Bay (4.5 out of 5), Ghadira Bay (4.4 out of 5). Scores are editorial ratings from the EuroShores team.

When is the best time to visit these beaches?

Most are at their best from June to September. Water temperatures average about 25 degrees C in season. Each beach page lists its exact season.

Which of these beaches are easiest to reach?

Ramla Bay, Golden Bay, Ghadira Bay, Ghar Lapsi, Siggiewi, Gnejna Bay and 8 more have easy access. The others need a walk, stairs or a rougher road, so check the access note on each page.
