Best Beaches in the Dodecanese in June

We rank the 15 beaches to visit in the Dodecanese in June. Apella Beach (Greece) comes first with 4.6 / 5, followed by Prasonisi Beach, Rhodes, Tsambika Beach.

The sea reaches about 25 degrees C in season, and most of these beaches are at their best from June to September. 14 of the 15 have easy access, and 3 are hidden gems.

Save on Pinterest

1Apella Beach

Apella, Karpathos · 4.6 / 5 · Challenging access · about 23 °C

A pretty bay of white pebbles and very clear water on the east coast of Karpathos, set under green hills.

2Prasonisi Beach, Rhodes

Prasonisi, Rhodes, Dodecanese · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Prasonisi is a sandy spit at the far southern tip of Rhodes, where a narrow sandbar joins the main island to a small rocky islet.

3Tsambika Beach

Rhodes, Greece · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

A long golden-sand crescent on the east coast of Rhodes, backed by a rocky headland and a hilltop monastery, with warm shallow water and a lively line of beach tavernas.

4Anthony Quinn Bay

Rhodes, Dodecanese · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 23 °C

A small, sheltered bay of clear turquoise water enclosed by pine-covered rocks near Faliraki on Rhodes.

5Lindos Beach

Rhodes · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

A sheltered curved bay beside Lindos on Rhodes, with rows of sunbeds along the sand and calm turquoise water.

6Glystra Beach, Rhodes

Lardos, Rhodes · 4.3 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Glystra is a small, quiet cove on the Lardos coast of Rhodes, with fine sand and pebbles and clear blue water below dry scrub hills.

7Kefalos Beach, Kos

Kefalos, Kos, Dodecanese · 4.3 / 5 · Easy access · about 26 °C

Kefalos is a long, open bay of sand on the south-west coast of Kos, with the small islet of Kastri and its white chapel of Agios Nikolaos at the western end.

8Livadi Beach, Astypalaia

Livadi, Astypalaia, Dodecanese · 4.3 / 5 · Easy access · about 26 °C

Livadi is the main beach of Astypalaia, a green valley bay with pebbles and sand below the whitewashed Chora and its Venetian castle.

9Mastichari Beach, Kos

Mastichari, Kos, Dodecanese · 4.3 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Mastichari is a small fishing village on the north-west coast of Kos, with a long beach of sand and fine pebbles, shallow water and a gentle slope into the sea.

10Kolymbia Beach, Rhodes

Kolymbia, Rhodes · 4.2 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Kolymbia has a long beach of sand and fine pebbles on the east coast of Rhodes, with calm shallow water and umbrellas along the shore.

11Stegna Beach, Rhodes

Stegna, Rhodes · 4.2 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Stegna is a small bay of sand and fine pebbles on the east coast of Rhodes, sheltered by a rocky headland.

12Faliraki Beach

Faliraki, Rhodes · 4.1 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

Faliraki is a long sandy beach on the east coast of Rhodes, with shallow clear water and a wide range of water sports.

13Kardamena Beach, Kos

Kardamena, Kos · 4.1 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Kardamena has a long beach of light sand on the south coast of Kos, with thatched parasols, sunbeds and warm shallow water.

14Marmari Beach, Kos

Marmari, Kos · 4.1 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Marmari is a long, wide beach of fine sand on the north coast of Kos, backed by low dunes and tamarisk trees.

15Tigaki Beach

Tigaki, Kos, Greece · 4.1 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

A long, gently shelving sandy beach on the north coast of Kos, with shallow water that suits families.

Frequently asked questions

Which are the top-rated beaches to visit in the Dodecanese in June?

Our top three are Apella Beach (4.6 out of 5), Prasonisi Beach, Rhodes (4.5 out of 5), Tsambika Beach (4.5 out of 5). Scores are editorial ratings from the EuroShores team.

When is the best time to visit these beaches?

Most are at their best from June to September. Water temperatures average about 25 degrees C in season. Each beach page lists its exact season.

Which of these beaches are easiest to reach?

Prasonisi Beach, Rhodes, Tsambika Beach, Anthony Quinn Bay, Lindos Beach, Glystra Beach, Rhodes and 9 more have easy access. The others need a walk, stairs or a rougher road, so check the access note on each page.
