Best Sandy Beaches in Croatia

We rank the 10 sandy beaches in Croatia. Rajska Plaza (Croatia) comes first with 4.6 / 5, followed by Sakarun Beach, Saplunara.

The sea reaches about 25 degrees C in season, and most of these beaches are at their best from June to September. 7 of the 10 have easy access, and 4 are hidden gems.

Save on Pinterest

1Rajska Plaza

Lopar, Rab, Croatia · 4.6 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Also called Paradise Beach, the best-known beach on Rab, a long stretch of brown sand where the warm, shallow water reaches far out.

2Sakarun Beach

Dugi Otok, Croatia · 4.6 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

A long, shallow, white-sand bay in the Kornati archipelago — rare for the Adriatic and ideal for families.

3Saplunara

Mljet, Croatia · 4.6 / 5 · Moderate access · about 25 °C

A rare sandy bay at the southeast end of Mljet, relatively quiet and set in protected surroundings.

4Nin Beach

Nin, Zadar County · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Queen Beach (Kraljicina plaza) is a sandy beach of more than 2 km on the Nin lagoon near Zadar.

5Porat Beach

Bisevo, Croatia · 4.5 / 5 · Moderate access · about 24 °C

A small sandy bay on the island of Bisevo, with very clear water, a few houses and terraced fields behind.

6Slanica Beach

Murter, Sibenik-Knin, Croatia · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

A sandy bay near the town of Murter with shallow water and pine trees along the shore.

7Sunj Beach

Lopud, Dubrovnik Riviera, Croatia · 4.5 / 5 · Moderate access · about 24 °C

A shallow crescent of sand about 200 metres long, backed by pines on the car free island of Lopud, a rare sandy beach near Dubrovnik.

8Bilin Zal Beach, Lumbarda

Lumbarda, Korcula · 4.3 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

Bilin Zal is a shallow cove of sand and fine pebbles near the village of Lumbarda at the eastern end of Korcula.

9Bačvice

Split, Croatia · 4.2 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Split's city beach, with shallow, sandy-bottomed water and the home of picigin, a ball game played in the shallows.

10Zaton Beach

Zaton, near Nin and Zadar · 4.2 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

A long, shallow beach between Zadar and Nin on the Adriatic, mostly sand with pebbles in places.

Frequently asked questions

Which are the top-rated sandy beaches in Croatia?

Our top three are Rajska Plaza (4.6 out of 5), Sakarun Beach (4.6 out of 5), Saplunara (4.6 out of 5). Scores are editorial ratings from the EuroShores team.

When is the best time to visit these beaches?

Most are at their best from June to September. Water temperatures average about 25 degrees C in season. Each beach page lists its exact season.

Which of these beaches are easiest to reach?

Rajska Plaza, Sakarun Beach, Nin Beach, Slanica Beach, Bilin Zal Beach, Lumbarda and 2 more have easy access. The others need a walk, stairs or a rougher road, so check the access note on each page.
