Hidden Gem Beaches in Greece

We rank the top 40 of 41 hidden gem beaches in Greece. Balos Lagoon (Greece) comes first with 4.8 / 5, followed by Kolona Beach, Lalaria Beach.

The sea reaches about 24 degrees C in season, and most of these beaches are at their best from June to September. 20 of the 40 have easy access, and 40 are hidden gems.

Save on Pinterest

1Balos Lagoon

Crete, Greece · 4.8 / 5 · Moderate access · about 25 °C

A shallow lagoon of pale pink sand and impossible blues at the tip of the Gramvousa peninsula.

2Kolona Beach

Kythnos, Cyclades · 4.7 / 5 · Moderate access · about 24 °C

A narrow strip of fine sand joining the main island of Kythnos to the islet of Agios Loukas, with water on both sides.

3Lalaria Beach

Skiathos · 4.7 / 5 · Challenging access · about 24 °C

A striking beach of smooth white pebbles beneath tall cliffs on the north coast of Skiathos, with sea arches and caves nearby.

4Porto Timoni

Afionas, Corfu · 4.7 / 5 · Challenging access · about 24 °C

Two back-to-back coves on the north-west coast of Corfu, separated by a narrow strip of land.

5Apella Beach

Apella, Karpathos · 4.6 / 5 · Challenging access · about 23 °C

A pretty bay of white pebbles and very clear water on the east coast of Karpathos, set under green hills.

6Glyka Nera Beach

Sfakia, Crete · 4.6 / 5 · Challenging access · about 24 °C

A pebble cove on the south coast of Crete beneath steep cliffs, known for cool freshwater springs that mix with the clear sea.

7Katergo Beach

Katergo, Folegandros · 4.6 / 5 · Challenging access · about 23 °C

A wild, unspoilt cove on the south-east side of Folegandros, backed by tall cliffs and with deep blue water.

8Kavourotrypes Beach

Sithonia, Halkidiki · 4.6 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

A string of small coves on the east coast of Sithonia, also known as Orange Beach.

9Kleftiko

Milos, Cyclades, Greece · 4.6 / 5 · Challenging access · about 24 °C

A cluster of white rock arches, caves and sheltered coves on the south-west coast of Milos, once used as a pirate hideout.

10Mylos Beach

Agios Nikitas, Lefkada · 4.6 / 5 · Moderate access · about 24 °C

A wild, mostly pebbly beach south of Agios Nikitas, framed by steep green hills and bright blue water.

11Myrtiotissa Beach, Corfu

Myrtiotissa, Corfu, Ionian Islands · 4.6 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

Myrtiotissa is a long golden beach on the west coast of Corfu, backed by steep green cliffs and a small monastery above.

12Preveli Beach

Rethymno, Crete, Greece · 4.6 / 5 · Challenging access · about 23 °C

A cove of sand and pebbles where a river meets the sea, below steep cliffs on the south coast of Crete.

13Seitan Limani

Akrotiri, Chania, Crete, Greece · 4.6 / 5 · Challenging access · about 23 °C

A tiny sandy inlet squeezed between steep rock walls on the Akrotiri peninsula, with deep turquoise water.

14Stavros Beach, Crete

Akrotiri, Chania, Crete · 4.6 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Stavros is a small, sheltered bay of fine sand and clear shallow water on the Akrotiri peninsula of Crete.

15Agiofarago Beach, Crete

Agiofarago, Heraklion, south Crete · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

Agiofarago is a small beach at the foot of a narrow limestone gorge on the south coast of Crete, in the Heraklion region near Matala.

16Agios Pavlos Beach, Rethymno

Agios Pavlos, Rethymno, Crete · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Agios Pavlos is a quiet bay on the south coast of Crete, with pale sand dunes, a small chapel and a rocky cape that shelters the water on one side.

17Agios Sostis Beach

Mykonos · 4.5 / 5 · Moderate access · about 23 °C

A quiet sandy beach on the north coast of Mykonos, far from the busy clubs of the south.

18Dafnoudi Beach

Fiskardo area, Kefalonia · 4.5 / 5 · Moderate access · about 24 °C

A small, sheltered pebble cove on the north tip of Kefalonia near Fiskardo, with calm turquoise water and a pine forest behind it.

19Fava Beach, Vourvourou

Vourvourou, Sithonia, Halkidiki · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Fava is a sandy beach at Vourvourou on the Sithonia peninsula of Halkidiki, with calm, shallow water and a view of small wooded islands in the bay.

20Gidaki Beach

Ithaca, Ionian Islands · 4.5 / 5 · Challenging access · about 24 °C

A remote pebble cove on the east coast of Ithaca, backed by pale cliffs and clear blue water.

21Kalamitsi Beach

Kalamitsi, Lefkada · 4.5 / 5 · Challenging access · about 23 °C

A string of pebble coves on the west coast of Lefkada below the village of Kalamitsi, with clear water under steep cliffs.

22Kokkinokastro Beach

Alonnisos, Greece · 4.5 / 5 · Moderate access · about 24 °C

A pebble beach on the east coast of Alonnisos, named for its red cliffs, with rocky islets just offshore.

23Mikro Alyko Beach

Naxos · 4.5 / 5 · Moderate access · about 24 °C

A small sandy cove on the south-west coast of Naxos, with clear turquoise water and a rocky headland on one side.

24Pefkoulia Beach, Lefkada

Pefkoulia, Agios Nikitas, Lefkada · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Pefkoulia is a long beach of golden sand and pebbles on the west coast of Lefkada, below steep green hills near Agios Nikitas.

25Petani Beach

Paliki, Kefalonia · 4.5 / 5 · Moderate access · about 23 °C

A long beach of pale sand and pebbles beneath white limestone cliffs on the west coast of the Paliki peninsula, with clear water and a relaxed atmosphere.

26Psili Ammos Beach, Serifos

Psili Ammos, Serifos, Cyclades · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Psili Ammos is a quiet cove of fine pale sand on the south-east coast of Serifos, with shallow turquoise water and a line of tamarisk trees behind the beach.

27Roukounas Beach, Anafi

Roukounas, Anafi · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

Roukounas is one of the main beaches of Anafi, a small island east of Santorini, with a long curve of sand below a rocky headland and clear water.

28Xerokambos Beach, Crete

Xerokambos, Sitia, Lasithi, Crete · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 26 °C

Xerokambos is a remote beach on the south-east coast of Crete, with pale sand, clear turquoise water and bare hills behind the shore.

29Xerokampos Beach, Crete

Xerokampos, Sitia, east Crete · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Xerokampos is a remote bay in the far east of Crete, with pale sand, flat white rocks and clear blue water.

30Dafni Beach, Zakynthos

Dafni, Vasilikos, Zakynthos · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Dafni is a long sandy beach on the Vasilikos peninsula in the south-east of Zakynthos, close to the Gerakas beach where sea turtles nest.

31Fakistra Beach, Pelion

Fakistra, Zagora, Pelion · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Fakistra is a small pebble beach on the Aegean coast of Pelion, reached by a steep road down through chestnut and olive trees.

32Kedrodasos Beach

Chania, Crete · 4.4 / 5 · Moderate access · about 23 °C

A quiet stretch of pale sand and clear water on the south-west coast of Crete, a short distance from Elafonisi.

33Marathias Beach, Zakynthos

Marathias, Keri, Zakynthos · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Marathias is a small bay near Keri on the south-west coast of Zakynthos, framed by white limestone rocks and shallow turquoise water.

34Triopetra Beach

Rethymno, South Crete · 4.4 / 5 · Moderate access · about 23 °C

A long, quiet beach of sand and fine pebbles on the south coast of Crete, named for the three rock formations standing in the water.

35Agia Roumeli Beach, Crete

Agia Roumeli, Sfakia, Crete · 4.3 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Agia Roumeli is a small village on the south coast of Crete with a long pebble beach, reached on foot through the Samaria Gorge or by ferry.

36Agnontas Beach, Skopelos

Agnontas, Skopelos · 4.3 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

Agnontas is a small fishing hamlet on a narrow green bay on the south-west coast of Skopelos.

37Glystra Beach, Rhodes

Lardos, Rhodes · 4.3 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Glystra is a small, quiet cove on the Lardos coast of Rhodes, with fine sand and pebbles and clear blue water below dry scrub hills.

38Kalogria Beach

Kalogria, Strofylia, Peloponnese · 4.3 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Kalogria is a very long beach of fine sand in the western Peloponnese, near Patras, backed by the Strofylia pine forest and coastal wetlands.

39Liapades Beach, Corfu

Liapades, Paleokastritsa, Corfu · 4.3 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

Liapades is a small village beach on the north-west coast of Corfu, just south of Paleokastritsa.

40Livadi Beach, Astypalaia

Livadi, Astypalaia, Dodecanese · 4.3 / 5 · Easy access · about 26 °C

Livadi is the main beach of Astypalaia, a green valley bay with pebbles and sand below the whitewashed Chora and its Venetian castle.

Frequently asked questions

Which are the top-rated hidden gem beaches in Greece?

Our top three are Balos Lagoon (4.8 out of 5), Kolona Beach (4.7 out of 5), Lalaria Beach (4.7 out of 5). Scores are editorial ratings from the EuroShores team.

When is the best time to visit these beaches?

Most are at their best from June to September. Water temperatures average about 24 degrees C in season. Each beach page lists its exact season.

Which of these beaches are easiest to reach?

Kavourotrypes Beach, Myrtiotissa Beach, Corfu, Stavros Beach, Crete, Agiofarago Beach, Crete, Agios Pavlos Beach, Rethymno and 15 more have easy access. The others need a walk, stairs or a rougher road, so check the access note on each page.
