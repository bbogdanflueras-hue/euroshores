Best Beaches with Sunbeds in the Costa de la Luz

We rank the 8 beaches with sunbeds and umbrellas in the Costa de la Luz. Playa de la Barrosa (Spain) comes first with 4.5 / 5, followed by Playa de la Fontanilla, Playa de la Victoria, Cadiz.

The sea reaches about 22 degrees C in season, and most of these beaches are at their best from June to September. 8 of the 8 have easy access, and 1 are hidden gems.

Save on Pinterest

1Playa de la Barrosa

Chiclana de la Frontera, Andalusia · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 21 °C

An 8 km sweep of fine golden sand on the Costa de la Luz, backed by pine woods and a low red cliff at the north end.

2Playa de la Fontanilla

Conil de la Frontera, Cadiz · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 21 °C

La Fontanilla is the main beach of Conil de la Frontera, a wide sweep of fine golden sand with shallow Atlantic water and a promenade.

3Playa de la Victoria, Cadiz

Cadiz, Andalusia · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 21 °C

Playa de la Victoria is a long beach of fine golden sand stretching along the modern side of Cadiz, with a wide promenade and Atlantic waves.

4Playa de Los Lances

Tarifa, Cadiz, Andalusia · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 21 °C

A long, open sandy beach on the Strait of Gibraltar just north-west of Tarifa, known for kitesurfing and windsurfing.

5Playa de Isla Canela, Huelva

Isla Canela, Ayamonte, Huelva, Andalusia · 4.3 / 5 · Easy access · about 22 °C

Isla Canela is a long beach of fine golden sand near Ayamonte on the Andalusian coast, close to the border with Portugal.

6Playa de Islantilla

Islantilla, Huelva, Costa de la Luz · 4.2 / 5 · Easy access · about 22 °C

Islantilla is a long, quiet beach of golden sand on the Huelva coast, with low dunes, pine woods and golf courses behind.

7Playa de Matalascanas

Matalascanas, Huelva, Costa de la Luz · 4.2 / 5 · Easy access · about 22 °C

Matalascanas is a long Atlantic beach of golden sand at the edge of Donana National Park.

8Playa de Regla, Chipiona

Chipiona, Cadiz, Costa de la Luz · 4.1 / 5 · Easy access · about 23 °C

Playa de Regla is a wide beach of fine sand in Chipiona, with the Sanctuary of Our Lady of Regla standing behind it.

Frequently asked questions

Which are the top-rated beaches with sunbeds and umbrellas in the Costa de la Luz?

Our top three are Playa de la Barrosa (4.5 out of 5), Playa de la Fontanilla (4.5 out of 5), Playa de la Victoria, Cadiz (4.4 out of 5). Scores are editorial ratings from the EuroShores team.

When is the best time to visit these beaches?

Most are at their best from June to September. Water temperatures average about 22 degrees C in season. Each beach page lists its exact season.

Which of these beaches are easiest to reach?

Playa de la Barrosa, Playa de la Fontanilla, Playa de la Victoria, Cadiz, Playa de Los Lances, Playa de Isla Canela, Huelva and 3 more have easy access. The others need a walk, stairs or a rougher road, so check the access note on each page.
