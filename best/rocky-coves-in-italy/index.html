Best Rocky Coves in Italy

We rank the 5 rocky coves in Italy. Baia di Ieranto (Italy) comes first with 4.7 / 5, followed by Cala Azzurra, Favignana, Cala degli Inglesi.

The sea reaches about 25 degrees C in season, and most of these beaches are at their best from June to September. 1 of the 5 have easy access, and 4 are hidden gems.

Save on Pinterest

1Baia di Ieranto

Massa Lubrense, Campania · 4.7 / 5 · Challenging access · about 24 °C

A secluded cove on the Sorrentine peninsula, set between steep rocky headlands with deep turquoise water and a rugged, unspoilt shoreline.

2Cala Azzurra, Favignana

Favignana, Egadi Islands, Sicily · 4.6 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Cala Azzurra is a small cove on the island of Favignana in the Egadi archipelago off west Sicily, with a narrow sand and pebble beach, pale rock and very clear turquoise water.

3Cala degli Inglesi

San Domino, Tremiti Islands · 4.6 / 5 · Moderate access · about 24 °C

A rocky cove on San Domino in the Tremiti Islands, with clear blue water and a rugged shoreline.

4Cala Rossa

Favignana, Egadi Islands, Sicily · 4.4 / 5 · Moderate access · about 25 °C

A rocky cove on the island of Favignana in the Egadi Islands, with pale tufa cliffs and clear turquoise water.

5Porto Selvaggio

Puglia · 4.4 / 5 · Challenging access · about 25 °C

A rocky cove in a regional park on the Salento coast, with pine woods and turquoise water.

Frequently asked questions

Which are the top-rated rocky coves in Italy?

Our top three are Baia di Ieranto (4.7 out of 5), Cala Azzurra, Favignana (4.6 out of 5), Cala degli Inglesi (4.6 out of 5). Scores are editorial ratings from the EuroShores team.

When is the best time to visit these beaches?

Most are at their best from June to September. Water temperatures average about 25 degrees C in season. Each beach page lists its exact season.

Which of these beaches are easiest to reach?

Cala Azzurra, Favignana have easy access. The others need a walk, stairs or a rougher road, so check the access note on each page.
