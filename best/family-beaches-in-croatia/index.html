Best Family Beaches in Croatia

We rank the 10 family-friendly beaches in Croatia. Rajska Plaza (Croatia) comes first with 4.6 / 5, followed by Sakarun Beach, Nin Beach.

The sea reaches about 25 degrees C in season, and most of these beaches are at their best from June to September. 10 of the 10 have easy access, and 3 are hidden gems.

Save on Pinterest

1Rajska Plaza

Lopar, Rab, Croatia · 4.6 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Also called Paradise Beach, the best-known beach on Rab, a long stretch of brown sand where the warm, shallow water reaches far out.

2Sakarun Beach

Dugi Otok, Croatia · 4.6 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

A long, shallow, white-sand bay in the Kornati archipelago — rare for the Adriatic and ideal for families.

3Nin Beach

Nin, Zadar County · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Queen Beach (Kraljicina plaza) is a sandy beach of more than 2 km on the Nin lagoon near Zadar.

4Slanica Beach

Murter, Sibenik-Knin, Croatia · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

A sandy bay near the town of Murter with shallow water and pine trees along the shore.

5Donje Celo Beach, Kolocep

Donje Celo, Kolocep, Elaphiti Islands · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

Donje Celo is a small village on Kolocep, the smallest inhabited Elaphiti island near Dubrovnik, with a small sandy beach in a sheltered bay.

6Bilin Zal Beach, Lumbarda

Lumbarda, Korcula · 4.3 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

Bilin Zal is a shallow cove of sand and fine pebbles near the village of Lumbarda at the eastern end of Korcula.

7Prapratno Beach, Peljesac

Prapratno, Peljesac · 4.3 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

Prapratno is a long sheltered bay on the Peljesac peninsula, a few kilometres from Ston, with a mix of fine pebbles and sand and pine trees right behind the shore.

8Bačvice

Split, Croatia · 4.2 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Split's city beach, with shallow, sandy-bottomed water and the home of picigin, a ball game played in the shallows.

9Bijeca Beach, Medulin

Bijeca, Medulin, Istria · 4.2 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Bijeca is a gently sloping beach of sand and fine pebbles on the Medulin bay in southern Istria, shaded by pine trees.

10Zaton Beach

Zaton, near Nin and Zadar · 4.2 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

A long, shallow beach between Zadar and Nin on the Adriatic, mostly sand with pebbles in places.

Frequently asked questions

Which are the top-rated family-friendly beaches in Croatia?

Our top three are Rajska Plaza (4.6 out of 5), Sakarun Beach (4.6 out of 5), Nin Beach (4.5 out of 5). Scores are editorial ratings from the EuroShores team.

When is the best time to visit these beaches?

Most are at their best from June to September. Water temperatures average about 25 degrees C in season. Each beach page lists its exact season.

Which of these beaches are easiest to reach?

Rajska Plaza, Sakarun Beach, Nin Beach, Slanica Beach, Donje Celo Beach, Kolocep and 5 more have easy access. The others need a walk, stairs or a rougher road, so check the access note on each page.
