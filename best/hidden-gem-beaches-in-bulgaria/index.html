Hidden Gem Beaches in Bulgaria

We rank the 6 hidden gem beaches in Bulgaria. Arkutino Beach, Primorsko (Bulgaria) comes first with 4.0 / 5, followed by Irakli Beach, Obzor, Kavatsite Beach, Sozopol.

The sea reaches about 24 degrees C in season, and most of these beaches are at their best from June to September. 3 of the 6 have easy access, and 6 are hidden gems.

1Arkutino Beach, Primorsko

Arkutino, Sozopol, Bulgaria · 4.0 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Arkutino is a sandy beach between Sozopol and Primorsko, set next to Arkutino lake and the Ropotamo nature reserve.

2Irakli Beach, Obzor

Irakli, near Obzor, Bulgaria · 4.0 / 5 · Moderate access · about 24 °C

Irakli is a long, undeveloped sandy beach south of Obzor, backed by the forested hills of the Emine cape.

3Kavatsite Beach, Sozopol

Kavatsite, Sozopol, Bulgaria · 4.0 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Kavatsite is a long sandy beach north of Sozopol, backed by dunes and a pine camping area.

4Krapets Beach, Shabla

Krapets, Shabla, Bulgaria · 4.0 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

Krapets is a small village on the northern coast near Shabla with a long sandy beach and few visitors.

5Silistar Beach, Tsarevo

Silistar, Tsarevo, Bulgaria · 4.0 / 5 · Moderate access · about 24 °C

Silistar is a quiet sandy beach on the far southern coast, close to the Strandzha Nature Park and the Turkish border region.

6Veleka Beach, Sinemorets

Sinemorets, Tsarevo, Bulgaria · 4.0 / 5 · Moderate access · about 24 °C

Near Sinemorets, the Veleka river reaches the Black Sea inside the Strandzha Nature Park.

Frequently asked questions

Which are the top-rated hidden gem beaches in Bulgaria?

Our top three are Arkutino Beach, Primorsko (4.0 out of 5), Irakli Beach, Obzor (4.0 out of 5), Kavatsite Beach, Sozopol (4.0 out of 5). Scores are editorial ratings from the EuroShores team.

When is the best time to visit these beaches?

Most are at their best from June to September. Water temperatures average about 24 degrees C in season. Each beach page lists its exact season.

Which of these beaches are easiest to reach?

Arkutino Beach, Primorsko, Kavatsite Beach, Sozopol, Krapets Beach, Shabla have easy access. The others need a walk, stairs or a rougher road, so check the access note on each page.
