Best Pebble Beaches in Italy

We rank the 27 pebble beaches in Italy. Cala Goloritzé (Italy) comes first with 4.8 / 5, followed by Cala Mariolu, Cala Luna.

The sea reaches about 24 degrees C in season, and most of these beaches are at their best from June to September. 14 of the 27 have easy access, and 11 are hidden gems.

Save on Pinterest

1Cala Goloritzé

Sardinia, Italy · 4.8 / 5 · Challenging access · about 24 °C

A secluded cove under a limestone needle, reached only on foot or by boat — worth every step.

2Cala Mariolu

Gulf of Orosei, Sardinia, Italy · 4.8 / 5 · Challenging access · about 23 °C

A pebble cove with vivid turquoise water below steep cliffs, reachable only by boat or on foot.

3Cala Luna

Gulf of Orosei, Sardinia, Italy · 4.7 / 5 · Challenging access · about 22 °C

A crescent of pale pebbles and coarse sand between limestone cliffs, with caves along the back and a freshwater pond behind the beach.

4Baia del Buondormire, Palinuro

Capo Palinuro, Cilento, Campania · 4.6 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

Baia del Buondormire is a small bay of clear turquoise water on Capo Palinuro, in the Cilento coast of Campania, enclosed by tall cliffs and rocks.

5Baia delle Zagare

Mattinata, Gargano, Puglia · 4.6 / 5 · Moderate access · about 24 °C

A pebble cove on the Gargano coast of Puglia, known for its white limestone sea stacks, clear turquoise water and dramatic cliffs backed by pine trees.

6Cala Fuili

Cala Gonone, Dorgali, Sardinia, Italy · 4.6 / 5 · Moderate access · about 22 °C

A pebble and rock cove near Cala Gonone on the east coast of Sardinia, set below green limestone cliffs.

7Lama Monachile

Polignano a Mare, Puglia · 4.6 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

A small pebble cove at the foot of Polignano a Mare, framed by tall limestone cliffs and houses built right at the edge.

8Spiaggia dei Gabbiani

Conero Riviera, Marche · 4.6 / 5 · Challenging access · about 23 °C

A pebble cove on the Conero Riviera in the Marche, set below pale cliffs with clear turquoise water along a quiet stretch of the Adriatic coast.

9Spiaggia di Portonovo

Portonovo, Conero, Marche · 4.6 / 5 · Easy access · about 23 °C

Portonovo is a bay of white pebbles and clear blue water below the green cliffs of the Conero headland.

10Marina di Praia

Praiano, Amalfi Coast · 4.5 / 5 · Moderate access · about 24 °C

A small pebbly cove at the bottom of a steep gorge, with a pretty old fishing hamlet, a few restaurants and a sea that is deep blue and very clear.

11Mingardo Beach

Marina di Camerota, Campania · 4.5 / 5 · Moderate access · about 23 °C

A quiet cove of small pebbles and clear water on the Cilento coast, set between green cliffs near Marina di Camerota.

12Paraggi Beach

Paraggi, Santa Margherita Ligure, Liguria · 4.5 / 5 · Moderate access · about 24 °C

A small sheltered bay on the Portofino coast, framed by pastel houses and wooded hills.

13Sansone Beach

Portoferraio, Elba · 4.5 / 5 · Moderate access · about 23 °C

A small bay of bright white pebbles and clear turquoise water on Elba north coast near Portoferraio.

14Spiaggia di Sirolo

Sirolo, Marche · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

Sirolo is a hill town on the Conero Riviera, and its beaches are white pebble coves under steep green cliffs.

15Camogli Beach

Camogli, Liguria · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

A curved pebble beach beneath the tall pastel houses and basilica of Camogli.

16Fiascherino Beach

Fiascherino, Lerici, Liguria · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 22 °C

A small cove on the Gulf of La Spezia between Lerici and Tellaro.

17Fornillo Beach

Positano, Amalfi Coast · 4.4 / 5 · Moderate access · about 24 °C

A pebbly beach in a small bay west of Positano town, calmer and less crowded than Spiaggia Grande.

18Isola Bella

Taormina, Sicily · 4.4 / 5 · Moderate access · about 25 °C

A small island and pebble bay below Taormina, linked to the shore by a narrow strip.

19Spiaggia Grande, Diamante

Diamante, Calabria · 4.4 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Spiaggia Grande is the main beach of Diamante, a pebble and coarse sand bay on the Riviera dei Cedri.

20Balai Beach

Porto Torres, Sardinia · 4.3 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

A small pebble and sand cove on the north-west coast of Sardinia near Porto Torres, with clear water and a rocky shoreline.

21Cala Gonone Beach

Cala Gonone, Sardinia, Italy · 4.3 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

A fine pebble beach in the small resort of Cala Gonone, on the Gulf of Orosei, with clear water beside the harbour.

22Spiaggia di Giardini Naxos

Giardini Naxos, Sicily · 4.3 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Giardini Naxos is a bay of grey pebbles and warm clear water at the foot of Taormina, with Mount Etna smoking in the distance.

23Spiaggia di Marina di Maratea

Maratea, Basilicata · 4.3 / 5 · Easy access · about 23 °C

Marina di Maratea is a small port with a pebble beach below steep green mountains, on the short Tyrrhenian coast of Basilicata.

24Monterosso al Mare

Monterosso al Mare, Cinque Terre, Liguria · 4.2 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

The main beach of the Cinque Terre, at Fegina below the village of Monterosso.

25Marina Grande Beach

Marina Grande, Sorrento, Campania · 4.1 / 5 · Moderate access · about 24 °C

A small public beach of volcanic sand and rocks beside the old fishing harbour of Marina Grande in Sorrento, with colourful houses and restaurants around it.

26Spiaggia di Sanremo

Sanremo, Liguria · 4.1 / 5 · Easy access · about 22 °C

Sanremo has several public beaches of pebbles and sand along its Riviera coast, with calm water and palm trees on the promenade.

27Spiaggia di Scalea

Scalea, Calabria · 4.1 / 5 · Easy access · about 24 °C

Scalea has a long beach of grey pebbles and sand along the Riviera dei Cedri on the northern Calabrian coast.

Frequently asked questions

Which are the top-rated pebble beaches in Italy?

Our top three are Cala Goloritzé (4.8 out of 5), Cala Mariolu (4.8 out of 5), Cala Luna (4.7 out of 5). Scores are editorial ratings from the EuroShores team.

When is the best time to visit these beaches?

Most are at their best from June to September. Water temperatures average about 24 degrees C in season. Each beach page lists its exact season.

Which of these beaches are easiest to reach?

Baia del Buondormire, Palinuro, Lama Monachile, Spiaggia di Portonovo, Spiaggia di Sirolo, Camogli Beach and 9 more have easy access. The others need a walk, stairs or a rougher road, so check the access note on each page.
