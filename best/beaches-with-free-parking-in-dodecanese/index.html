Best Beaches with Free Parking in the Dodecanese

We rank the 4 beaches with free parking in the Dodecanese. Prasonisi Beach, Rhodes (Greece) comes first with 4.5 / 5, followed by Stegna Beach, Rhodes, Kardamena Beach, Kos.

The sea reaches about 25 degrees C in season, and most of these beaches are at their best from June to September. 4 of the 4 have easy access.

Save on Pinterest

1Prasonisi Beach, Rhodes

Prasonisi, Rhodes, Dodecanese · 4.5 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Prasonisi is a sandy spit at the far southern tip of Rhodes, where a narrow sandbar joins the main island to a small rocky islet.

2Stegna Beach, Rhodes

Stegna, Rhodes · 4.2 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Stegna is a small bay of sand and fine pebbles on the east coast of Rhodes, sheltered by a rocky headland.

3Kardamena Beach, Kos

Kardamena, Kos · 4.1 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Kardamena has a long beach of light sand on the south coast of Kos, with thatched parasols, sunbeds and warm shallow water.

4Marmari Beach, Kos

Marmari, Kos · 4.1 / 5 · Easy access · about 25 °C

Marmari is a long, wide beach of fine sand on the north coast of Kos, backed by low dunes and tamarisk trees.

Frequently asked questions

Which are the top-rated beaches with free parking in the Dodecanese?

Our top three are Prasonisi Beach, Rhodes (4.5 out of 5), Stegna Beach, Rhodes (4.2 out of 5), Kardamena Beach, Kos (4.1 out of 5). Scores are editorial ratings from the EuroShores team.

When is the best time to visit these beaches?

Most are at their best from June to September. Water temperatures average about 25 degrees C in season. Each beach page lists its exact season.

Which of these beaches are easiest to reach?

Prasonisi Beach, Rhodes, Stegna Beach, Rhodes, Kardamena Beach, Kos, Marmari Beach, Kos have easy access. The others need a walk, stairs or a rougher road, so check the access note on each page.
