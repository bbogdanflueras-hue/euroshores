Most Romantic Beach Getaways

2 min read · Last updated 2026-10-27

A romantic beach is as much about atmosphere as sand: a dramatic setting, privacy, sunset light and somewhere to sit with a drink afterwards. These beaches on our list stand out for couples, whether you want a quiet cove or a lively evening promenade. Check each beach page for access, season and current rules before you go.

Quiet coves and hidden spots

  • Cala Goloritze in Sardinia: a pebble cove under a limestone needle, reached on foot with a challenging walk. Access is regulated, so check the current rules.
  • Cala Mariolu in Sardinia: pale pebbles and clear water. It is usually visited by boat or by a long walk from the coast.
  • Stiniva on Vis: a pebble cove that is entered through a narrow rock gap and feels hidden from the sea.
  • Dubovica on Hvar: a small pebble cove down a steep path, ideal if you start early and bring supplies.
  • San Blas Bay on Gozo: a secluded cove down a steep road, rewarding for those who plan their return climb.
  • Gjipe in Albania: a canyon-framed beach reached by a walk, with a wild feel.

Sunset and evening views

  • Elafonissi Beach in Crete: pink-tinted sand and shallow lagoons, lovely in late afternoon after day visitors leave.
  • Potami Beach near Himara: a long pebble curve that is a good place for a sunset swim.
  • Ses Illetes in Formentera: shallow turquoise water and sunset views over the Mediterranean.
  • Sveti Stefan in Montenegro: a famous island village view, best at golden hour from the beach.

Dramatic scenery

  • Praia da Marinha in the Algarve: cliffs, arches and clear water, best on a calm day.
  • Benagil Cove in the Algarve: a sea cave with an opening to the sky.
  • As Catedrais in Galicia: arches and caves that you can walk through at low tide, so check the tide table.
  • Qeparo in Albania: a curved beach with clear water and an old village above.

Planning a romantic day

  • Go early or late, because the middle of the day is when day trippers arrive.
  • Pack food, water and shade for remote coves, where there are often no facilities.
  • Check the tide, wind and any booking rules in advance.
  • Wear proper shoes for the walk, and keep valuables light.

A note on Navagio Beach

Navagio on Zakynthos is one of Europe's best-known beaches, but landings are currently banned because of landslide risk. You can still see the cove from the cliff viewing platform, if it is open. Confirm the current situation before you plan a trip.
