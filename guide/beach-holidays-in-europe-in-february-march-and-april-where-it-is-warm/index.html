Beach Holidays in Europe in February, March and April: Where It Is Warm

2 min read · Last updated 2026-11-27

From February to April most of the Mediterranean is still too cool for a long swim, but the days get longer and warmer fast. This guide shows where you can have real beach weather in late winter and spring, using long-term averages, and which beaches on EuroShores to pick.

February: still only the Atlantic islands are warm

Average sea temperatures in February are about 19 C in Tenerife and Gran Canaria, 18 C in Lanzarote, Fuerteventura and Madeira, 17 C on the Antalya coast and 16 C in Cyprus, Rhodes and Kos. Most of the Mediterranean, including Malta, Crete, the Algarve and the Costa del Sol, is around 15 C. For a beach holiday in February, the Canary Islands are the safest choice.

March: the Canaries, the Algarve and southern Spain warm up

Average daytime highs in March are about 20 C in the Canary Islands, 18 C in the Algarve and on the Costa Blanca, and 17 C on the Costa del Sol and in Cyprus. The sea is still cool, so March is a month for sunny beach walks and short swims. Easter Sunday falls on 28 March in 2027, so book early if you travel over Easter.

April: the eastern Mediterranean catches up

By April average highs reach about 21 C in Cyprus, 20 C in the Canary Islands and on the Dalaman coast of Turkey, and 19 C in the Algarve, on the Costa del Sol, in Crete and in Malta. The sea is still only around 17 to 19 C in most places, so it is ideal for walking, sunbathing and quieter beaches before the summer crowds.

Beaches to consider in spring

  • Canary Islands: Maspalomas Dunes, Playa de Papagayo and Playa de Sotavento.
  • Algarve: Praia da Marinha and Praia da Falesia, for cliff walks above empty beaches.
  • Cyprus: Nissi Beach and Konnos Bay, the warmest choice in the Mediterranean by April.
  • Malta: Golden Bay and Ramla Bay on Gozo, with spring flowers on the hills.
  • Crete: Balos Lagoon, much quieter in April than in August.

Tips for a spring beach trip

  • Check whether boats and roads to remote beaches are already running; some only start in May.
  • Lifeguards often begin work in June, so swim with care.
  • Spring is the best time for walking: the hills are green and the paths are cool.
