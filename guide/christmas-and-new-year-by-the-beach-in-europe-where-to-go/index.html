Christmas and New Year by the Beach in Europe: Where to Go

5 min read · Last updated 2026-11-27

If you would rather spend Christmas and New Year by the sea than in the snow, Europe has a handful of places where late December still means sunshine, a walk on the sand and sometimes a swim. This guide focuses on the holiday period itself: what the weather is usually like, what happens on and around the beaches, and which beaches on EuroShores suit a festive trip.

December at a glance

  • Canary Islands: the warmest choice. Weather2Travel gives an average December sea temperature of about 21 C around Santa Cruz de Tenerife.
  • Madeira: mild, with the sea usually around 19 to 20 C, and the biggest New Year party in this list.
  • Cyprus and Malta: often sunny, with the sea still close to 18 to 20 C in early December and cooling through the month.
  • Algarve: cooler sea, around 16 to 18 C, but often bright, calm days that are good for long beach walks.

These are long-term averages. A winter storm can bring rain and big waves anywhere, so check the forecast a few days before you go.

1. Madeira: fireworks over Funchal bay

Christmas is the biggest party of the year in Madeira. The official season runs from 1 December 2026 to 10 January 2027, with street lights, a Christmas market and events in Funchal almost every day. The best-known local tradition is the Market Night at the Farmers' Market in Funchal on 23 December.

The highlight is the New Year's Eve fireworks over Funchal bay, which Guinness World Records listed in 2006 as the largest fireworks display in the world. The show for 2025 to 2026 lasted about eight minutes. Hotels with a view of the bay and the hills above the city sell out early, and many cruise ships anchor in the bay for the night.

  • Porto Santo Beach: about 9 km of golden sand on the neighbouring island, the best real beach in the archipelago. Ferries and short flights link it with Madeira.
  • Praia do Seixal: a black-sand beach on the north coast below green cliffs, best on calm days.

2. Canary Islands: Christmas in the warmest sea

The Canaries are the only place in Europe where a long swim on Christmas Day is realistic for most people. Towns hold Christmas markets and nativity scenes, and New Year is celebrated in the street with fireworks and, as in the rest of Spain, twelve grapes eaten at midnight.

Las Palmas de Gran Canaria usually builds a large sand nativity scene on Las Canteras beach, which has drawn huge crowds for 20 years. In November 2026 the organisers announced that it would not take place this year after a dispute with the city council, so check local news before you plan around it.

  • Playa de las Canteras, Gran Canaria: a city beach with a natural reef that keeps the water calm; easy to combine with city life in Las Palmas.
  • Maspalomas Dunes, Gran Canaria: the sunny south, with a long beach and huge dunes.
  • Playa de las Teresitas, Tenerife: a calm, sheltered beach near Santa Cruz.
  • Playa de las Americas, Tenerife: the sunniest coast of the island, with plenty of restaurants open over the holidays.

For help choosing an island and coast, see our Canary Islands winter guide.

3. Cyprus: Christmas swims with the locals

Cyprus is quiet in December, and some beach bars close, but the sun is reliable. Groups of winter swimmers meet all year, and the holidays are their big moment: in Paphos they swim at Christmas, in Larnaca on the last Saturday of the year, and in Limassol on New Year's morning, followed by food, drinks and greetings on the shore.

  • Alykes Beach, Kato Paphos: close to the harbour and the winter swimmers of Paphos.
  • Finikoudes Beach, Larnaca: a town beach with a palm-lined promenade.
  • Governor's Beach, near Limassol: dark sand below white cliffs, good for a quiet winter walk.
  • Nissi Beach, Ayia Napa: white sand and clear water, very calm out of season.

4. Malta: a sunny city break with beaches

Malta works best as a Christmas city break with beach walks rather than a swimming holiday. Valletta and the towns are decorated for the season, and local charity swims are held around Christmas.

  • Golden Bay and Ghajn Tuffieha Bay: two sandy bays on the west coast, good for sunset walks.
  • Fond Ghadir, Sliema: a rocky seafront on the promenade, handy for a short dip if the sea is calm.

5. The Algarve: empty beaches and a New Year dip

The Algarve is cooler, but December often brings clear, calm days, and beaches that are packed in August are almost empty. In Ferragudo, near Portimao, a traditional New Year's Day swim in the sea draws locals and visitors.

  • Praia Grande, Ferragudo: a wide sandy beach at the mouth of the Arade river.
  • Praia da Rocha, Portimao: a long beach below cliffs, with a promenade and restaurants open all year.
  • Praia da Falesia: about 6 km of sand below red and orange cliffs, ideal for a long walk.

Tips for a beach Christmas

  • Book early: flights and hotels in Madeira and the Canaries for the week between Christmas and New Year are among the most expensive of the winter.
  • Check opening days: many restaurants close on 25 December and 1 January, or need a booking for set menus.
  • Lifeguards rarely work in winter. Swim near other people, stay close to shore and keep out of the water when there are warning flags.
  • Days are short: the sun sets at about 18:00 in the Canaries and before 17:00 in Cyprus in late December.
