Sea Temperature in Europe by Month: 29 Beach Regions Compared

3 min read · Last updated 2026-11-27

If you want warm water, the eastern Mediterranean wins in every month from May to October. Along the Antalya coast in Turkey and in Cyprus, the sea is already above 20 C in May and reaches about 28 C in August. Spain's Atlantic and Costa Brava coasts and the Algarve stay noticeably cooler, and September is warmer than June almost everywhere.

What the numbers say

  • May: only south Turkey, Cyprus and the Canary Islands reach about 20 C or more. Everywhere else the sea is still around 17 to 19 C, fine for a quick dip but cool for long swims.
  • June: most of the Mediterranean passes 21 C, from Croatia and Montenegro to Greece, southern Italy, Malta and Mallorca. Spain's Costa del Sol, Costa de la Luz and Costa Brava lag behind at about 20 C.
  • July and August: the warmest months nearly everywhere, with 24 to 29 C across most of the Mediterranean and the biggest crowds.
  • September: warmer than June in every Mediterranean region, with fewer people. Malta, the Dodecanese, Crete and Cyprus stay at 25 C or more.
  • October: still 23 to 25 C in Cyprus, south Turkey, Malta and the Canaries, but below 20 C in Istria, the Costa Brava and Brittany.
  • The Atlantic is cool all summer. The Algarve only reaches about 20 C in August, and Brittany about 17 C.

How to use this for a 2027 trip

  • For a warm-water trip in May or early June, choose Cyprus, the Antalya coast or the Canaries.
  • For a family trip in late June, Croatia, Montenegro, the Ionian Islands and Puglia combine warm, calm water with shorter flights from northern Europe.
  • For the best mix of warm sea and fewer people, go in September, especially to Malta, Crete, Sicily or the Dodecanese.
