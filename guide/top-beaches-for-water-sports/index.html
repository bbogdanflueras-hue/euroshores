Top Beaches for Water Sports

1 min read · Last updated 2026-10-10

Wind is what makes the difference.

  • Zlatni Rat (Croatia) — reliable afternoon winds for windsurfing, with water sports, beach bars and showers. Mornings are calmer if you just want to swim.
  • Benagil (Portugal) — kayaking into the sea cave. Check conditions and local rules before you go.
  • Es Trenc (Spain) — calm shallows for paddleboarding.

Choosing the right beach

Windsurfers want steady wind, usually in the afternoon, while paddleboarders and kayakers want the opposite: flat water, which is most common in the morning. That is why one beach, like Zlatni Rat, can suit both at different times of day. Always check the forecast, wear a buoyancy aid, and tell someone where you are going.
