Snorkelling Basics at Rocky Coves

1 min read · Last updated 2026-11-11

Rocky coves often have clear water and plenty to see, which makes them good places to try snorkelling. These basics help you start safely.

Gear

  • A mask that seals well on your face, with no strap tightened too hard.
  • A snorkel and, if you like, short fins.
  • Water shoes to protect your feet when you enter over rocks.
  • A rash vest or thin wetsuit if the water is cool.

Choose the conditions

Pick calm days with little wind and swell, and enter from a sheltered side of the cove. Good visibility is usually best in the morning, before the wind rises.

Technique

Breathe slowly through the snorkel and keep your movements gentle. Look down and around, and do not touch or stand on marine life. If water gets in the mask, tilt your head back, press the top of the mask and breathe out through your nose.

Safety

  • Never snorkel alone, and tell someone where you are going.
  • Stay close to shore until you are confident.
  • Watch for boats and use a bright float if boat traffic is possible.
  • Leave sea urchins and jellyfish alone.
