Best Beaches in Italy for 2027: Sardinia, Sicily and the Amalfi Coast

2 min read · Last updated 2026-11-27

Italy's best beaches are mostly on its islands. Sardinia alone has several of the clearest seas in the Mediterranean, but many of its famous coves now need a booking in summer. These are our top-rated Italian beaches for 2027, with the access rules to plan around.

Sardinia

  • Cala Goloritze: limestone spire and white pebbles on the Gulf of Orosei. Booking is required, about 7 euros, and you walk in on a trail; boats cannot land passengers.
  • Cala Mariolu: small white pebbles and very clear water, reached almost only by boat from Santa Maria Navarrese, Arbatax or Cala Gonone.
  • Cala Luna: a long beach with caves, by boat from Cala Gonone or a two-hour walk from Cala Fuili.
  • La Pelosa, Stintino: very shallow water and an offshore watchtower. Booking required from mid-May to mid-October, about 3.50 euros.
  • Cala Brandinchi, San Teodoro: shallow turquoise water; access has been capped in recent summers.
  • Tuerredda, south coast: booking required in summer, with a daily cap of about 1,100 visitors in 2026.
  • Porto Giunco, Villasimius: white sand with a flamingo lagoon behind it.

Sicily and the small islands

  • Spiaggia dei Conigli, Lampedusa: a turtle nesting beach in a nature reserve, with limited places booked online in two daily sessions.
  • Scala dei Turchi, near Agrigento: white marl cliffs above the beach; the cliffs themselves are closed to walkers.

Mainland Italy

  • Cala Violina, Tuscany: about 700 visitors a day in summer, booked online for about 1 euro, plus paid parking.
  • Spiaggia Grande, Positano: the classic Amalfi Coast view. Most of the beach is paid beach clubs, with a small free section.
  • Tropea, Calabria: clear water below a clifftop old town, with the Santa Maria dell'Isola church on its rock.
  • Vignanotica, Gargano, Puglia: a pebble cove under white cliffs, reached by a path down from the coast road.

Planning tips for 2027

  • Booking windows for Sardinian beaches often open only 48 to 72 hours ahead and sell out in July and August. See our guide to beaches you must book in 2027.
  • June and September give warm water and far fewer people. The sea in Sardinia and Sicily is usually 24 C or more in September. See our sea temperature by month guide.
  • Many Italian beaches are mostly paid beach clubs (lidi), with a free public strip. Arrive early for the free part.
