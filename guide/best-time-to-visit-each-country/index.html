Best Time to Visit Each Country

1 min read · Last updated 2026-10-10

Mediterranean beaches are at their best from late May to mid-June and again in September: warm sea, long days and noticeably fewer people than July and August.

Country by country

  • Greece, Italy, Spain: May–October; sea is warmest in August and September.
  • Croatia, Montenegro: June–September for swimming; shoulder months are lovely for walking.
  • Portugal (Algarve): June–September; the Atlantic stays cooler than the Med.
  • Corsica: June–September.

Why shoulder season works

In July and August the most famous beaches fill by late morning, parking is the first thing to go, and boat-only or booking-limited beaches can sell out. Late May, June and September give you most of the warmth with far more space. Sea temperatures lag the air, so the water is usually warmest in late summer. Our beach pages list a typical in-season temperature for each beach, from roughly 21 degrees C on the Algarve to 27 degrees C at Myrtos in Kefalonia.

Booking and regulated beaches

Some beaches now limit visitors in summer. La Pelosa in Sardinia requires a reservation from mid-May to mid-October, and Cala Goloritzé caps daily numbers in summer. Navagio in Greece is reportedly closed to landings through 31 October 2026. See each beach page for what we found, and confirm on the official sites before you travel.
