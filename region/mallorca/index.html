About beaches in Mallorca

Mallorca has everything from long sandy bays to small coves between pine-covered cliffs. The south-east and north-east coasts are known for turquoise water and narrow calas, while the west coast is rockier and more dramatic.

When to go

May to October is the beach season. July and August are the busiest, while June and September are often warm with fewer people.

Getting around

Hire a car to reach the hidden coves. Roads to the popular calas get busy and parking is limited, so start early in the day.

Easiest beaches to reach

Es Trenc, Formentor Beach, Cala Mondragó, Cala Mesquida.

Quieter hidden gems

Cala Varques, Platja de Son Real.
