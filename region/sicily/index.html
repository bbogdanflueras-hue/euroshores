About beaches in Sicily

Sicily has sandy bays, rocky coves, white cliffs and volcanic shores, with clear water and strong food traditions close to every beach. The small islands such as Lampedusa and Favignana add some of the clearest water in Italy.

When to go

May to October is the beach season. July and August are hot and busy, while June and September have warm water and fewer people.

Getting around

A rental car is the easiest way to travel between beaches. Public transport is limited outside the cities, and nature reserve beaches may have rules or limited parking.
