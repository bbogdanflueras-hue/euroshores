About beaches in the Costa del Sol and Andalusia

Southern Spain's Mediterranean coast mixes long urban beaches, pebble coves below cliffs and wild shores in Cabo de Gata. The climate is warm for much of the year and chiringuitos serve grilled fish on the sand.

When to go

May to October is the beach season, with very hot weather in July and August. April and November can still be pleasant for walking.

Getting around

Trains and buses serve the main resorts, but a car is useful for the wilder beaches. Wind and sea state can change quickly, so check conditions before swimming.

Easiest beaches to reach

Los Genoveses Beach, Playazo de Rodalquilar, Playa de Cabopino, Playa Burriana.

Quieter hidden gems

Playa de Monsul, Playa de los Muertos, Carboneras, Playazo de Rodalquilar, Playa de Salobrena, Granada.
