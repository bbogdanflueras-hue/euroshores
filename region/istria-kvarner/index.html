About beaches in Istria and Kvarner

Istria and the Kvarner Gulf in northern Croatia mix rocky coves, concrete bathing platforms, pebble bays and a few sandy beaches on the islands of Rab and Losinj. The sea is clear and the coast is green.

When to go

June to September is the swimming season. The northern Adriatic warms up later than the south, so July to early September is the most reliable.

Getting around

Roads are good and distances are short, so a car or a bike works well. Many beaches have platforms and ladders instead of sand, so bring water shoes.

Easiest beaches to reach

Rajska Plaza, Kamenjak Beach, Premantura, Lone Bay, Stara Baska Beach.

Quieter hidden gems

Kamenjak Beach, Premantura, Pinizule Beach, Valun Beach, Cres.
