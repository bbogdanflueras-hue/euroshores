About beaches in Puglia

Puglia is the heel of Italy, with sandy Ionian beaches in Salento, rocky coves around Polignano and cliffs on the Gargano coast. The sea is clear and shallow in many places, and old whitewashed towns sit near the shore.

When to go

June to September is the swimming season. The Ionian side is calm and warm in summer, while the Adriatic side can be breezier.

Getting around

A car is the practical choice. Many beaches have paid lidos with sunbeds, and free sections fill early in July and August.

Easiest beaches to reach

Lama Monachile, Torre dell'Orso Beach, Punta Prosciutto, Pizzomunno Beach.

Quieter hidden gems

Vignanotica Beach, Gargano, Baia delle Zagare, Torre Guaceto Beach, Baia dei Turchi.
