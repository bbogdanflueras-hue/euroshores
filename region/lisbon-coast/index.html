About beaches in the Lisbon coast

The Lisbon coast has golden beaches in Cascais, long dune beaches at Costa da Caparica and sheltered bays in the Arrabida natural park. Many beaches are within an hour of the city.

When to go

June to September is the best swimming season. The Atlantic water is cool, and wind can be strong on exposed beaches.

Getting around

Trains reach Cascais and Estoril, and buses and ferries serve other beaches. In summer, arrive early for parking, and check the flags before swimming.

Easiest beaches to reach

Praia da Figueirinha, Setubal, Praia da Adraga, Praia Atlantica, Troia, Praia do Guincho.

Quieter hidden gems

Praia da Ursa, Portinho da Arrabida, Praia da Figueirinha, Setubal, Praia de Galapos, Arrabida.
