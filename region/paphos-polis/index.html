About beaches in Paphos and Polis

The west coast of Cyprus around Paphos is quieter and wilder than the southeast. Near Paphos town and Coral Bay you find sandy resort beaches with calm water, while further north the Akamas Peninsula and the Polis and Latchi coast have pebbly coves, clear sea and mountain scenery. Some of the more remote beaches are reached by unpaved tracks and have no facilities.

When to go

May to October is the main season, and the sea is warmest from July to October. Summer is hot, so early morning and late afternoon are the best times to walk to remote beaches. Spring is good for hiking in Akamas, although the sea is still cool. Sea turtles nest on parts of this coast in summer, so keep to marked areas and avoid lights on the sand at night.

Getting around

Paphos airport is the nearest gateway, and a hire car is the best way to explore, especially north of Pegeia where public transport is limited. Roads to some beaches on the Akamas are rough, and you should check your rental agreement before driving on unpaved tracks. Polis and Latchi make a relaxed base for the north coast, while Paphos and Coral Bay suit visitors who want restaurants and bars close by.
