About beaches in Corsica

Corsica mixes mountains with long sandy bays, clear water and granite rocks. The south and south-east have some of the best-known white-sand beaches, while the west coast is wilder.

When to go

June to September is the main season. The water is warmest in August and early September.

Getting around

A car is the best way to get around, though the roads are slow and winding. Some beaches need a short walk, and ferries connect the island with France and Italy.

Easiest beaches to reach

Palombaggia, Rondinara Beach, Santa Giulia, Aregno Beach.

Quieter hidden gems

Palombaggia, Saleccia, Plage de Roccapina.
