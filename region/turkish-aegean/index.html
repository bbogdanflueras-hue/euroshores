About beaches in the Turkish Aegean

North of Bodrum, the Aegean coast of Turkey is breezier and less crowded than the Mediterranean resorts. The Cesme Peninsula near Izmir has sandy beaches such as Ilica and Alacati, which is a popular windsurfing and kitesurfing spot. Further north, Ayvalik, Dikili and Assos have olive groves, stone villages and quieter pebble and sand beaches.

When to go

June to September is the best time for swimming, and the sea is warm into October. Summer winds are stronger here than on the southern coast, which suits watersports but means some beaches are rougher in the afternoon. Spring and autumn are good for visiting the old towns and ancient sites.

Getting around

Izmir airport is the main gateway, and buses connect Izmir with Cesme, Dikili and Ayvalik. A hire car makes it easier to reach Assos and the smaller beaches. Ferries run between Cesme and the Greek island of Chios in summer.
