About beaches in Liguria

Liguria, the Italian Riviera, has small pebble beaches, colourful fishing villages and steep green hills falling to the sea. The Cinque Terre and Portofino are the best-known areas, with swimming from rocks and tiny coves.

When to go

June to September is the swimming season. Spring is good for walking the coastal paths, but the sea is still cool.

Getting around

Trains and ferries are the best way to travel, and cars are discouraged in the villages. Many beaches are small and use paid lidos in summer.

Easiest beaches to reach

Baia del Silenzio, Camogli Beach, Fiascherino Beach, Spiaggia di Alassio.

Quieter hidden gems
