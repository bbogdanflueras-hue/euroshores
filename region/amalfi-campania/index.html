About beaches in the Amalfi Coast and Campania

The Amalfi Coast and wider Campania combine steep cliffs, small pebble beaches and island bays around Capri, Ischia and Procida. Beaches here are often small and tucked below villages, with colourful views.

When to go

May to September is the beach season. July and August are the busiest, so early mornings and shoulder months work best.

Getting around

Boats and ferries are often easier than driving on the narrow coast road. Many small beaches are managed by paid lidos, so expect to pay for sunbeds in summer.

Easiest beaches to reach

Spiaggia Grande, Cala Bianca, Marina di Camerota, Spiaggia dei Maronti, Marina del Cantone Beach, Nerano.

Quieter hidden gems

Baia di Ieranto, Baia del Buondormire, Palinuro, Mingardo Beach, Marina di Praia.
