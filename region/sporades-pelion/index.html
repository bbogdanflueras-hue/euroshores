About beaches in Pelion and the Sporades

Pelion and the Sporades in the northern Aegean have green hills, pine forests and clear water. Skiathos and Skopelos are known for sandy bays, while Pelion has pebble beaches below forested mountains.

When to go

June to September is the main season. The northern Aegean can be breezy, so choose a sheltered bay when the wind picks up.

Getting around

Ferries link the islands from Volos and Agios Konstantinos. A car or local bus helps for the beaches, and boat trips reach the coves with no road.

Easiest beaches to reach

Koukounaries Beach, Banana Beach, Milia Beach, Panormos Beach.

Quieter hidden gems
