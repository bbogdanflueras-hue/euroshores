About beaches in the Dodecanese

The Dodecanese islands in the south-eastern Aegean combine long sandy beaches on Rhodes and Kos with small bays on Symi, Patmos and Karpathos. The sea is warm and the weather is reliably sunny.

When to go

May to October is the beach season. July and August are hot and busy, while late May, June and September are ideal.

Getting around

Ferries and flights connect the islands, and a car or scooter helps for the quieter beaches. Winds can be strong on the exposed coasts, so choose the sheltered side.

Easiest beaches to reach

Tsambika Beach, Prasonisi Beach, Rhodes, Lindos Beach, Anthony Quinn Bay.

Quieter hidden gems

Apella Beach, Glystra Beach, Rhodes, Livadi Beach, Astypalaia.
