About beaches in Ibiza, Menorca and Formentera

Ibiza, Menorca and Formentera are the smaller Balearic Islands, with white sand, shallow turquoise water and protected dunes. Menorca has quiet pine-backed calas, Formentera has long white beaches, and Ibiza combines sunset coves with beach clubs.

When to go

May to October is the beach season. July and August are the busiest and hottest, while June and September are warm with more space.

Getting around

Rent a car or scooter to reach the quieter calas. Parking at the best beaches is limited in summer, so arrive early and respect protected dune areas.

Easiest beaches to reach

Ses Illetes, Cala d'Hort, Cala Saona, Cala Galdana.

Quieter hidden gems

Cala Macarelleta, Cala Pregonda, Cala en Turqueta, Cala d'Hort.
