About beaches in Montenegro

Montenegro's short Adriatic coast has pebble and sand beaches below steep mountains, from the Bay of Kotor to Budva and the long sandy beaches near Ulcinj. The water is clear and the scenery is dramatic.

When to go

June to September is the main season. July and August are the busiest and hottest, while late May and September are quieter.

Getting around

A car or taxi helps reach the smaller beaches. Many beaches are managed with paid sunbeds, and parking near Budva fills quickly in summer.

Easiest beaches to reach

Sveti Stefan, Queens Beach, Ada Bojana Beach, Montenegro, Velika Plaža, Ulcinj.

Quieter hidden gems

Valdanos Bay, Drobni Pijesak, Ada Bojana Beach, Montenegro, Zanjice Beach, Lustica.
