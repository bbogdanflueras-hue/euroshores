About beaches in Normandy

Normandy has wide sandy beaches, white chalk cliffs at Etretat and the historic D-Day landing beaches. The tides are large and the sea is cool, so many visitors come for walking, history and scenery as well as swimming.

When to go

July and August are warmest for swimming, while spring and autumn suit walking and visits to the landing beaches.

Getting around

A car is the easiest way to travel along the coast. Check tide times before walking on the sand, and stay clear of cliff edges and unexploded-ordnance warning areas.

Easiest beaches to reach

Etretat Beach, Plage de Deauville, Arromanches Gold Beach, Omaha Beach.
