About beaches in Bodrum, Marmaris and Datca

The southwest corner of Turkey has sheltered bays, pine forests and warm shallow water. Bodrum has lively resorts and quieter fishing villages such as Gumusluk, Marmaris and Icmeler have long sandy bays with calm water, and the Datca Peninsula is a quieter finger of land between the Aegean and the Mediterranean. Akyaka is known for its cool river water and sandy shore at the head of a bay.

When to go

May to October is the main season, with the warmest sea in August and September. July and August can be very hot. Wind can be strong on parts of the coast in summer, which suits windsurfing and kitesurfing but makes some beaches choppier. June and September are the best months for a calmer visit.

Getting around

Bodrum and Dalaman airports serve the region, and boats link Bodrum, Marmaris and nearby coves in summer. Minibuses run between most towns, and a hire car is the easiest way to reach the Datca Peninsula. Roads on the peninsula are winding, so allow extra time.
