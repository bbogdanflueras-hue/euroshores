About beaches in Antalya, Kemer and Alanya

The Antalya coast is Turkey's biggest beach holiday region, with long sandy and pebble beaches backed by the Taurus Mountains. Konyaalti runs along the edge of Antalya city, Kemer and Beldibi have pebble beaches with mountain views, Side and Alanya have sandy beaches beside Roman and medieval ruins, and Cirali and Phaselis combine beaches with ancient sites and national park scenery.

When to go

The season is long: May to October for swimming, with the sea warm into November. July and August are very hot and crowded. Spring and autumn are better for sightseeing at Phaselis, Side and the Antalya old town. Many resort beaches have paid sunbed areas, while public sections stay free.

Getting around

Antalya airport is close to the region, and there are good bus links to Kemer, Side and Alanya. Dolmus minibuses run along the coast, and hire cars are easy to arrange. Cirali and Adrasan are quieter villages away from the big resorts, and they are reached by a short drive or minibus from the main road.
