About beaches in Fethiye, Kalkan and Kas

The western end of Turkey's Turquoise Coast is a landscape of pine-covered mountains dropping into deep blue bays. Oludeniz has the famous lagoon and the paragliding views from Babadag, Patara has one of the longest sand beaches in the Mediterranean, and smaller coves such as Kaputas and Butterfly Valley sit under steep cliffs. Iztuzu near Dalyan is a long sandbar beach protected for nesting loggerhead turtles.

When to go

May to October is the beach season, with the sea warmest from July to September. July and August are hot and busy, especially at Oludeniz. Late May, June and late September give warm water and more space. Patara and Iztuzu are nesting sites for sea turtles, and some areas have rules about sunbeds and night access in summer.

Getting around

Dalaman airport is the main gateway, and shuttle buses and dolmus minibuses connect Fethiye, Kalkan and Kas. A hire car is useful for Patara and Kaputas, while Butterfly Valley is usually reached by boat from Oludeniz. Boat trips from Fethiye, Kas and Kalkan visit coves that have no road access.
