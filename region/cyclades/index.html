About beaches in the Cyclades

The Cyclades are the Greek island group in the Aegean Sea, known for white villages, clear water and dramatic volcanic or white-rock coastlines. Beaches range from organised sandy bays to wild coves.

When to go

Late May to early October is the best time. The meltemi wind blows most in July and August, so the choice of a sheltered beach matters.

Getting around

Ferries link the islands, and on each island a car, scooter or local bus helps. Book ferries and accommodation early for summer.

Easiest beaches to reach

Sarakiniko, Plaka Beach, Agios Prokopios Beach, Golden Beach Paros.

Quieter hidden gems

Kolona Beach, Kleftiko, Katergo Beach, Mikro Alyko Beach.
