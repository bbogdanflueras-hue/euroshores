About beaches in Dalmatia

Dalmatia stretches along the Croatian Adriatic coast, with pebble beaches, pine-backed coves and islands such as Brac, Hvar and Korcula. The water is very clear and many beaches sit below old stone towns.

When to go

June to September is the main swimming season, with the sea warmest in July and August. May and October are quieter but cooler.

Getting around

Ferries connect the islands and a car helps on the mainland coast. Most beaches are pebble or rock, so water shoes are useful, and popular spots get busy by midday in summer.

Easiest beaches to reach

Punta Rata, Pupnatska Luka Beach, Korcula, Slanica Beach, Nin Beach.

Quieter hidden gems

Dubovica, Saplunara, Nugal Beach, Pupnatska Luka Beach, Korcula.
