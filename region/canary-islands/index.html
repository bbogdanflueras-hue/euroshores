About beaches in the Canary Islands

The Canary Islands have mild weather all year and a wide range of beaches, from golden dunes to black volcanic sand. Wind and Atlantic swell affect some beaches, so check conditions before swimming.

When to go

Beach weather is possible all year. Summer is hotter and drier, winter is mild, and the Atlantic water stays cooler than the Mediterranean even in summer.

Getting around

Flights or ferries link the islands, and a hire car is the easiest way to get around each island. Check wind and waves before you swim.

Easiest beaches to reach

Playa de las Teresitas, Playa de Famara, Playa de las Canteras, El Cotillo Beach.

Quieter hidden gems

Playa de Cofete, Playa de Benijo, Tenerife, Playa de Mogan, Gran Canaria, Playa de El Medano, Tenerife.
