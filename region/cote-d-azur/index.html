About beaches in the French Riviera

The French Riviera has pebble beaches in Nice and Cannes, sandy bays near Saint-Tropez and rocky coves around Cap d'Ail. The water is clear and many beaches are served by private beach clubs.

When to go

June to September is the main season. July and August are busy and expensive, while June and September are warm with more space.

Getting around

Trains run along the coast, which is easier than driving in summer. Public beaches are free but small, and private sections charge for sunbeds.

Easiest beaches to reach

Plage des Marinieres, Villefranche, Plage de la Garoupe, Plage de la Croisette, Plage de la Salis.
