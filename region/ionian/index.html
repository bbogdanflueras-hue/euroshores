About beaches in the Ionian Islands

The Ionian Islands on the west coast of Greece are known for green hills, limestone cliffs and clear turquoise water. Corfu, Kefalonia, Zakynthos and Lefkada offer everything from long sandy beaches to small pebble coves reached by boat or a short walk.

When to go

May to October is the beach season, with the warmest sea in August and September. June and September give warm days with fewer visitors.

Getting around

A rental car makes it easier to reach the quieter coves, and boats serve beaches with no road access. Parking at the best-known beaches fills early in July and August.

Easiest beaches to reach

Gerakas Beach, Pefkoulia Beach, Lefkada, Agios Nikitas Beach, Lefkada, Kathisma Beach.

Quieter hidden gems

Porto Timoni, Mylos Beach, Myrtiotissa Beach, Corfu, Petani Beach.
