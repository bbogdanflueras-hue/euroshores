About beaches in the Costa Brava

The Costa Brava in Catalonia has pine-covered hills, rocky headlands and small sandy coves with clear water. Fishing villages sit close to many beaches, and coastal paths link the calas.

When to go

June to September is the main season, with the sea warmest in August. May and September are quieter.

Getting around

A car helps for the smaller coves, but walking paths connect many beaches. Parking is limited in July and August, so arrive early.

Easiest beaches to reach

Sa Tuna Beach, Begur, Tamariu Beach, Platja Gran, Tossa de Mar, Platja de Cadaques.

Quieter hidden gems

Sa Tuna Beach, Begur, Platja de Cadaques, Platja de Calella de Palafrugell.
