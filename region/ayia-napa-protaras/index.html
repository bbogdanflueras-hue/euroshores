About beaches in Ayia Napa and Protaras

Ayia Napa and Protaras sit on the sunny southeast tip of Cyprus, where long sandy bays, shallow turquoise water and low limestone headlands make the island's most reliable family beaches. Ayia Napa has the livelier resort scene and Nissi Beach, while Protaras is calmer and strung along a run of small sandy coves. Cape Greco, between the two, adds sea caves, cliffs and a protected coastal park.

When to go

The swimming season runs from May to October, with the warmest sea in August and September. July and August are hot and crowded, and the most popular beaches fill with sunbeds by late morning. May, June and October are much quieter, and the water in June and October is still comfortable for most people.

Getting around

Larnaca airport is about 45 minutes away by road, and Paphos airport is much further. Buses link Ayia Napa, Protaras and Larnaca in summer, but a hire car or taxi is easier for the smaller coves and Cape Greco. Many beaches here have paid sunbed areas alongside free public sand, and parking can be tight in July and August.
