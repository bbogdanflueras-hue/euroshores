About beaches in Valencia and the Costa Blanca

Valencia and the Costa Blanca offer long sandy city beaches, calm coves and rocky headlands such as Calpe and Javea. Water is usually shallow and warm, and resorts are close to airports.

When to go

May to October is the beach season. July and August are the busiest, while June and September have warm water and more space.

Getting around

Trains, trams and buses link many towns, and a car is useful for the coves. Beaches near the resorts have lifeguards and services in summer.

Easiest beaches to reach

Playa del Arenal, Javea, Playa de Moraira, Playa del Moncayo, Guardamar, Playa Paraiso, Villajoyosa.

Quieter hidden gems

Playa de Calblanque, Cala del Moraig.
