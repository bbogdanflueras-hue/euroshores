About beaches in Brittany

Brittany has pink granite rocks, white sand coves, dunes and big tides. The water is cooler than the Mediterranean but the beaches are wide, scenic and often uncrowded.

When to go

July and August are the warmest months for swimming, and June and September can be pleasant. Check tide times, as the sea retreats far on many beaches.

Getting around

A car is the easiest way to travel the coast. Many beaches have rip currents or strong tides, so swim where lifeguards are on duty and check the tide table.

Easiest beaches to reach
