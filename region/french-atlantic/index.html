About beaches in the French Atlantic coast

The French Atlantic coast has long sandy beaches, dunes and pine forests from the Basque coast to Vendee and Charente-Maritime. Surfing, cycling and oysters are part of the experience, and waves can be large.

When to go

July and August have the warmest water. June and September are quieter, but the Atlantic stays cooler than the Mediterranean.

Getting around

Swim only between the lifeguard flags, as currents can be strong. Cycle paths link many beaches, and a car helps for the wilder dunes.

Easiest beaches to reach

Plage des Dames, Noirmoutier, Lacanau Ocean Beach, Biarritz Grande Plage, Hossegor Lake Beach.

Quieter hidden gems

Plage de la Conche des Baleines, Ile de Re.
