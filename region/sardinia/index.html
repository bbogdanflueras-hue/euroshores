About beaches in Sardinia

Sardinia is the second-largest island in Italy, with white-sand coves, turquoise water and granite headlands. The north and south coasts have shallow, sheltered bays, while the east coast has rugged cliffs and some beaches that can only be reached on foot or by boat.

When to go

June to September is the main season. July and August are hot and crowded, and some beaches limit daily visitor numbers or require booking in peak summer.

Getting around

Renting a car is the easiest way to explore the island. For the most protected beaches you may need to book ahead or arrive early, so check the local rules before you go.

Easiest beaches to reach

Cala Brandinchi, La Pelosa, Tuerredda Beach, Porto Giunco.

Quieter hidden gems

Cala Goloritzé, Cala Mariolu, Cala Fuili, Spiaggia di Piscinas.
