About beaches in the Albanian Riviera

The Albanian Riviera has clear water, pebble coves and quiet sandy bays between the Ionian Sea and the Ceraunian mountains. It is far less crowded than neighbouring coasts, with beach bars and small family-run places to stay.

When to go

June to September is the swimming season. The sea is warm from July, and September is often calm and pleasant.

Getting around

A car is the easiest way to travel the coast road, and boats serve some remote coves. Facilities are simple outside the main resorts, so bring water and cash.

Easiest beaches to reach

Palasa Beach, Albanian Riviera, Porto Palermo Beach, Himara, Potami Beach, Saranda Beach.

Quieter hidden gems
