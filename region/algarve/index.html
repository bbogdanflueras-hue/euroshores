About beaches in the Algarve

The Algarve is the southern coast of Portugal, known for golden cliffs, sea stacks and sheltered coves in the east and wilder Atlantic beaches on the west coast. The south coast has calmer, warmer water, while the west coast near Sagres is cooler and better for surfing.

When to go

May to October is the main beach season. July and August are the busiest and most expensive months, while June and September often give the best balance of warm weather and space.

Getting around

A car makes it easiest to reach the smaller beaches, and parking at popular cliff-top beaches fills early in summer. Towns such as Lagos, Albufeira and Tavira make good bases.

Easiest beaches to reach

Praia da Bordeira, Praia Dona Ana, Praia de Odeceixe, Praia do Amado.

Quieter hidden gems

Benagil Cove, Praia do Carvalho, Praia da Bordeira, Praia do Beliche.
