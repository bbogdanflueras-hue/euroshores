About beaches in Crete

Crete is the largest Greek island, with long sandy beaches, pink-tinted sand in the west and gorges that meet the sea in the south. The north coast is easier to reach, while the south and west coasts have wilder beaches.

When to go

May to October is the main beach season and the sea stays warm into October. The meltemi wind in July and August can affect exposed beaches, so sheltered bays are a good backup.

Getting around

Car hire is the norm. Some of the best-known beaches need a boat trip or a long walk. Chania and Rethymno are good bases for the west and centre.

Easiest beaches to reach

Elafonissi Beach, Falassarna Beach, Falasarna Beach, Stavros Beach, Crete.

Quieter hidden gems

Balos Lagoon, Preveli Beach, Seitan Limani, Glyka Nera Beach.
