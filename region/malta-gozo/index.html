About beaches in Malta and Gozo

Malta, Gozo and Comino have sandy bays, rocky swimming spots, tidal pools and clear blue water. Gozo is quieter, with small bays and cliffs, while Malta has the longest sandy beaches.

When to go

May to October is the swimming season. The sea is warmest in August and September, and spring is cooler but quiet.

Getting around

Buses and ferries link the islands, and a car helps for the remote coves. Many rocky swimming spots have ladders and no sand, so bring water shoes.

Easiest beaches to reach

Ramla Bay, Golden Bay, Ghadira Bay, Gnejna Bay.

Quieter hidden gems

St Peter's Pool, San Blas Bay, Wied il-Ghasri, Gozo, Mgarr ix-Xini.
