Contact EuroShores

EuroShores is an independent, editor-run guide to the beaches of Europe. We read every message and use it to correct and improve the guides.

Send a correction or a tip

Parking rules, fees, opening seasons and access routes change every year. If you visited a beach recently and something on its page is out of date, tell us through the Suggest a beach form. Name the beach, say what changed and when you saw it. We check the details against official sources before we update the page.

Suggest a beach

Know a beach that should be in the guide? Use the same form and include the nearest village, what makes it worth a trip and how you get there. We add beaches only when we can confirm access, facilities and a free-licence photo.

Photo credits and takedowns

Photos come from Wikimedia Commons under free licences and are credited to their authors on each beach page. If you are the author and want a credit changed or a photo removed, send the beach name and the file name through the form and we will act on it promptly.

Partnerships and press

We have no paid placements and no sponsored rankings. If you run a tourism board, a ferry line or a local guide service and want to share official information about a beach, send it through the form with a link to the source.

How we respond

We aim to reply to messages that need an answer within a week. Corrections are usually live on the site within a few days of verification.
